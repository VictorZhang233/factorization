## 0. Overview

### 0.1 Scope, method, and what "verified" means here

This chapter covers large-momentum effective theory (LaMET) [Ji:2013dva, Ji:2014gla, Ji:2020ect, Ji:2024oka] and the
factorization ("matching") theorems that relate Euclidean, equal-time correlators in a fast-moving hadron to light-cone
parton observables, together with the competing/complementary short-distance-factorization (pseudo-PDF, "good lattice
cross section") programs [Radyushkin:2017cyf, Ma:2014jla, Ma:2017pxb]. It is written as a frontier chapter of the
monograph: for every result I give (i) the exact statement in our conventions, (ii) the hypotheses, (iii) the proof
architecture (what is proven, what is assumed), (iv) the rigor grade with justification, (v) the relation to the rest of
factorization theory, (vi) verified bibliographic data (in `lamet.bib`).

Sources. Every statement attributed to a paper was checked against the **arXiv LaTeX source of that paper, downloaded and
read during this work** (marked *(read)*), unless marked *(abstract)* (only the INSPIRE abstract was read), *(title)*
(title only), or *(recall)* (not re-checked; avoided wherever possible). Bibliographic data: INSPIRE API, one hit per query, recorded in
`logs/refs_batch*.txt`.

### 0.3 Problems found in the literature (all verified by the scripts)

1. **Hybrid-scheme matching kernel, δ term** (Ji, Liu, Liu, Zhang, Zhao et al., NPB 964 (2021) 115311, eq. (hybridm2);
   repeated in RMP 93 (2021) 035005, eq. (hybridm2)) *(read)*. The extra hybrid term is printed as
   $\frac{3a}{2}\big[-\frac{1}{|1-\xi|_+}+\frac{2{\rm Si}((1-\xi)\lambda_S)}{\pi(1-\xi)}\big]$ with
   $\frac{1}{|u|_+}\equiv\lim_{\beta\to0^+}[\theta(|u|-\beta)/|u|+2\delta(u)\ln\beta]$. The coordinate-space hybrid
   correction $\frac{3a}{2}\delta(1-\alpha)\ln\frac{z^2}{z_S^2}\theta(|z|-z_S)$ vanishes at $z=0$, so its Fourier
   transform must integrate to zero (vector-current conservation of the hybrid scheme). With the printed prescription
   $\int d\xi\,C_{\rm hyb}=1+3a\ln(\lambda_Se^{\gamma_E})$. **Correct form**: the whole bracket is a plus distribution on
   $\mathbb R$, $\frac{3a}{2}\big[-\frac{1}{|1-\xi|}+\frac{2{\rm Si}((1-\xi)\lambda_S)}{\pi(1-\xi)}\big]^{[-\infty,\infty]}_{+(1)}$,
   as written later by Su, Holligan, Ji, Yao, Zhang, Zhang, NPB 991 (2023) 116201, eq. (C1 hybrid) *(read)*.
   `s2`: printed − exact $=3a\ln(\lambda_Se^{\gamma_E})\,\phi(1)$ to $10^{-12}$ for 12 parameter sets; the corrected form
   reproduces the exact Fourier transform to $10^{-12}$. (For $\lambda_S\simeq1$, $a\simeq0.06$ the printed form would shift
   the normalization by ≈10%.)
2. **Collins–Soper exponent in the quasi-TMD matching** (Ji, Liu, Liu, PLB 811 (2020) 135946, eq. (factorization); RMP
   2021 eqs. (quasiTMDmatch), (TMD-CS_kernel)) *(read)*. Both papers define $2\zeta\frac{d}{d\zeta}\ln f^{\rm TMD}=K$
   but print $e^{\pm K\ln(\zeta_z/\zeta)}$ and $(\zeta_{z,1}/\zeta_{z,2})^{K}$. The one-loop expressions quoted in the RMP
   itself fix the coefficient: only $e^{\frac12K\ln(\zeta_z/\zeta)}$ cancels the $L_b\ln(\zeta_z/\zeta)$ terms (`s4`), in
   agreement with ESZ 2019 and ESSZ 2022 (which use $\frac12\gamma_\zeta$ with $\gamma_\zeta\equiv K$). The text
   of the RMP elsewhere (eq. for $f^{\rm TMD}(\zeta)$ evolution) has the correct $\frac12K$. Journal versions not checked.
3. **BVZ power-correction coefficient** (Braun, Vladimirov, Zhang, PRD 99 (2019) 014013, eq. (cR)) *(read)*: relative to
   their own eqs. (t4-Qparallel), (t4-Qperp) (with the standard $[0,1]$ plus distribution) the expressions for $R^\parallel_{\mathcal Q}$
   **and** $R^\perp_{\mathcal Q}$ omit $q(x)\ln(1-x)$ inside the braces. `s4` shows that (t4-Qparallel) and (t4-Qperp) are the correct
   second derivatives (agreement $10^{-6}$), while eq. (cR) as printed differs by ≈30% (∥) and ≈24% (⊥) at $x=0.5$. The asymptotic claim
   $\propto\Lambda^2/(x^2(1-x)P_z^2)$ is unaffected ($\bar x\ln\bar x\to0$).
4. **Sign of $g$ in Wilson lines** (IJJSZ 2018): the light-cone link $P\exp(-ig\int A^+)$ and the space-like link
   $P\exp(-ig\int A^z)$ correspond to opposite covariant-derivative conventions (since $A^z=-A_z$). Harmless at the orders
   computed ($g^2$); all formulas below use CONTEXT conventions.
5. **Lattice mixing statement** (JZZ, PRL 120 (2018) 112001) *(read)*: "no lower-dimension operator can mix" is correct,
   but same-dimension mixing $\Gamma\leftrightarrow\{\slashed n,\Gamma\}$ exists for chirally non-symmetric lattice fermions
   [Constantinou:2017sej, Green:2017xeu]; the multiplicative statement must be read with this proviso (§3.5).
6. **Original matching form** (Ji 2013): $\int_x^1\frac{dy}{y}Z(\frac xy,\frac{\mu}{P^z})$ — superseded:
   $\int_{-1}^{1}\frac{dy}{|y|}C(\frac xy,\frac{\mu}{|y|P^z})$ [Izubuchi:2018srq]. Using the hadron momentum in $C$ is
   wrong at $O(\alpha_s)$ (terms $\propto\ln|y|\,P_{qq}$) — **[V]** for the early form.

### 1.1 Binding conventions (CONTEXT.md) and translation table

Metric $(+,-,-,-)$; $v^\pm=(v^0\pm v^3)/\sqrt2$; $D_\mu=\partial_\mu-igA^a_\mu t^a$;
$W[b,a]=P\exp\big(ig\int_a^bdz^\mu A_\mu\big)$ (later points to the left), $W\to U(b)WU^\dagger(a)$.
Light-cone PDF (hadron along $+z$):
$$
f(x,\mu)=\int\frac{dw^-}{4\pi}e^{-ixP^+w^-}\langle P|\bar\psi(w)\gamma^+W[w,0]\psi(0)|P\rangle,\qquad w=(0^+,w^-,0_T),
$$
$f_{\bar q}(x)=-f_q(-x)$. Light-cone Ioffe-time distribution (ITD): with $\lambda\equiv P\cdot w=P^+w^-$,
$$
I(\lambda,\mu)\equiv\frac{\langle P|\bar\psi(w)\gamma^+W[w,0]\psi(0)|P\rangle}{2P^+}=\int_{-1}^{1}dx\,e^{ix\lambda}f(x,\mu),\qquad
f(x,\mu)=\int\frac{d\lambda}{2\pi}e^{-ix\lambda}I(\lambda,\mu).
$$
Space-like separation $z^\mu=z\,\hat e_z^\mu$, $\hat e_z=(0,0,0,1)$, $z\in\mathbb R$; hadron $P^\mu=(P^0,0,0,P^z)$,
$P^z>0$; **Ioffe time $\lambda\equiv P\cdot z=-P^zz$**; $-z^2=z_3^2>0$.

$L_b=\ln(\mu^2b_T^2/b_0^2)$, $\zeta_z=(2xP^z)^2$.

### 1.2 Quasi-PDF, ITD, pseudo-PDF (definitions)

**Definition 1.1 (quasi-PDF; Ji 2013).** For $\Gamma\in\{\gamma^z,\gamma^0\}$,
$$
\tilde q_\Gamma(x,P^z;\mu)=N_\Gamma\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{dz}{4\pi}\,e^{ixP^zz}\,
\langle P|\big[\bar\psi(z\hat e_z)\,\Gamma\,W[z\hat e_z,0]\,\psi(0)\big]_R|P\rangle,
\qquad N_{\gamma^z}=1,\ N_{\gamma^0}=\frac{P^z}{P^0},
$$
$[\dots]_R$ = renormalized operator (§3). Normalization: $\int dx\,\tilde q=\frac{N_\Gamma}{2P^z}\langle P|\bar\psi\Gamma\psi|P\rangle=1$
per valence quark (vector-current conservation) *if* the scheme preserves the local limit (ratio/hybrid; not
$\overline{\rm MS}$, §4.6). Support: $x\in\mathbb R$ at finite $P^z$ [Ji:2013dva, Izubuchi:2018srq].
($e^{ixP^zz}=e^{-ix\lambda}$, matching the light-cone $e^{-ixP^+w^-}$.)

**Definition 1.2 (quasi-ITD).** Lorentz decomposition [Radyushkin:2017cyf] (read):
$\langle P|\bar\psi(z)\gamma^\alpha W[z,0]\psi(0)|P\rangle=2P^\alpha\mathcal M(\lambda,-z^2)+2z^\alpha\mathcal N(\lambda,-z^2)$.
For $z^0=0$ the choice $\alpha=0$ isolates $\mathcal M$; $\alpha=z$ is contaminated by $\mathcal N$, which changes the
one-loop kernel by $2a(1-\alpha)$ (§4.6). Define $\tilde h_\Gamma(\lambda,z^2)\equiv\langle P|O_\Gamma(z)|P\rangle/(2P^\Gamma)$
($P^{\gamma^0}=P^0$, $P^{\gamma^z}=P^z$). Then
$$
\tilde q(x,P^z)=\int\frac{d\lambda}{2\pi}\,e^{-ix\lambda}\,\tilde h\Big(\lambda,-\frac{\lambda^2}{P_z^2}\Big)
\quad\text{(fixed $P^z$: $z$ and $\lambda$ vary together).}
$$

**Definition 1.3 (pseudo-PDF; Radyushkin 2017).** At fixed $z^2$, $\mathcal P(x,-z^2)=\int\frac{d\lambda}{2\pi}e^{-ix\lambda}\tilde h(\lambda,z^2)$
(the transform runs over $P^z$). **Reduced ITD**: $\mathfrak M(\lambda,z^2)=\tilde h(\lambda,z^2)/\tilde h(0,z^2)$
[Radyushkin:2017cyf, Orginos:2017kos] — UV divergences ($Z$ factors, $e^{\delta m|z|}$) cancel in the ratio.

| representation | transform | arguments | support |
|---|---|---|---|
| quasi-ITD $\tilde h$ | — | $\lambda,\ z^2$ | — |
| quasi-PDF $\tilde q$ | $z\to xP^z$ at fixed $P^z$ | $x,\ P^z$ | $\mathbb R$ |
| pseudo-PDF $\mathcal P$ | $\lambda\to x$ at fixed $z^2$ | $x,\ z^2$ | $[-1,1]$ (Prop. 1.4) |

### 1.3 Support and analyticity (rigorous in perturbation theory)

**Proposition 1.4 (support; Radyushkin 1983).** For every Feynman diagram contributing to
$\langle p|\bar\psi(z)\Gamma W[z,0]\psi(0)|p\rangle$ (and to multiparton correlators), the Fourier transform in
$\lambda=p\cdot z$ at fixed $z^2$ is supported in $[-1,1]$; equivalently $\tilde h(\lambda,z^2)=\int_{-1}^1dx\,e^{ix\lambda}\mathcal P(x,-z^2)$.
*Proof architecture:* α-(Schwinger-)representation of each diagram; the dependence on $(pz)$ enters through
$\exp[-i(pz)\sum_k\alpha_k c_k/\sum\dots]$ with the coefficient a convex combination bounded by 1 in modulus
[Radyushkin:1983wh] (cited, *recall* for the original; the statement and use are read in [Radyushkin:2017cyf,
Radyushkin:2018cvn, Izubuchi:2018srq]). **Grade [R]** in perturbation theory, diagram by diagram.

**Corollary 1.5 (entire functions; Paley–Wiener).** If $\mathcal P(\cdot,-z^2)$ is a distribution supported in $[-1,1]$,
then $\lambda\mapsto\tilde h(\lambda,z^2)$ extends to an entire function of exponential type $\le1$. Applied to the OPE
coefficient function, this is the precise form of Ma–Qiu's statement that "$\sigma_n$ is analytic in $\omega$ with the
only singularity at $\omega=\infty$" [Ma:2017pxb] *(read)*. **Grade [R]** (given 1.4).

## 2. The LaMET principle: content, formalization, status

### 2.1 Ji 2013: the original argument *(read)*

[Ji:2013dva] starts from the twist-2 operators $O^{\mu_1\dots\mu_n}=\bar\psi\gamma^{(\mu_1}iD^{\mu_2}\cdots iD^{\mu_n)}\psi-{\rm traces}$
with $\langle P|O^{\mu_1\dots\mu_n}|P\rangle=2a_n(P^{\mu_1}\cdots P^{\mu_n}-{\rm traces})$, $a_n=\int dx\,x^{n-1}q(x)$. Taking all
indices equal to $z$, the trace terms are $O((P^z)^{n-2}M^2)$ and higher-twist operators contribute $O((P^z)^{n-2}\Lambda^2)$, hence
$$
\langle P|\bar\psi\gamma^ziD^z\cdots iD^z\psi|P\rangle=2a_n(P^z)^n\big[1+O(\Lambda^2/P_z^2,M^2/P_z^2)\big].
$$
"Inverting" this tower into a non-local equal-time operator gives Definition 1.1 with the claim
$\tilde q(x,P^z)=q(x)+O(\Lambda^2/P_z^2,M^2/P_z^2)$. Picture: the space-like segment $(0,z\hat e_z)$ is tilted toward the
light cone by the boost; its invariant length cannot change, but the off-light-cone-ness produces only power corrections.

*What is and is not established in that paper.* The moment relation is **[R]** (Lorentz covariance of local matrix
elements). The inversion is **heuristic**: (i) the sum over $n$ does not commute with $P^z\to\infty$ (the moments of the
renormalized quasi-PDF do not exist, §4.6); (ii) UV renormalization is not commutative with $P^z\to\infty$. Ji already
recognized (ii): his one-loop result in $A^z=0$ gauge with a transverse cutoff $\mu$ and IR cutoff $\lambda$,
$q^{(1)}=\frac{\alpha_s}{2\pi}\big[T(x)\frac{\Lambda}{P^z}+P(x)\ln\frac{\Lambda P^z}{\lambda^2}\big]$,
$\Lambda=\sqrt{\mu^2+(1-x)^2P_z^2}-(1-x)P^z$, $T=C_Fx/(1-x)^2$, $P=C_F\frac{1+x^2}{1-x}$ *(read)*, exhibits a linear
divergence for $\mu\to\infty$ first and the standard DGLAP logarithm for $P^z\to\infty$ first; the two limits are related
by a "matching condition" $q(x,\mu,P^z)=\int_x^1\frac{dy}{y}Z(\frac xy,\frac{\mu}{P^z})q(y,\mu)$, which is the first statement
of the LaMET matching (its form was later corrected, §0.3 item 6). The paper lists the intended generalizations: GPDs,
TMDs (staple links along $\pm z$ closed by a transverse link on a finite lattice), Wigner distributions, distribution
amplitudes, light-front wave functions, higher-twist distributions, and $\Delta G$ via $F^{3\mu}\tilde F^{3}{}_\mu$.

### 2.2 Ji 2014: LaMET as an effective field theory *(read)*

[Ji:2014gla] formulates: for a Euclidean (lattice) observable $F$ depending on a large hadron momentum $P^z$ and a UV
cutoff,
$$
F(P^z/\Lambda)=Z(P^z/\Lambda,\Lambda/\mu)\,f(\mu)+O(1/P_z^2)+\dots,\qquad
\gamma(\alpha_s)=\frac{1}{Z}\frac{\partial Z}{\partial\ln P^z},\quad \frac{\partial F}{\partial\ln P^z}=\gamma F+O(P_z^{-2}),
$$
with $f(\mu)$ defined in the theory with $P^z\to\infty$ (a light-front correlator). The analogy is HQET: taking
$m_Q\to\infty$ inside integrals does not change IR physics but changes UV physics; the difference is a perturbative
matching factor. The paper asserts that "the factorization in terms of Feynman diagrams can be proved order by order as
in the renormalization program" without giving the proof. Further concepts introduced: **universality class** (all
Euclidean operators with the same infinite-boost limit, e.g. $\gamma^z$ vs $\gamma^0$; [Hatta:2013gta] for $\Delta G$),
**quasi light-front wave functions** with links to $z=\pm\infty$, and the **space-time picture**: valence correlation
length $\sim1/(xP^z)$; the smallest resolvable momentum fraction $x_{\min}\sim\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/P^z$.

### 2.3 The RMP formulation and the "momentum renormalization group" *(read)*

[Ji:2020ect] (§II–III): (a) the quasi-PDF is the physical momentum density $N_P(k^z)$ of quarks in a hadron of momentum
$P$ (with a gauge link); (b) **momentum RGE**: $\frac{dO(P)}{dP}=i\frac{d\omega}{dP}\langle P|[\hat O,\hat K]|P\rangle
\xrightarrow{P\gg M}C(\alpha_s(P))\otimes O(P)+O(M^2/P^2)$, "the proof of the above equation is non-trivial, and it can
be analyzed on a case-by-case basis"; at one loop $P^z\frac{d\tilde q}{dP^z}=\frac{\alpha_sC_F}{\pi}\big[\big(\frac{1+y^2}{1-y}\big)_+-\frac32\delta(1-y)\big]$
(the $-\frac32\delta$ reflects the operator anomalous dimension; it follows from the $\overline{\rm MS}$ kernel of §4.6 by
$d\ell_p/d\ln P^z=-2$); "it is the hadron-momentum dependence of the physical momentum distribution that generates the
DGLAP evolution in the infinite-momentum limit"; (c) **matching**:
$\tilde q(y,P^z,\mu)=\int_{-1}^1\frac{dx}{|x|}C\big(\frac yx,\frac{\mu}{xP^z}\big)q(x,\mu)+O\big(\frac{\Lambda^2}{(yP^z)^2},\frac{\Lambda^2}{((1-y)P^z)^2}\big)$,
"true to all orders in perturbation theory", with the inverse ("Weinberg-type EFT") expansion
$q(x,\mu)=\int\frac{dy}{|y|}\tilde C\big(\frac xy,\frac{\mu}{yP^z}\big)\tilde q(y,P^z,\mu)+O\big(\frac{\Lambda^2}{(xP^z)^2},\frac{\Lambda^2}{((1-x)P^z)^2}\big)$;
(d) the effective Lagrangian of LaMET is full QCD; $P$ enters only through external states.

### 2.4 Ji 2020 and 2024: Euclidean effective theory "in the spirit of Weinberg" *(read)*

[Ji:2020byp, Ji:2024oka] add: a leading large-velocity Lagrangian for collinear quarks
$\mathcal L^{(0)}_{q,v}=\bar\psi_v[iv\cdot D+\frac{i\bar v\cdot D}{2\gamma^2}+iD_\perp\frac{1}{2i\bar v\cdot D}iD_\perp]\slashed{\bar v}\psi_v$
($\gamma=$ boost factor) which "formally converges to SCET or light-front quantization as $v\to c$"; the finite boost is a
**non-perturbative regulator of light-cone (rapidity) divergences**; a full expansion
$$
f(x,\mu)=\int\frac{dy}{y}C_2\Big(\frac yx,\frac{P^z}{\mu}\Big)\tilde f\Big(y,\frac{P^z}{\mu}\Big)
+\Big(\frac{\Lambda_{\rm QCD}}{P^z}\Big)^2\sum_i\int\frac{dy_1dy_2dy_3}{y_1y_2y_3}C_{4i}\,\tilde f_i(y_1,y_2,y_3)+\dots
$$
with twist-4 *equal-time* multiparton correlators $\tilde f_i$; "all power corrections have power divergences and the
subtractions must be defined consistently with the IR renormalon poles of $C_2$"; analogy with Symanzik's improved
("perfect") action, $\mathcal L^{\rm Parton}_{\rm eff}=C_0\mathcal L^{(0)}_v+\sum_i\gamma^{-i}C_i\mathcal L^{(i)}_v$;
"due to asymptotic freedom of QCD, the two orders of limits differ only in perturbation theory but are equivalent for
non-perturbative physics"; the expansion "breaks down at small $x\sim\Lambda/P$"; at finite $P^z$ the quasi-PDF is
analytic at $y=0,1$ (no PDF-type endpoint singularities), which are regenerated only as $P^z\to\infty$.

### 2.5 Formalization and grades

Let $\mathcal O_E$ be a renormalized Euclidean (equal-time) operator whose formal infinite-boost limit is a light-front
operator $\mathcal O_{LF}$ (universality class $[\mathcal O_{LF}]$).

**Conjecture 2.1 (LaMET principle, non-perturbative form).** For hadron states $|P\rangle$ and $P^z\gg\Lambda_{\rm QCD},M$,
$\langle P|\mathcal O_E|P\rangle(P^z,\mu)=\big[\mathcal Z(P^z/\mu,\alpha_s(\mu))\otimes\langle P|\mathcal O_{LF}|P\rangle(\mu)\big]
+\sum_{k\ge1}(\Lambda/P^z)^{2k}\times(\text{matrix elements of higher-twist equal-time operators})$, where $\mathcal Z$ has a
perturbative expansion free of IR sensitivity, and the power expansion is asymptotic, organized by the hard scales of the
*partons* (e.g. $|x|P^z$, $|1-x|P^z$).
**Grade [C].** No non-perturbative proof exists for any member of the class; it would require a non-perturbative OPE /
factorization in QCD (cf. the non-perturbative status of the OPE, monograph Ch. 4 and Ch. 16). Evidence: perturbation theory
(Thm 2.2), renormalon analyses [Braun:2018brg, Liu:2023onm], exactly solvable models (§11), lattice data.

**Theorem 2.2 (perturbative content).** For partonic external states, at leading power, the statement of Conjecture 2.1
holds order by order in renormalized perturbation theory for: the quark non-singlet quasi-PDF (Thm 4.3), the
flavor-singlet/gluon quasi-PDFs (with mixing), quasi-GPDs/DAs (§9), and quasi-TMDs (with the reduced soft function,
§10). **Grade [A]** in each case; one-loop explicit **[R]$_{1L}$**; two-loop explicit for non-singlet PDF/DA, quasi-TMD hard
kernel **[R]$_{2L}$**. The order-of-limits argument itself ("IR physics unchanged") is a heuristic; the actual proofs are the
OPE route and the collinear-subtraction (CFP/Collins) route of §4, and the boost/limits route of ESSZ for TMDs (§10).

*Relation to the rest of the monograph.* LaMET matching is the Euclidean analogue of hard-scattering factorization
(Ch. 5–8): the quasi-PDF plays the role of a "lattice cross section" with hard scale $|x|P^z$ [Ma:2014jla]; its proof uses
the same collinear power counting, Ward identities (for $A^+$ gluons) and subtraction structure, but with **no final
state and no Glauber pinch** for the collinear case (single hadron, space-like operator); for TMDs the soft/rapidity
structure of Ch. 11 reappears with $P^z$ as an off-light-cone rapidity regulator.

## 3. Renormalization of space-like Wilson-line operators

### 3.1 Background: renormalization of Wilson lines (1979–1986)

Smooth non-light-like Wilson lines/loops are multiplicatively renormalizable: a linearly divergent self-energy
exponentiates as $e^{-\delta m\,L}$ ("mass renormalization of a test particle", [Polyakov:1980ca]); end points and cusps
give logarithmic divergences; interior gluon–link vertices are renormalized by the coupling
[Dotsenko:1979wb, Brandt:1981kf, Craigie:1980qs]; the auxiliary one-dimensional field formalism and BRST proof are reviewed
in [Dorn:1986dt]. The one-loop quark two-point function with a link ("connector") is in [Stefanis:1983ke].

### 3.2 Theorem 3.1 (multiplicative renormalizability)

**Statement.** Let $O^B_\Gamma(z)=\bar\psi_0(z\hat n)\,\Gamma\,W_0[z\hat n,0]\,\psi_0(0)$ (bare fields and coupling),
$\hat n$ space-like, $z\neq0$, flavour non-singlet or singlet, any Dirac matrix $\Gamma$. In QCD with a gauge-invariant
regulator there exist constants $Z_j$ (independent of $z$, $\Gamma$, external states) and, for cut-off regulators, a
linearly divergent $\delta m$, such that
$$
O^R_\Gamma(z,\mu)=Z_j^{-2}\,e^{-\delta m|z|}\,O^B_\Gamma(z)
$$
has finite Green functions with elementary fields at $z\neq0$, to all orders in renormalized perturbation theory;
$Z_j=Z_\psi^{1/2}Z_Q^{1/2}Z_V$ is the renormalization of the "heavy–light current" $\bar Q\psi$; in dimensional
regularization $\delta m=0$ (MS-bar), $Z_j=1+\frac{\alpha_sC_F}{4\pi}\frac{3}{2\epsilon}+\dots$, i.e.
$Z_O=Z_j^2=1+\frac{\alpha_sC_F}{4\pi}\frac{3}{\epsilon}+\dots$; the operator does **not** mix with gluon operators or other
flavours. (Sign convention: $\delta m$ here is the mass counterterm such that $e^{-\delta m|z|}$ removes $e^{+\delta m|z|}$
growth; signs of $\delta m$ differ between papers.)

**Proof architecture A (auxiliary field; [Ji:2017oey, Green:2017xeu], *read*).** Extend QCD by a colour-triplet,
spinless Grassmann field $Q$ with $\mathcal L_Q=\bar Q\,i\hat n\cdot D\,Q$ (+ $\delta m$ term for cut-off regulators).
Its propagator in a background field is $S_Q(x,y)=\theta(x^z-y^z)\delta(x^0-y^0)\delta^2(\vec x_\perp-\vec y_\perp)W[x,y]$,
so $O_\Gamma(z)=\langle\bar\psi(z)\Gamma Q(z)\,\bar Q(0)\psi(0)\rangle_Q$: a product of two *local* composite operators
$\bar J=\bar\psi Q$, $J=\bar Q\psi$ (up to $\det(in\cdot D)$, a constant [JZZ]). Steps: (1) QCD+$Q$ is renormalizable with
a finite set of counterterms ($Z_Q$, $Z_1^{QQg}$ related to $Z_g$ by Slavnov–Taylor identities, $\delta m$): BRST argument
as for HQET [Bagan:1993zv] and Wilson lines [Dorn:1986dt]; JZZ: "the above arguments are valid independent of whether
the heavy quark field … is defined by a timelike or spacelike vector" (asserted). (2) The heavy–light currents
renormalize multiplicatively ($J^B=Z_JJ^R$) by the standard local-operator argument (no lower-dimensional operators with the
same quantum numbers; for the gluonic analogue see §3.6). (3) Integrating out $Q$ gives Theorem 3.1. On the lattice
GJS realize $Q$ as a static-quark-like field with action $\frac{1}{1+am_0}\bar\zeta(\nabla_n+m_0)\zeta$, propagator
$\theta(\xi)e^{-m\xi}W$, $m=a^{-1}\ln(1+am_0)$; the mass term "can not be forbidden by any symmetry".

**Proof architecture B (diagrammatic; [Ishikawa:2017faj], *read*).** Coordinate-space power counting of diagrams with
gluons attached to the link: insertions change the superficial degrees of divergence of the 3-D ($\Delta\omega_3$) and 4-D
($\Delta\omega_4$) loop integrations; "gauge-link-irreducible" (GLI) diagrams have no overall UV divergence from any 3-D
sub-integration (the $l_z$ integral produces $\delta^{(m)}(r_j-r_0)$ that vanishes at separated link points); hence UV
divergences arise only when all loop momenta are large, are local in coordinate space, and come from three topologies:
(a) link self-energy (linear + log; the linear part exponentiates: $1+c\int_0^rdr_1+c^2\int\!\!\int+\dots={\cal P}e^{c\,r}$,
$c^{(1)}=-\frac{\alpha_sC_F}{\pi}\frac1a$ in their cut-off), (b) gluon–link vertex corrections (absorbed by coupling
renormalization [Dotsenko:1979wb]), (c) quark–link end-point vertices (constant counterterm $Z_{vq}$ by locality). Result
$\tilde F^R=e^{-C|\xi_z|}Z_w^{-1}Z_v^{-1}\tilde F^b$, no mixing. Gluons: [Li:2018tpe] (requires a gauge-invariant UV regulator).

**Explicit checks.** One loop: many groups. Two loops (DR): [Ji:2015jwa] (assuming the heavy–light equivalence at two loops,
multiplicativity verified). Three loops: anomalous dimension and VEV of the quark operator [Braun:2020ymy] *(read)*;
heavy–light current to three loops [Chetyrkin:2003vi].

**Grade [A].** Justification: two independent all-order arguments, explicit multi-loop confirmation, and the classic
Wilson-line results; but (i) the spacelike-$n$ extension of the HQET/BRST renormalizability proof is asserted rather than
written out; (ii) the IMQY power counting is at the level of superficial degrees and "bookkeeping forests", not a full
BPHZ/Zimmermann proof; (iii) at $z=0$ the operator is a local current with different renormalization (contact terms
from the $Q$ equation of motion), so all statements are for $z\neq0$.

### 3.3 Anomalous dimension; space-like = time-like

With $\mu\frac{d}{d\mu}O^R=\gamma_O O^R$: $\gamma_O=2\gamma_{hl}$, where (BCK convention $a_s=\alpha_s/(4\pi)$,
$\mu^2\frac{d}{d\mu^2}$) $(\gamma_Q)_1=2=\frac32C_F$, $(\gamma_Q)_2=\frac{127}{9}+\frac{28}{27}\pi^2-\frac{10}{9}n_f$,
$(\gamma_Q)_3$ given in [Braun:2020ymy] App. A *(read)* ($C_F=4/3$ inserted there). Hence at one loop
$\frac{d\ln O^R}{d\ln\mu^2}=\frac{3\alpha_sC_F}{4\pi}$, which is the coefficient of $\ln\mu^2$ in the one-loop
$\overline{\rm MS}$ matrix element $C_0=1+a(\frac32L_z+\frac52)$ (`s1` C). **Theorem 3.2 (BCK).** Green functions of the
non-local operator for space-like and time-like Wilson lines are related by an explicit substitution rule
(analytic continuation in $v^2$: $\ln^n(-2\omega)\to\ln^n(2\omega)+\dots-i\frac\pi2\dots$); renormalization constants and
anomalous dimensions coincide for space-like and time-like separations, to all orders. **Grade [A]** (argument via
analytic continuation; explicit 3-loop agreement).

### 3.4 The linear divergence, its renormalon, and leading-renormalon resummation

On the lattice $\delta m=m_{-1}(a)/a+m_0$; $m_{-1}=O(\alpha_s)$ is universal, while $m_0=O(\Lambda_{\rm QCD})$ depends on the
condition used to fix $\delta m$ (static potential $V^R(L_0)=0$, large-$z$ decay of hadron or vacuum matrix elements,
auxiliary-field propagator, RI/MOM quark Green function) [Ji:2020brr] *(read)*. The perturbative series for the
linearly divergent self-energy has the pole-mass renormalon at $u=1/2$: truncating at $n\sim1/\alpha_s$ leaves an
ambiguity $O(a\Lambda)\cdot\frac1a=O(\Lambda)$ in $\delta m$ [Beneke:1994sw, Bigi:1994em]. Consequences:
(i) a residual $e^{-m_0|z|}$ changes the quasi-PDF by a Lorentzian smearing of width $\delta=m_0/P^z$:
$\tilde f_1-\tilde f_2=\frac1\pi\int dy'\frac{\delta}{\delta^2+(y-y')^2}[\tilde f_2(y')-\tilde f_2(y)]=O(\delta)$ —
a **linear** power correction $O(\Lambda/P^z)$ [Ji:2020brr]; (ii) to restore $O(\Lambda^2/P_z^2)$ accuracy $m_0$ must be
chosen consistently with the renormalon prescription used in the matching kernel — the **leading-renormalon resummation
(LRR)**: $\delta m$ and the Wilson coefficients are both defined with the same (e.g. principal-value) Borel prescription
[Zhang:2023bxs, Holligan:2023rex] *(abstract + read for 2305.05212)*. **Grade [A]** for the cancellation pattern at
large-$\beta_0$; implementation is scheme choice.

### 3.5 Lattice-specific issues

* **Mixing** (chirally non-symmetric fermions): $\phi_R=Z_\phi(\phi+r_{\rm mix}\slashed n\phi)$ for the heavy–light
  bilinear; hence for $z\ne0$ [Green:2017xeu] *(read)*
  $$O^R_\Gamma(z)=Z_\phi^2e^{-m|z|}O_{\Gamma'}(z),\qquad\Gamma'=\Gamma+r_{\rm mix}{\rm sgn}(z)\{\slashed n,\Gamma\}+r_{\rm mix}^2\slashed n\Gamma\slashed n,$$
  three parameters ($m$ linear, $Z_\phi$ log, $r_{\rm mix}$ finite, $O(g^2)$). Consistent with one-loop lattice
  perturbation theory [Constantinou:2017sej] and with the symmetry analysis [Chen:2017mie]. For $\Gamma=\gamma^t$,
  $\{\gamma^z,\gamma^t\}=0$: no mixing at $O(a^0)$ — the reason for the now standard $\gamma^t$ choice. Static-quark
  relations $Z_V^{\rm stat}=Z_\phi^+$, $Z_A^{\rm stat}=Z_\phi^-$ [GJS]. Improvement and scheme conversion: [Green:2020xco].
* **Order of limits $a\to0$ vs $z\to0$:** the lattice matrix element is finite at $z=0$ (conserved current) while the
  $\overline{\rm MS}$ one has $\ln z^2$ singularities ("$\ln[(z^2+a^2)/a^2]$" behaviour) — motivation of ratio/hybrid schemes.
* **Euclidean vs Minkowski:** equal-time matrix elements computed in Euclidean lattice QCD coincide with the Minkowski LSZ
  matrix elements; uniqueness proved to all orders in PT [Briceno:2017cpo] *(abstract)* — **[A]**.
* **Finite volume:** effects for spatially non-local operators are exponentially small but grow with $z$ [Briceno:2018lfj] *(abstract)*.
* **Gauge fixing precision** matters for gauge-fixed (RI/MOM, Coulomb) renormalization [Zhang:2024omt] *(title)*.
* **Gradient flow** alternative: smeared quasi-PDFs are finite in the continuum and matchable [Monahan:2016bvm, Monahan:2017hpu] *(abstract)*.

### 3.6 Gluon operators

$O_g(z)=g_\perp^{\mu\nu}F^{n_1}{}_{\mu}(z/2)W^{ab}F^{n_2}{}_{\nu}(-z/2)$, $n_i\in\{\hat e_z,\hat e_t\}$. In the auxiliary-field
picture $J^{\mu\nu}=F^{\mu\nu}_aQ^a$ mixes (Dorn) with $J_2^{\mu\nu}=(n^\nu F^{\mu n}-n^\mu F^{\nu n})Q/n^2$ and with an EOM
operator $J_3$; for $z\ne0$ and transverse indices the relevant currents renormalize multiplicatively; four independent
unpolarized (three helicity) gluon quasi-PDF correlators are multiplicatively renormalizable on the lattice
[Zhang:2018diq] *(abstract; RMP read)*; diagrammatic proof [Li:2018tpe]. One-loop: $Z_{J^{n_ti}}=1+\frac{\alpha_s}{4\pi}(\frac76C_A-\frac43n_fT_F)\frac1\epsilon$,
$Z_{J^{n_z\nu}}=1+\frac{\alpha_s}{4\pi}(\frac16C_A-\frac43n_fT_F)\frac1\epsilon$ (RMP *read*); two-loop ADs [Braun:2020ymy].
Gluon and singlet-quark quasi-PDF operators do not mix under renormalization (mixing appears only in the matching, §4.10).
**Grade [A].**

(Thm 4.3) holds in scheme $X$ with $C^X=\bar Z'_X\otimes C$ ($\bar Z'_X$ = Fourier transform of $Z'_X(z)$ at fixed parton momentum,
a convolution in $\xi$) provided the conversion factor
$Z'_X(z)$ is perturbative **for all $z$ that contribute to the Fourier transform**. For the ratio and RI/MOM schemes
$Z'_X$ contains $\alpha_s(1/|z|)$ logarithms; at $|z|\sim\Lambda^{-1}$ this is not perturbative, so momentum-space
matching in those schemes is **not justified** beyond the short-distance region — "it is not legitimate to use
momentum-space factorization for any other scheme that differ from $\overline{\rm MS}$ non-perturbatively" [Ji:2020ect]
*(read)*. The hybrid scheme satisfies the condition by construction (conversion constant for $|z|>z_S$). **Grades:**
ratio/RI-MOM momentum-space matching at large $z$: **[C]** (unjustified; error is a non-perturbative distortion
concentrated at the end points); hybrid: **[A]** (inherits Thm 4.3).

**Proposition 3.4 (hybrid kernel, corrected).** For $\Gamma=\gamma^t$ and $Z_X$ = zero-momentum matrix element,
the coordinate kernel is $\mathcal C_{\rm hyb}(\alpha,z)=\mathcal C_{\rm ratio}(\alpha,z)+\frac{3a}{2}\delta(1-\alpha)\ln\frac{z^2}{z_S^2}\theta(|z|-z_S)$

C_{\rm hyb}(\xi)=C_{\rm ratio}(\xi)+\frac{3a}{2}\Big[-\frac{1}{|1-\xi|}+\frac{2\,{\rm Si}((1-\xi)\lambda_S)}{\pi(1-\xi)}\Big]^{[-\infty,\infty]}_{+(1)},\qquad\lambda_S=z_Sp^z .
$$
*Proof.* For $u=\xi-1\neq0$: $\int\frac{d\zeta}{2\pi}e^{iu\zeta}\ln\frac{\zeta^2}{\lambda_S^2}\theta(|\zeta|-\lambda_S)

$\int du\,[\dots]=2\ln(\lambda_Se^{\gamma_E})$. ∎ Verified numerically (`s2`, 24 checks, $10^{-12}$). **Grade [R]$_{1L}$.**

## 4. The matching (factorization) theorem for quasi-PDFs

### 4.1 Precise statement with explicit hypotheses

Notation: $O^R_\Gamma(z,\mu)$ is the $\overline{\rm MS}$-renormalized operator of Thm 3.1; $\tilde h(\lambda,z^2;\mu)$ its
hadronic ITD (Def. 1.2); $I(\lambda,\mu)$, $f(x,\mu)$ the light-cone ITD/PDF; iso-vector (non-singlet) quark case unless
stated. The following hypotheses are used (grade of each hypothesis in brackets):

* **(H1) multiplicative renormalizability** — Theorem 3.1 **[A]**.
* **(H2) space-like OPE in renormalized QCD perturbation theory.** As $z^2\to0$ at fixed $\lambda$,
  $$O^R_{\gamma^\alpha}(z,\mu)=\sum_{n\ge0}\Big[C_n(-z^2\mu^2)\frac{(-i)^n}{n!}z_{\mu_1}\cdots z_{\mu_n}O_1^{\alpha\mu_1\dots\mu_n}(\mu)
  +C'_n(-z^2\mu^2)\frac{(-i)^n}{n!}z_{\mu_1}\cdots z_{\mu_n}O_2^{\alpha\mu_1\dots\mu_n}(\mu)\Big]+\text{(higher twist)},$$
  with $O_{1,2}$ the traceless symmetric twist-2 quark/gluon operators, $C_n=1+O(\alpha_s)$, $C'_n=O(\alpha_s)$ (zero for
  non-singlet) [Izubuchi:2018srq] *(read; sign of $(-i)^n$ depends on the derivative convention; with our ITD phase the
  hadronic expansion is $\sum_nC_n\frac{(i\lambda)^n}{n!}a_{n+1}$)*. Status: proven for products of local operators in scalar
  theories (Zimmermann; Wilson–Zimmermann; Brandt); for QCD "widely assumed" (IJJSZ's words) — via the auxiliary field
  the non-local operator is a product of two local operators of a non-covariant extended theory, for which no
  Zimmermann-level proof is written **[A]** (cf. monograph Ch. 4–5 and Ch. 16 on the status of the OPE).
* **(H3) uniformity in Ioffe time.** For hadron states and $0<-z^2\le z_0^2$, the twist expansion of $\tilde h$ holds with a
  remainder bounded uniformly in $\lambda\in\mathbb R$: $|\tilde h-\mathcal C\otimes I|\le K\,(-z^2)\Lambda^2(1+|\ln(-z^2\mu^2)|)^k$
  **[C]** (perturbatively, for partonic states, the coefficient functions are entire in $\lambda$ — Cor. 1.5 — but the
  hadronic remainder bound is non-perturbative).
* **(H4) structure of the leading power correction.** The $O(z^2)$ term has the form
  $z^2\Lambda^2\int_{-1}^1d\alpha\,e^{i\alpha\lambda}\rho_4(\alpha,z^2)$ with $\rho_4$ supported in $[-1,1]$, smooth inside
  (multi-parton generalization by Prop. 1.4) **[A]** in PT (twist-4 OPE) / **[C]** non-perturbatively.
* **(H5) large-distance control.** The region $|z|>z_0$ contributes to $\tilde q(x,P^z)$ at fixed $x\in(0,1)$ only beyond
  leading power: $\int_{|\lambda|>z_0P^z}\frac{d\lambda}{2\pi}e^{-ix\lambda}\big[\tilde h-\mathcal C\otimes I\big]=o(1)$ as
  $P^z\to\infty$ (rate specified when needed) **[C]**. (Support: exponential decay at fixed $P^z$, §7; Regge/threshold
  decay of the leading twist; renormalon models §5.)

**Theorem 4.1 (coordinate-space factorization; pseudo-PDF form).** Under (H1)–(H2), for partonic states and to all orders
in $\alpha_s$ at leading twist,
$$
\tilde h(\lambda,z^2;\mu_R)=\int_{-1}^{1}d\alpha\;\mathcal C\Big(\alpha,-z^2\mu^2,\frac{\mu_R}{\mu}\Big)\,I(\alpha\lambda,\mu),\qquad
\mathcal C(\alpha,-z^2\mu^2)\equiv\int\frac{d\lambda}{2\pi}e^{-i\alpha\lambda}\sum_nC_n(-z^2\mu^2)\frac{(i\lambda)^n}{n!},
$$
${\rm supp}\,\mathcal C\subset[-1,1]$; equivalently $\mathcal P(x,-z^2)=\int_{|x|}^1\frac{dy}{|y|}\mathcal C(\frac xy,-z^2\mu^2)f(y,\mu)+(y<0\ \text{analog})$,
and $\int_{-1}^1dx\,x^n\mathcal P(x,-z^2)=C_n(-z^2\mu^2)a_{n+1}(\mu)$. With (H3) the same holds for hadrons up to
$O(z^2\Lambda^2,z^2M^2)$ uniformly in $\lambda$. **Grades:** perturbative statement **[A]** (cond. on H2); hadronic remainder
**[C]**; one-loop $\mathcal C$ **[R]$_{1L}$**; two-loop **[R]$_{2L}$** [Li:2020xml].

**Theorem 4.2 (parton momentum).** If Thm 4.1 holds for all $z$ (as an identity of formal power series for partonic states),
then the quasi-PDF obeys
$$
\tilde q(x,P^z;\mu_R)=\int_{-1}^{1}\frac{dy}{|y|}\,C\Big(\frac xy,\frac{\mu_R}{\mu},\frac{\mu}{|y|P^z}\Big)f(y,\mu),\qquad
C(\xi,\tfrac{\mu}{p^z})=\int\frac{d\lambda}{2\pi}e^{-i\xi\lambda}\int_{-1}^1d\alpha\,e^{i\alpha\lambda}\,\mathcal C\Big(\alpha,\frac{\lambda^2\mu^2}{p_z^2}\Big).
$$
*Proof.* Insert Thm 4.1 into Def. 1.2: $\tilde q(x)=\int\frac{d\lambda}{2\pi}e^{-ix\lambda}\int d\alpha\,\mathcal C(\alpha,\lambda^2\mu^2/P_z^2)
\int dy\,e^{i\alpha y\lambda}f(y)$; substitute $\lambda'=y\lambda$ ($d\lambda=d\lambda'/|y|$, $\lambda^2/P_z^2=\lambda'^2/(yP^z)^2$,
$e^{-ix\lambda}=e^{-i\frac xy\lambda'}$). ∎ **Grade [R]** given Thm 4.1. In particular the argument is the *parton*
momentum $|y|P^z$; using $P^z$ (early literature) is wrong at $O(\alpha_s)$ **[V]** [Izubuchi:2018srq] *(read)*.

**Theorem 4.3 (LaMET matching theorem).** (i) *[Perturbative]* Under (H1)–(H2) the momentum-space factorization of
Thm 4.2 holds for partonic states at leading power, to all orders; the same follows independently from the
collinear-subtraction (CFP) analysis of §4.3. **Grade [A].** (ii) *[Hadronic]* Under (H1)–(H5),
$$
\tilde q_\Gamma(x,P^z)=\int_{-1}^1\frac{dy}{|y|}C_\Gamma\Big(\frac xy,\frac{\mu}{|y|P^z}\Big)q^\Gamma_M(y,\mu)
+\frac{\Lambda^2}{P_z^2}\,\rho_4''(x)+o(P_z^{-2}),
$$
where $q^\Gamma_M$ is the target-mass-transformed PDF of Prop. 5.1 (exact in $M^2/P_z^2$; $q_M=f+O(M^2/P_z^2)$), the sign follows from
(H4) with $z^2=-\lambda^2/P_z^2<0$ (Lemma 5.2), and logarithmic $z^2$-dependence of $\rho_4$ produces only logarithmic modifications.
This implies relative corrections $O\big(\frac{\Lambda^2}{x^2P_z^2}\big)$ at small $x$ and $O\big(\frac{\Lambda^2}{(1-x)^{2-\delta}P_z^2}\big)$ at
large $x$ if $\rho_4/f\sim(1-x)^\delta$ (Cor. 5.3). **Grade:** the implication is **[R]** (this work, Lemma 5.2, Cor. 5.3, Prop. 5.1);
the hypotheses (H3)–(H5) are **[C]**; hence the hadronic statement is **[C]**, with renormalon (§5.3) and model (§11) support.

### 4.2 Proof architecture A — the OPE route (Izubuchi, Ji, Jin, Stewart, Zhao 2018) *(read)*

1. *Multiplicative renormalization* of $\tilde O_\Gamma(z)$ in coordinate space, $\tilde O_\Gamma(z,\mu)=Z_{\psi,z}e^{\delta m|z|}\tilde O_\Gamma(z,\epsilon)$
   [Ji:2017oey, Ishikawa:2017faj]; in $\overline{\rm MS}$ the power divergence is absent.
2. *OPE* (H2) of the renormalized operator; only the twist-2 quark (and gluon) towers survive at leading power; trace
   terms in $\langle P|O_1^{\mu_0\dots\mu_n}|P\rangle=2a_{n+1}(P^{\mu_0}\cdots P^{\mu_n}-{\rm tr})$ are $O(M^2/P_z^2)$; higher
   twist $O(z^2\Lambda^2)$; "the only approximation we have made so far is ignoring the higher-twist effects".
3. *Re-summation of the tower*: $\tilde h=\sum_nC_n\frac{(i\lambda)^n}{n!}a_{n+1}$ with $a_{n+1}=\int dy\,y^nf(y)$; defining
   $\mathcal C$ as the Fourier transform of the generating series (it "corresponds to a pseudo-PDF with all
   $a_{n+1}=1$"), its support is $[-1,1]$ (Prop. 1.4), giving Thm 4.1.
4. *Quasi-PDF*: Fourier transform at fixed $P^z$ → Thm 4.2 ($p^z=|y|P^z$).
5. *Pseudo-PDF* and *equivalence*: the quasi- and pseudo-PDF are two representations of one function,
   $\tilde q(x,\frac{\mu}{P^z})=\int\frac{d\lambda}{2\pi}e^{-ix\lambda}\int_{-1}^1dy\,e^{iy\lambda}\mathcal P(y,\frac{\mu^2\lambda^2}{P_z^2})$,
   and the kernels obey the relation of Thm 4.2 (valid to all orders). "There is in principle no fundamental difference
   in applying either one to lattice calculations"; they differ in finite-$P^z$ systematics (§8).
6. *Moments*: $\int dx\,x^n\tilde q=(i\frac{d}{d\lambda})^n\tilde h|_{\lambda\to0}$ involves derivatives of
   $C_{n'}(\lambda^2\mu^2/P_z^2)$, which contain $\ln\lambda^2$: the moments of the quasi-PDF do not exist; the pseudo-PDF
   moments do ($=C_na_{n+1}$) because $z^2$ is held fixed.
7. *Other schemes*: $\tilde h^X=Z'_X\tilde h^{\overline{\rm MS}}$ ⇒ $\mathcal C^X=Z'_X\mathcal C$, $C^X=\bar Z'_X\otimes C$
   (Prop. 3.3 for the caveat at large $z$).

*Assessment.* Steps 1, 3–7 are rigorous consequences of step 2 for partonic states (exact identities between formal power
series). Step 2 is the hypothesis (H2). The quasi-PDF requires $\tilde h$ for **all** $z$, while the OPE is an expansion at
small $z$: for partonic states (massless, on shell) there is no other scale and the leading-twist expression is valid for
all $z$ order by order; for hadrons, (H3)–(H5) are needed. IJJSZ state "LaMET is not equivalent to the expansion from the
OPE, as the former is more general" (e.g. TMDs, where no simple OPE exists).

### 4.3 Proof architecture B — collinear subtraction in light-cone gauge (Ma–Qiu 2014; RMP 2021) *(read)*

[Ma:2014jla] (published 2018) and [Ji:2020ect, §III.B]:

1. Represent $\tilde f_{q/q}$ of an on-shell quark as a forward "cut-vertex" diagram with vertex
   $\frac{\gamma^z}{2P^z}\delta(\tilde x-k^z/P^z)$ (resp. $\gamma^0$). Soft divergences cancel between real and virtual
   contributions (the $[\delta(1-\tilde x-y)-\delta(1-\tilde x)]$ structure, current conservation).
2. In light-cone gauge $n\cdot A=0$ ($A^+=0$) the leading collinear regions are ladders
   $\tilde f=C_0\sum_iK^i$ with 2PI kernels [Ellis:1978ty, Curci:1980uw]; the operator (with its space-like link) and its
   multiplicative renormalization are entirely in the top kernel $C_0$ ("the renormalization of quasi-quark distribution
   is local and fully included in $C_0$").
3. Collinear projector $\hat{\mathcal P}W\equiv\int\frac{d^4k}{(2\pi)^4}\delta(x-\frac{k\cdot n}{p\cdot n})\frac{{\rm Tr}[\gamma\cdot n\,W\,\gamma\cdot p]}{4p\cdot n}+{\rm UVCT}$
   (the light-cone PDF cut vertex), $(1-\hat{\mathcal P})W$ collinear finite. Iterating,
   $\tilde f^{\rm ren}=\big[C_{\rm ren}\frac{1}{1-(1-\hat{\mathcal P})K}\big]\big[\frac{1}{1-\hat{\mathcal P}K}\big]$: the second
   factor is the light-cone PDF of the parton, the first is IR safe ⇒ $\tilde f=f\otimes\mathcal C+O((\tilde xP_z)^{-2})$.
4. RMP version (Feynman gauge first): in the collinear region $k^z\simeq k^+/\sqrt2$, $\gamma^z\to\gamma^+/\sqrt2$, so the
   quasi vertex reduces to the light-cone one; longitudinally polarized $A^+$ gluons attach to the space-like link with a
   $1/P^z$ suppression compensated by the boost factor — eliminated in $A^+=0$ gauge. Then: (a) no collinear divergences in
   the upper part $H$; (b) kernels with non-collinear legs are finite; (c) regions $R_i$ ($k_1..k_i$ collinear) are
   subtracted recursively "in a way similar to the BPHZ relation" ⇒ $\tilde q=\int\frac{dx}{x}C(y,x,P^z)q(x,\epsilon_{IR})$ for
   bare quantities; renormalization affects only $C$; the relation is inverted order by order.

*Assessment.* This is the standard CFP/Collins-style collinear factorization (cf. Ch. 7–8): **[A]**. Specific points left
at the sketch level: light-cone-gauge spurious poles $1/(n\cdot k)$ in the presence of the space-like link; the claim that
$C_0$ contains no collinear divergence; equivalence of the cut-vertex representation for the equal-time operator. No
Glauber or final-state issues arise (single hadron, no measured final state). "While the proof is only for perturbative
free quark states, the factorization formulas are widely believed to be true nonperturbatively as well" (RMP).

### 4.4 Proof architecture C — "good lattice cross sections" (Ma–Qiu 2018) *(read)*

[Ma:2017pxb]: a good LCS $\sigma_n(\omega,\xi^2,P^2)=\langle P|T\{\mathcal O_n(\xi)\}|P\rangle$ ($\omega=P\cdot\xi$) is
(i) calculable with Euclidean time ($\xi^0=0$), (ii) renormalizable (continuum limit), (iii) has the same factorizable
collinear divergences as PDFs. Examples: current–current products $\xi^{d_1+d_2-2}Z_1Z_2j_1(\xi)j_2(0)$ (scalar, vector,
flavour-changing $V'$), the Wilson-line bilinear $\bar\psi(\xi)\slashed\xi\Phi\psi(0)$, tensor correlators ($T_{33}$ of
Chambers et al.). Claim: $\sigma_n=\sum_a\int_{-1}^1\frac{dx}{x}f_a(x,\mu)K_n^a(x\omega,\xi^2,x^2P^2,\mu)+O(\xi^2\Lambda^2)$,
$K_n^a=\sum_J\frac{2}{S_a}W^{(J,a)}_n\Sigma_J(x\omega,x^2P^2\xi^2)$ with exact target-mass sums
$\Sigma_J=\sum_i\binom{J-i}{i}\omega^{J-2i}(-P^2\xi^2/4)^i$. Proof: OPE for $|\omega|\ll1$, then **analyticity**: "if we fix
$\xi$ at short distance while we increase $\omega$ … we cannot introduce any new perturbative divergence … $\sigma_n$
remains analytic … only possible singularity is at $\omega=\infty$". LO: $K_q^{q(0)}=2x\omega e^{ix\omega}$,
$K_S^{q(0)}=\frac{i}{\pi^2}x\omega(e^{ix\omega}-e^{-ix\omega})$; momentum space $\tilde K^{q(0)}_{S}=-2i\frac{x^2\tilde\omega^2}{1-x^2\tilde\omega^2}$
with cuts for $\tilde\omega^2>1$ ("good" only for $\tilde\omega^2<1$).

*Assessment.* For partonic states the coefficient functions are entire in $\omega$ (Cor. 1.5) — **[R]** in PT given
Prop. 1.4. The extension of the *hadronic* remainder $O(\xi^2\Lambda^2)$ from $|\omega|\ll1$ to finite $\omega$ is the
uniformity hypothesis (H3) — **[C]**; the "no new divergence" argument is perturbative.

**Remark 4.5.1 (what a complete proof of N4 would require).** For local operators in renormalizable scalar theories the
short-distance expansion of a T-product is a theorem of renormalized perturbation theory (Wilson 1969 [Wilson:1969zs]; Zimmermann's normal-product
analysis [Zimmermann:1972tv]). The non-local operator is a product $\bar J(z)J(0)$ of two local composite
operators of the extended theory N1, so N4 would follow from an OPE theorem for N1. The two gaps are: (i) the propagator of
$Q$, $\theta(x^z)\delta(x^0)\delta^2(x_\perp)$, is not Lorentz covariant, so the standard Euclidean power counting of
Zimmermann's forest formula has to be redone with the anisotropic degree of divergence (exactly IMQY's "3-D vs 4-D"
counting, which however is formulated only for the renormalization, not for the OPE); (ii) $\det(in\cdot D)$ is set to a
constant, which is legitimate in dimensional regularization/axial-gauge-like arguments but requires care with the
$\delta(0)$-type terms of a non-covariant propagator. Both are technical; no counter-indication is known; the explicit
two- and three-loop computations agree with the OPE structure. This is why N4 is graded [A], not [C].

**Remark 4.5.2 (why there is no Glauber problem for collinear quasi-PDFs).** The quasi-PDF is a matrix element of an
equal-time product of fields at space-like separation; there is no measured final state, no time ordering
(fields at space-like separation (anti)commute), and the external state is a single hadron. The obstruction of Ch. 10
(Glauber pinches in Drell–Yan with two hadrons) cannot arise. For quasi-TMDs the relevant issue is the soft/rapidity
structure (§10); no published dedicated Glauber analysis exists for quasi-TMDs (searched; §13).

**Remark 4.5.3 (where non-perturbative input enters).** All [C] grades in §4 come from three sources: (a) the uniform
twist expansion in the Ioffe time (H3), (b) the large-$|z|$ contribution (H5), (c) the identification of the perturbative
remainder of the partonic calculation with the hadronic higher-twist matrix elements (H4). These are the precise
non-perturbative content of the "LaMET principle" (Conj. 2.1); §5–§7 collect the evidence.

  $[\delta(1-\xi)-\delta(\xi-k^z/p^z)]/((1-\xi)p^z)$; numerator $-2(p^0k^z+p^zk^0)$; result

  DR); its Fourier transform at $\xi\neq1$ gives $\tilde q_{\rm t}=-\frac{a}{|1-\xi|}$.

Sum (`s1` D2, exact for 9 values):

## 5. Power corrections

Three distinct sources: (i) **target-mass corrections** (TMC) from the trace terms of twist-2 operators — exactly
computable; (ii) **dynamical higher twist** (twist-4 multi-parton correlators, $O(z^2\Lambda^2)$ in coordinate space);
(iii) **linear** corrections $O(\Lambda/P^z)$ associated with the Wilson-line self-energy (UV renormalon of $\delta m$)
and with the threshold region. In the following $c\equiv M^2/(4P_z^2)$.

### 5.1 Target-mass corrections: exact results

The leading-twist part of the matrix element involves $\langle P|O^{\mu_1\dots\mu_N}|P\rangle=2a_N(P^{\mu_1}\cdots P^{\mu_N}-{\rm traces})$
with $a_N=\int dx\,x^{N-1}q(x)$. In $d=4$, $T_N(z)\equiv z_{\mu_1}\cdots z_{\mu_N}(P^{\mu_1}\cdots P^{\mu_N}-{\rm tr})
=(P\cdot z)^N\sum_{i=0}^{\lfloor N/2\rfloor}\binom{N-i}{i}\big(-\tfrac{P^2z^2}{4(P\cdot z)^2}\big)^i$ (a Chebyshev-$U$/Gegenbauer
$C^{(1)}$ polynomial). For $z=z\hat e_z$ the variable $-P^2z^2/(4(P\cdot z)^2)=c$ is **independent of $z$**, so at fixed $P^z$
each moment is multiplied by a constant.

**Proposition 5.1 (TMC).**
(a) *[$\Gamma=\gamma^z$; Chen, Cohen, Ji, Lin, Zhang 2016, App. B (read)]* $\langle x^{N-1}\rangle_{\tilde q}=K_N\langle x^{N-1}\rangle_q$,
$K_N=\sum_i\binom{N-i}{i}c^i$, and, with $f_\pm=\sqrt{1+4c}\pm1$,
$$
\tilde q_{\gamma^z}(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+4c}}\Big[\frac{f_+}{2}\,q\Big(\frac{2x}{f_+}\Big)-\frac{f_-}{2}\,q\Big(-\frac{2x}{f_-}\Big)\Big],
$$
with inverse $q(x)=\sqrt{1+4c}\sum_{n\ge0}\frac{f_-^n}{f_+^{n+1}}\big[(1+(-1)^n)\tilde q(\frac{f_+^{n+1}x}{2f_-^n})+(1-(-1)^n)\tilde q(-\frac{f_+^{n+1}x}{2f_-^n})\big]$
[Chen:2016utp]. Particle number is conserved; the support becomes $[-f_-/2,f_+/2]\simeq[-c,1+c]$.
(b) *[$\Gamma=\gamma^0$; new closed form, this work]* Using $u_{\mu_1}z_{\mu_2}\cdots z_{\mu_N}T^{\mu_1\dots\mu_N}=\frac1N\,u\cdot\partial_zT_N(z)$ with
$u=\hat e_t$, $u\cdot z=0$:
$$
K^\perp_N=\sum_i\binom{N-i}{i}\frac{N-2i}{N}c^i=K_N-\frac{2c}{N}\frac{dK_N}{dc},\qquad
\tilde q_{\gamma^0}(x;c)=\tilde q_{\gamma^z}(x;c)-2c\int_0^1\frac{dy}{y}\,\partial_c\tilde q_{\gamma^z}\Big(\frac xy;c\Big),
$$
using that $g\mapsto\int_0^1\frac{dy}{y}g(x/y)$ divides the $N$-th moment by $N$. Consequences: $K_1^\perp=K_2^\perp=1$ — **no
target-mass correction to the normalization and to the momentum fraction $\langle x\rangle$ for $\gamma^0$** (for $N=2$ the
operator is $T^{0z}$ and $g^{0z}=0$); $K_3^\perp=1+\frac23c$, $K_4^\perp=1+\frac32c$ (vs. $K_3=1+2c$, $K_4=1+3c+c^2$).
(c) *[expansion]* At $O(c)$: $\tilde q_{\gamma^z}=q-c\,(xq'+q)$ and $\tilde q_{\gamma^0}=q-c\,(xq'+3q)+2c\int_{|x|}^1\frac{dy}{y}q(\tfrac xy)$,
which are exactly eqs. (target-parallel) and (target-perp) of [Braun:2018brg] *(read)* (with $v^2=-1$, $(pv)^2=P_z^2$) — an
independent check of (b).
(d) *[commutation with matching]* Because $c$ is $\lambda$-independent at fixed $P^z$, the leading-twist OPE gives, to all orders in
$\alpha_s$, $\tilde q_\Gamma=C_\Gamma\otimes q^\Gamma_M$, where $q^\Gamma_M$ is the TMC-transformed PDF of (a)/(b) (the
$\gamma^z$ case includes the $z^\alpha$ structure of the OPE, which also scales with $K_N$). For the pseudo-PDF the TMC parameter
$-M^2z^2/(4\lambda^2)$ varies with $\lambda$ and the TMC is $O(z^2M^2)$, suppressed as $O(1-x)$ at large $x$ [Braun:2018brg].
**Grades:** (a)–(c) **[R]** (algebraic, given the twist-2 OPE); (b) is new (`s6`: 40/40 checks — $K^\perp_N$ identity for
$N\le12$, moments of the closed forms, inverse series, $O(c)$ terms vs BVZ for both $\Gamma$); (d) **[R]** cond. on (H2).
Size: [Radyushkin:2017ffo] *(abstract)*: TMCs "are rather small", and in TMD-based models they are cancelled by
higher-twist contributions; changes "may be safely neglected for $p_3\gtrsim2M$". (An earlier TMC formula [Alexandrou:2015rja]
did not conserve particle number [Chen:2016utp].)

### 5.2 Dynamical twist-4: the Fourier lemma (this work)

**Lemma 5.2 ($z^2\leftrightarrow\partial_x^2$).** Let $\rho$ be a distribution supported in $[-1,1]$, $r(\lambda)=\int_{-1}^1d\alpha\,e^{i\alpha\lambda}\rho(\alpha)$
(an entire function, Cor. 1.5). At fixed $P^z$ (so that $z^2=-\lambda^2/P_z^2$),
$$
\int\frac{d\lambda}{2\pi}e^{-ix\lambda}\Big[z^2\Lambda^2\,r(\lambda)\Big]=+\frac{\Lambda^2}{P_z^2}\,\rho''(x)
$$
as distributions on $\mathbb R$; pointwise for $x$ in any open interval where $\rho\in C^2$. If $\rho(x)\sim x^{a}$ ($x\to0^+$) and
$\sim(1-x)^{b}$ ($x\to1^-$), then $\rho''/\rho\to a(a-1)/x^2$ resp. $b(b-1)/(1-x)^2$.
*Proof.* $\lambda^2e^{-ix\lambda}=-\partial_x^2e^{-ix\lambda}$, and $\int\frac{d\lambda}{2\pi}e^{-ix\lambda}r(\lambda)=\rho(x)$; since
$z^2\Lambda^2r=-\frac{\Lambda^2}{P_z^2}\lambda^2r$, the transform is $-\frac{\Lambda^2}{P_z^2}(-\rho'')$. The endpoint ratios are elementary. ∎
(`s5` L1: numerical check with $\rho\propto x^{3.5}(1-x)^{4.5}$, $10^{-6}$.) **Grade [R].**
*Remarks.* (1) Logarithmic $z^2$-dependence of the twist-4 coefficient (twist-4 anomalous dimensions) adds convolutions with the
transform of $\ln\lambda^2$ ($-2\gamma_E\delta(x)-[1/|x|]_{\rm reg}$) and modifies the endpoint powers by logarithms only.
(2) This is the mechanism behind every "$\Lambda^2/(x^2P_z^2)$" statement in the literature: a $z^2$-suppressed remainder in
coordinate space becomes a *second derivative* in $x$.

**Corollary 5.3 (endpoint dichotomy).** Let $q\sim(1-x)^b$ and $\rho_4\sim(1-x)^{b+\delta}$ at $x\to1$. Then the relative
power correction to $\tilde q$ behaves as $\frac{\Lambda^2}{P_z^2}(b+\delta)(b+\delta-1)(1-x)^{\delta-2}\times$const. Hence:
$\delta=1$ gives $\Lambda^2/((1-x)P_z^2)$ (BVZ renormalon model, see 5.3), $\delta=0$ gives $\Lambda^2/((1-x)^2P_z^2)$
(the RMP/Ji power counting "spectator momentum $(1-x)P^z$"). Near $x\to0$ with $q\sim x^{a}$, $\rho_4\sim x^{a}$: $\Lambda^2/(x^2P_z^2)$.
*The two published large-$x$ counting rules are therefore not in conflict: they are statements about the (unknown) endpoint
behaviour of the twist-4 distribution.* **Grade [R]** as an implication; the endpoint behaviour of $\rho_4$ itself is **[C]**.

### 5.3 Renormalon model of the twist-4 term (Braun, Vladimirov, Zhang 2018) *(read)*

Bubble-chain (large-$\beta_0$) Borel transform of the coordinate-space coefficient function. Results: the UV renormalon at
$w=1/2$, $B[H]\stackrel{w\to1/2}{=}\frac{-4C_F}{w-1/2}\sqrt X$, is the Wilson-line linear divergence (removed by
normalization, §3.4); the leading IR renormalon is at $w=1$,
$B[H^\parallel](w)\stackrel{w\to1}{=}\frac{-4C_F}{1-w}[\alpha+\bar\alpha\ln\bar\alpha]X$,
$B[H^\perp]\stackrel{w\to1}{=}\frac{-4C_F}{1-w}[\alpha+\bar\alpha\ln\bar\alpha+\alpha\bar\alpha]X$. Assuming the ambiguity is
cancelled by a twist-4 term of the same functional form:
$$
\mathcal I^{\parallel}(\tau)=I(\tau)+\kappa\,z^2\Lambda^2\int_0^1d\alpha\,(\alpha+\bar\alpha\ln\bar\alpha)\,I(\alpha\tau),\qquad
|\kappa|\simeq\frac{\pi e^{5/3+2\gamma_E}}{4\beta_0}\simeq1.5\ (\text{indicative; sign undetermined})
$$
($z^2<0$ is the 4-vector square; ∥/⊥ = $\Gamma$ along/perpendicular to $z$, i.e. $\gamma^z$/$\gamma^0$). Normalizing to the
$P=0$ matrix element replaces $k(\alpha)$ by $[k(\alpha)]_+$ (normalization to the vacuum correlator does not change the
power correction). Momentum space (Lemma 5.2):
$$
\mathcal Q^\parallel(x)=q(x)+\frac{\kappa\Lambda^2}{P_z^2}\frac{d^2}{dx^2}\int_{|x|}^1\frac{dy}{y}(y+\bar y\ln\bar y)\,q\Big(\frac xy\Big)
=q(x)+\frac{\kappa\Lambda^2}{x^2P_z^2}\Big\{\int_{|x|}^1\frac{dy}{y}\frac{y^2}{[1-y]_+}q\Big(\frac xy\Big)+q(x)-|x|q'(x)\Big\},
$$
with the $[0,1]$ plus distribution (so that $q(x)\ln(1-x)$ appears when the integral is written over $[x,1]$; §0.3 item 3:
BVZ's eq. (cR) omits this term, eq. (t4-Qparallel) is correct, `s4`). Hence
$\mathcal Q=q\{1+O(\frac{\Lambda^2}{x^2(1-x)P_z^2})\}$ while $\mathcal P(x,z)=q\{1+O(z^2\Lambda^2(1-x))\}$; the
$P=0$ normalization adds $-\frac14\bar xx^2q''/q$ to $R^\parallel$ ($-\frac5{12}$ for ⊥), i.e. a $\Lambda^2/((1-x)^2P_z^2)$ term
(Cor. 5.3 with $\delta=0$, because $[k]_+$ contains $-\frac14\delta(\bar\alpha)$; $\int_0^1k=\frac14$).
**Grade [P]**: rigorous statement about the large-order behaviour of the bubble chain; the identification with the actual
twist-4 matrix element (functional form, $\kappa$) is a model **[C]**. Extension to quasi-/pseudo-GPDs: [Braun:2024snf]
*(abstract)* — the point $x=\pm\xi$ is special: corrections suppressed by the **first** power of the hard scale, from soft
momenta, unrelated to the Wilson-line UV renormalon; normalization can strongly suppress power corrections.

### 5.4 Linear power corrections and their removal

(1) *Residual mass.* A residual $e^{-m_0|z|}$ ($m_0=O(\Lambda)$ from the renormalon ambiguity of $\delta m$) smears the quasi-PDF
with a Lorentzian of width $m_0/P^z$ (§3.4): an $O(\Lambda/(xP^z))$ effect in hybrid/mass-subtracted schemes [Ji:2020brr].
(2) *Matching coefficients.* In $\overline{\rm MS}$ the Wilson coefficients $C_k(\alpha_s(\mu),z^2\mu^2)$ of the quasi-ITD carry an
IR renormalon at $u=\frac12$ (same strength as the pole mass; BVZ classify the same singularity as the UV renormalon of the
Wilson-line self-energy — in $\overline{\rm MS}$, where the linear divergence is not subtracted, it shows up in the coefficient
functions) whose ambiguity is linear in $z$ — a "twist-three" term
$\propto m_0z$ multiplying the leading twist universally (spin-independent). **LRR** [Zhang:2023bxs] *(read)*: resum the
leading renormalon in $C_k$ with a prescription (PV), fix $m_0$ from the $P^z=0$ lattice matrix element at short $z$ consistently
with that prescription; result $m_0=0.161^{+0.025}_{-0.002}$ GeV (NLO), $0.164^{+0.016}_{-0.003}$ GeV (NNLO) on their ensemble;
"improvement of matching accuracy by a factor of more than 3–5" for $x=0.2$–$0.5$ at $P^z=1.9$ GeV. Normalized (ratio)
schemes have no $w=\frac12$ term [Braun:2018brg]. **Grade [A]** (large-$\beta_0$ renormalon structure, pole-mass normalization
cross-checked with lattice perturbation theory to 20 loops [Zhang:2023bxs]); the PV prescription is a scheme choice.
(3) *Threshold region.* The heavy–light Sudakov hard kernel has a linear IR renormalon in its **imaginary part** (strength
equal to the pole-mass renormalon in the large-$\beta_0$ approximation); it correlates with UV renormalons of NLP operators
in the threshold expansion of the quasi-PDF kernel and in the TMD expansion of quasi-LFWF amplitudes, "anticipating the
presence of linear power correction"; the light–light Sudakov kernel has none [Liu:2023onm] *(read)*. **Grade [P]**
(bubble chain). Relevance: slow convergence of the imaginary part of the lattice Collins–Soper kernel (§10.3).

### 5.5 Small $x$ and the limits of the expansion

From Lemma 5.2/Cor. 5.3 and the RG analysis of §4.8, the expansion parameter at small $x$ is $\Lambda/(|x|P^z)$, and the
perturbative matching itself involves $\alpha_s(2|x|P^z)$: LaMET is predictive only for $2|x|P^z\gg\Lambda$; numerically the RGR
analysis places the breakdown near $2xP^z\approx0.7$ GeV [Su:2022fiu]; Ji: "the expansion breaks down at small $x\sim\Lambda/P$"
[Ji:2024oka]. The region $x\lesssim\Lambda/P^z$ (and correspondingly $1-x\lesssim\Lambda/P^z$) must be **excluded** or
complemented by other information (e.g. moments, §8). **Status:** this is part of the theorem's statement (domain of
validity), not a defect.

## 6. Threshold factorization and resummation

### 6.1 Theorem 6.1 (threshold factorization of the matching kernel; Ji, Liu, Su 2023) *(read)*

**Statement.** For the non-singlet $\overline{\rm MS}$ kernel ($\Gamma=\gamma^t$), at leading power in $1-\xi$ (all terms
$\propto(1-\xi)^{-1}\ln^k|1-\xi|$ and $\delta(1-\xi)$), to all orders in $\alpha_s$:
$$
C\Big(\xi,\frac{p^z}{\mu}\Big)\Big|_{\xi\to1}=H\Big(\frac{4p_z^2}{\mu^2}\Big)\,p^z\,J_f\Big(\frac{(1-\xi)p^z}{\mu},\frac{4p_z^2}{\mu^2}\Big),\qquad p^z=|y|P^z,
$$
where (i) $H(L_z)\equiv|H_{\rm HL}(L_z)|$, $L_z\equiv\ln(4p_z^2/\mu^2)$, is the modulus of the **heavy–light Sudakov form
factor** (a light on-shell quark turned into a static space-like link), identical to the hard kernel of quasi-TMD and
quasi-LFWF factorization; $H_{\rm HL}(\frac{\zeta_z}{\mu^2},{\rm sgn}z)=H^{-{\rm sgn}z}_{\rm LFWF}$,
$H^{\pm}_{\rm LFWF}=1+\frac{\alpha_sC_F}{4\pi}\big(-4-\frac{5\pi^2}{6}+2L_\pm-L_\pm^2\big)$, $L_\pm=L_z\pm i\pi$ [Ji:2021znw];
(ii) $J_f(z\mu)\equiv\tilde J(z^2\mu^2)\,e^{i\,{\rm sgn}(z)A(L_z)}$ with ${\rm sgn}(z)A={\rm Arg}\,H_{\rm HL}$, and the
**space-like jet function** $\tilde J(\mu z)=\langle\Omega|\mathcal T\,W_{n,+}(zn_z)W_z^\dagger(zn_z)W_z(0)W_{n,-}(0)|\Omega\rangle$ (light-like
links $W_{n,\pm}$ and the space-like link $W_z$; a "transition form factor" of a light-like into a space-like static
source) — real, below threshold, depending only on $|z|$, conjugate to the semi-hard scale $|1-\xi|p^z$.
(Our operator $\bar\psi(z)\gamma^tW[z,0]\psi(0)$ coincides with JLS's, so ${\rm sgn}(z)$ means the same thing; their
$\lambda=zp^z=-\lambda_{\rm ours}$.)

One-loop ingredients ($a=\alpha_sC_F/2\pi$, $l_z=\ln(e^{2\gamma_E}\mu^2z^2/4)$):
$H^{(1)}=a\big(-2+\frac{\pi^2}{12}-\frac{L_z^2}{2}+L_z\big)$, $\tilde J^{(1)}=a\big(\frac{l_z^2}{2}+l_z+\frac{\pi^2}{12}+2\big)$,
${\rm Im}\,J_f^{(1)}=a\pi\,{\rm sgn}(z)(L_z-1)$. RGEs:
$\frac{d\ln H}{d\ln\mu}=\Gamma_{\rm cusp}L_z+\tilde\gamma_H$, $\frac{d\ln\tilde J}{d\ln\mu}=\Gamma_{\rm cusp}l_z-\tilde\gamma_J$,
$\frac{d\ln H_{\rm HL}}{d\ln\mu}=\Gamma_{\rm cusp}L_z+\tilde\gamma_H-i\pi\,{\rm sgn}(z)\Gamma_{\rm cusp}$;
$\tilde\gamma_J=2\gamma_{HL}-2\gamma_s=-\frac{\alpha_sC_F}{\pi}+O(\alpha_s^2)$ (equal to the time-like heavy-quark jet function
anomalous dimension [Jain:2008gb]); $\tilde\gamma_H=\gamma_C$ of the quasi-TMD hard kernel. Momentum space:
$p^zJ_f=\delta(1-y)[1+a(2+\frac{\pi^2}{4}+\frac{L_z^2}{2}-L_z)]+a[\mathcal P\frac{\ln(1-y)^2}{|1-y|}+(L_z-1)(\mathcal P\frac{1}{1-y}+\mathcal P\frac{1}{|1-y|})]$.

**Proof architecture.** (1) *Leading region* (fig. 1 of JLS): collinear quark lines with hard vertices at the two
quark–link junctions (scale $\zeta_z=4p_z^2$), and semi-hard gluons at $|1-\xi|p^z$ exchanged between the collinear
quark and the static link — analogous to DIS at $x\to1$ [Sterman:1986aj, Becher:2006mr] (cited there). (2) The jet
function is defined as a vacuum Wilson-loop correlator; it is related to the time-like heavy-quark jet function by
$t\to-i|z|$ "to all orders in PT" (two proofs in JLS App. E), which imports the known NNLO result [Jain:2008gb].
(3) Consistency of RGEs: the combined evolution reproduces the threshold limit of DGLAP (JLS §6). At one loop the sum of
anomalous dimensions is $\Gamma_{\rm cusp}[\ln(\lambda^2e^{2\gamma_E})-i\pi\,{\rm sgn}z]$, $\mu$-independent, as it must be
(checked here). (4) *Fixed-order verification*: NLO reproduces the one-loop kernel at power $(1-\xi)^{-1}$;
NNLO: all 41 non-vanishing singular terms ($\delta$, $\frac{\ln^k(1-y)}{1-y}$, $k\le3$) "consistent with the exact
results" [Li:2020xml, Chen:2020ody], with two constants $c_a$ (two-loop phase) and $c_H$ (two-loop $\ln H$) **extracted** from
that comparison ($c_H=0.0725C_F^2-0.0840C_FC_A+0.1453C_Fn_fT_F$). Independent confirmation: the NNLO quasi-TMD coefficient
function of [delRio:2023pse] *(read)* "agree[s]" with the JLS two-loop $H$.
**Own check (`s3`):** the exact one-loop coordinate kernel satisfies
$e^{i\lambda}\tilde G(\lambda)=1+a[H^{(1)}+J_f^{(1)}]+O(\ln|\lambda|/|\lambda|)$ up to $|\lambda|=10^5$ for three values of
$L_z$, including the sign of the phase for $\lambda<0$; the momentum-space $(1-\xi)^{-1}$ terms are reproduced on both sides
of $\xi=1$. Also the hard kernel of the quasi-TMD matching determined in `s4` equals $H^{(1)}$ (universality, §10.2).
**Grade:** **[A]** to all orders (leading-region analysis + RG consistency, standard SCET-type argument, not a full
Collins-style proof); **[R]$_{1L}$**, **[R]$_{2L}$** (NNLO check with two fitted constants; dRV cross-check of $H$).
Relation: same structure as the threshold factorization of DIS (monograph Ch. 8, Ch. 14) with the final-state jet replaced by the
space-like jet; the phase is the new element.

### 6.2 Resummed kernel and its domain

With $\mu_i=|1-\xi||xP^z|$ (or $|1-y|P^z$ in matching, $|1-x|P^z$ in inverse matching), $\eta=2a_\Gamma(\mu_i,\mu)$:
$C=H(\alpha(\zeta_z))\exp[2S(\zeta_z,\mu)-2S(\mu_i,\mu)-a_H(\zeta_z,\mu)+a_J(\mu_i,\mu)]\,\tilde J(l_z\!=\!-2\partial_\eta,\alpha(\mu_i))
\big[\frac{\sin({\rm sgn}(1-\xi)\hat A+\eta\pi/2)}{|1-\xi|}(\frac{2|1-\xi||xP^z|}{\mu_i})^\eta\big]_\star\frac{\Gamma(1-\eta)e^{-\eta\gamma_E}}{\pi}$,
$\hat A=A(\alpha(\zeta_z))+\pi a_\Gamma(\zeta_z,\mu)$ [Ji:2023pba]. The perturbative jet function requires
$\Lambda\ll|1-\xi||xP^z|\ll|xP^z|$. **Numerical lesson** [Ji:2024hit] *(abstract)*: factorizing the kernel into a hard
part at the active-quark momentum $xP^z$ and a threshold **soft function** at the spectator momentum $(1-x)P^z$,
resummation of $\alpha_s^k\ln^{2k}(1-x)$ is "crucial"; perturbative matching **breaks down when $(1-x)P^z\to\Lambda$**,
and is valid when both $xP^z$ and $(1-x)P^z\gg\Lambda$; LRR can be combined with threshold resummation. Earlier Mellin-space
NLL resummation: [Gao:2021hxl] *(abstract)* (threshold logs from "the subtle interplay of collinear and soft divergences of an
underlying 3D momentum distribution"; marginal effect within the data sensitivity at that time).
**Consequence for the theorem's domain (precise form):** at fixed order the matching theorem is uniform only on
$x\in[x_0,1-x_0]$ with $x_0P^z\gg\Lambda$; with threshold resummation the large-$x$ boundary is set by $(1-x)P^z\gg\Lambda$.

### 6.3 Renormalons in the threshold limit (Liu, Su 2024) *(read)*

**Proposition 6.2.** In the large-$\beta_0$ approximation: (a) the threshold asymptotics is governed by UV physics: in
coordinate/moment space the hard scale is $|\lambda|/|z|$ (or $2N/|z|$); (b) the heavy–light Sudakov hard kernel has a **linear
IR renormalon in its imaginary part**, with the strength of the pole-mass renormalon; (c) it is cancelled by UV renormalons
of next-to-leading-power operators: in the threshold expansion of the quasi-PDF kernel and in the TMD expansion of the
quasi-LFWF amplitude; (d) the light–light Sudakov kernel has no linear renormalon (their App. B); (e) heuristic origin:
the analytic continuation of the linear static potential, $gA^z\sim iV$, giving $-i\frac{\delta m}{2p^z}$ corrections; the
quark field renormalization in **Coulomb gauge** also has a linear renormalon. **Grade [P]** (all orders in the bubble chain;
the non-perturbative identification is a conjecture). Phenomenology: explains the slow convergence of the imaginary part of
lattice Collins–Soper kernel extractions; Borel-resummed hard kernels reduce it (their App.).

### 6.4 GPDs and DAs at threshold

[Holligan:2025baj] *(abstract)*: threshold factorization for quasi-GPDs at $\xi\neq0$ — the large logarithms matter only in
the threshold limit; LaMET predictions are reliable on $[-1+x_0,-\xi-x_0]\cup[-\xi+x_0,\xi-x_0]\cup[\xi+x_0,1-x_0]$ with
$x_0$ set by hard parton momenta; the inverse matching "does not spread the nonperturbative effects or power corrections to
the perturbatively calculable regions" (model study). For $x\to\pm\xi$ see §9.1. **Grade [A]** (formalism), numerical claims
model-dependent.

## 7. The Fourier transform: long-distance behaviour, analyticity, and the "inverse problem"

The quasi-PDF requires $\tilde h(\lambda,-\lambda^2/P_z^2)$ for all $\lambda$, while lattice data exist for $|z|\le z_L$
(i.e. $|\lambda|\le\lambda_L=z_LP^z$), with deteriorating signal. This is hypothesis (H5) of §4.1 in practice.

### 7.1 Exponential decay of space-like correlators (Ji, Liu, Su 2026) *(read)*

**Theorem 7.1 (locality ⇒ exponential decay; scalar, $\vec p=0$, no one-particle intermediate states).** Assume
(a) spectrum: vacuum, massive one-particle states, and multi-particle states separated from the external state (mass $m$) by a
gap $\Delta m>0$; (b) $\phi$ a local field, tempered, with power-law bounds; (c) locality: $[\phi(x),\phi(0)]=0$ for $x^2<0$.
Then $f(0,\vec z)\equiv\langle\vec p=0|\phi(0,\vec z)\phi(0)|\vec p=0\rangle=2Z+O(e^{-\Delta m|\vec z|})$, and the rate is sharp.
*Proof architecture:* insert $P_\Omega+P_1+P_{n\ge2}$, separate the crossed vacuum channel to keep permutation symmetry;
continue in complex time $w=t-it_E$: $G_>$ analytic for ${\rm Im}\,w<0$, $G_<$ for ${\rm Im}\,w>0$, both bounded by
$Ce^{-\Delta m|{\rm Im}\,w|}$ (spectral condition + temperedness); by locality their boundary values agree on the space-like edge
$|{\rm Re}\,w|<|\vec z|$; the edge-of-the-wedge theorem glues them into one function analytic off the cuts
$(-\infty,-|\vec z|)\cup(|\vec z|,\infty)$; the bound is transported to $w=0$ by conformal mapping and Jensen's formula
(subharmonicity of $\log|G|$), exactly as in Fredenhagen's vacuum theorem [Fredenhagen:1984bz]. **Grade [R]** under the stated
axioms (the "very moderate regularity assumptions" near the cuts are stated, not proven, for interacting theories).
*Extensions in the paper:* one-particle intermediate states via spectral representations of form factors and their
"locality completion" (sharper bounds depending on masses and thresholds); arbitrary external momentum via a spectral
representation of the forward correlator, **proven to all orders in $\phi^3/\phi^4$ theory** from parametric representations
(**[R] in PT** for those theories), with the support property $x\in(-1,1)$ of the spectral distribution "a result of locality";
two-loop $\phi^3$ checks of the asymptotic forms.
*Application to QCD:* the Wilson line is the propagator of an auxiliary static quark, so $\tilde h$ becomes a correlator of the
local operators $\bar Q\Gamma\psi$; the mass gap is a heavy–light **binding energy** $\Lambda_X=m_X-m_Q$ ($\approx500$ MeV for light
quarks, from $B^{(*)}$ masses; renormalon-scheme dependent split $m_Q(\tau)+\Lambda(\tau)$, scheme-independent physical masses).
Leading/next-to-leading asymptotics, e.g. pion valence:
$\tilde h^{\rm LA}=[A_2e^{i{\rm sgn}(z)\phi_2}+A_1e^{i{\rm sgn}(z)\phi_1}e^{-iP^zz}+A_3e^{i{\rm sgn}(z)\phi_3}e^{iP^zz}]e^{-\Lambda|z|}$,
$\tilde h^{\rm NLA}=\tilde h^{\rm LA}+[\dots]'\,e^{-\Lambda|z|}/|z|$; nucleon unpolarized: $A_2e^{i\phi_2{\rm sgn}z}e^{-\Lambda|z|}(1+A_2'/|z|\dots)$.
**Grade for QCD [A]/[C]**: relies on confinement (existence of the gap in the extended theory with a static colour source),
on the HQET reduction, and on the extension of the scalar theorem to gauge theories with Wilson lines.

### 7.2 Analyticity strip and truncation bound (this work)

**Lemma 7.2 (analyticity strip).** If $|\tilde h(\lambda,-\lambda^2/P_z^2)|\le A(1+|\lambda|)^ke^{-m|\lambda|/P^z}$ for all real $\lambda$,
then $\tilde q(x,P^z)$ extends to a holomorphic function in the strip $|{\rm Im}\,x|<m/P^z$; in particular it is real-analytic on
$\mathbb R$ — **no endpoint singularities at $x=0,\pm1$ at finite $P^z$**; the PDF's $x^a(1-x)^b$ behaviour can emerge only as
the strip width $m/P^z\to0$. *Proof:* the integral $\int\frac{d\lambda}{2\pi}e^{-ix\lambda}\tilde h$ converges absolutely and
uniformly on $|{\rm Im}\,x|\le m/P^z-\epsilon$; Morera + Fubini. ∎ **Grade [R]** (cond. on Thm 7.1 for QCD). This makes precise
the statement of [Ji:2024oka] that the quasi-PDF is analytic at $y=0,1$ at finite $P^z$, and shows why (H5) and the endpoint
regions are linked: a Lorentzian with poles at $\pm im/P^z$ (`s5` L3) is the prototype.

**Proposition 7.3 (truncation bound).** Under the hypothesis of Lemma 7.2 with $k=0$ beyond $\lambda_L$,
$\big|\tilde q(x)-\int_{|\lambda|\le\lambda_L}\frac{d\lambda}{2\pi}e^{-ix\lambda}\tilde h\big|\le\frac{AP^z}{\pi m}e^{-mz_L}$, $z_L=\lambda_L/P^z$.
**[R]** (elementary; `s7` P1). *Numerics:* for $m=0.5$ GeV, $e^{-mz_L}=0.079$ at $z_L=1$ fm and $0.022$ at $1.5$ fm — the
suppression is **not** parametrically small at present $z_L$; the error budget therefore depends on how well $A$ and $m$ (or the
LA/NLA parameters) are constrained. This is the precise content of the disagreement in §7.3.

**Proposition 7.4 (degrees of freedom of the restricted ITD problem; classical).** For the pseudo-PDF (support $[-1,1]$) the data
$\tilde h(\lambda)$, $|\lambda|\le\lambda_L$, determine $\mathcal P$ uniquely (the ITD is entire, Cor. 1.5), but the inversion is
unstable: the time–frequency limiting operator $(Kf)(x)=\int_{-1}^1\frac{\sin\lambda_L(x-y)}{\pi(x-y)}f(y)dy$ has $\approx2\lambda_L/\pi$
eigenvalues near 1 and the others decay super-exponentially (Slepian; Landau–Widom: $N(\epsilon)=\frac{2\lambda_L}{\pi}+\frac{1}{\pi^2}\ln\frac{1-\epsilon}{\epsilon}\ln\lambda_L+o(\ln\lambda_L)$;
classical results of harmonic analysis, not in INSPIRE). `s7` P2: $\#\{\text{eig}>\tfrac12\}=3,6,10,16$ for $\lambda_L=5,10,15,25$
vs $2\lambda_L/\pi=3.2,6.4,9.5,15.9$. So with $\lambda_L\approx10$ the data carry about six independent "modes" of $\mathcal P$ in
$[-1,1]$; everything else must come from prior information (functional forms, positivity, asymptotics). **Grade [R].**

### 7.3 Practice: extrapolation vs inverse problem

LaMET practice: fit $\tilde h$ at $z\in[z_{\min},z_L]$ to physics-motivated asymptotic forms (exponential decay with
$\Lambda\in[0.01,0.99]$ GeV or tighter, algebraic factors from Regge behaviour; [Gao:2021dbh, Su:2022fiu, Ji:2026vir]), then
Fourier transform. **The debate** (2025–2026): [Dutrieux:2025jed] *(abstract)*: with realistic data the uncertainty from the
inverse problem "remains significant without very restrictive assumptions"; the crux lies at $\lambda=zP_z\sim5$–15 where the
signal is "barely existent"; LaMET does not provide a "direct" computation of the $x$-dependence any more than SDF does.
[Chen:2025cxr] *(abstract)*: LaMET "theoretically solves the ill-posed inverse problem" at fixed $x\in[x_{\min},x_{\max}]$;
some (not all) current data qualify for asymptotic extrapolation; recasting it as data-driven inverse problem "with ad hoc
mathematical conditioning could lead to unnecessarily conservative errors". [Ji:2026vir] provides the asymptotic forms and
"reliable error estimates" with realistic data. **Assessment.** Lemma 7.2 + Prop. 7.3 show that, *given* a theorem-level bound on
the tail (Thm 7.1 for QCD, which is [A]/[C]) with known constants, the momentum-space quasi-PDF at fixed $x$ is a well-posed
quantity with exponentially small truncation error; Prop. 7.4 shows that *without* such information (pure SDF data) the
$x$-dependence is determined only up to $\sim2\lambda_L/\pi$ modes. Both sides' mathematical statements are therefore compatible;
the disagreement is about the size of $e^{-mz_L}$-type effects and the reliability of the asymptotic parameters with present data
— an empirical, not a theoretical, question. Related: Bayesian/NN reconstructions [Karpie:2019eiq] *(abstract)*.

## 8. LaMET and short-distance factorization (SDF): equivalence, contrast, critiques

### 8.1 The pseudo-PDF / SDF program *(read: Radyushkin 2017, 2018; abstracts otherwise)*

Radyushkin's starting point [Radyushkin:2017cyf]: the correlator is a function $\mathcal M(\nu,-z^2)$ of the two invariants;
the quasi-PDF mixes the $\nu$ and $z^2$ dependences, while at fixed $z^2$ the $\nu$-transform (pseudo-PDF) has support
$[-1,1]$ and a TMD-like interpretation ("the $z^2$ dependence reflects the transverse-momentum dependence"). The reduced ITD
$\mathfrak M=\mathcal M(\nu,z^2)/\mathcal M(0,z^2)$ cancels the link UV divergence; its $z^2$ dependence at small $z$ is
perturbative evolution, matched to $\overline{\rm MS}$ by the one-loop relation of §4.6 [Radyushkin:2017lvu, Radyushkin:2018cvn,
Zhang:2018ggy]; first lattice exploration [Orginos:2017kos]; moments from non-local matrix elements after the continuum limit
[Karpie:2018zaz]; physical-point applications [Joo:2019jct, Joo:2020spy]; review [Radyushkin:2019mye]. In SDF the factorization
theorem used is Thm 4.1 (coordinate space) — **[A]** at leading twist, applied only for $|z|\lesssim0.2$–$0.3$ fm, with the
$O(z^2\Lambda^2)$ remainder estimated or fitted.

### 8.2 Equivalence and contrast (Ji 2022, published 2025) *(read)*

**Proposition 8.1.** At $P^z=\infty$ the two approaches are two representations of the same function (Thm 4.2, [Izubuchi:2018srq]);
at finite $P^z$ they are **different expansions** of the same data: LaMET expands in $\Lambda/(x(1-x)P^z)$ at fixed $x$ and
yields $f(x)$ for $x\in[x_{\min}\sim\Lambda/P^z,\,1-x_{\min}]$; SDF expands in $z^2\Lambda^2$ at fixed $\lambda$ and yields the
leading-twist ITD for $\lambda\in[0,\lambda_{\max}]$, $\lambda_{\max}=z_{\max}P^z$ ("$\sim3$ for $P^z=3$ GeV" with
$z_{\max}\simeq0.2$ fm); "the correlations in $[0,\lambda_{\max}]$ allow a resolution of $\Delta x\sim1/\lambda_{\max}$"; any sharp
construction of $f(x)$ from SDF data "involves additional assumptions"; LaMET "use[s] legitimately more lattice data than SD-OPE
would allow" because the Fourier transform filters higher twist without a manual twist separation, "even though the twist
expansion itself breaks down beyond $z\sim0.4$ fm"; **complementarity**: SDF data (moments, small-$\lambda$ ITD) plus endpoint
asymptotics constrain $x\in[0,x_{\min}]\cup[x_{\max},1]$ where LaMET is not predictive [Ji:2022ezo].
**Grade:** the equivalence at $P^z=\infty$ is **[R]** (given Thm 4.1); the claim that the LaMET Fourier transform filters higher
twist at fixed $x$ is precisely Lemma 5.2 + Thm 4.3(ii), hence **[C]** at the hadronic level; the resolution statement is made
quantitative by Prop. 7.4 (≈$2\lambda_{\max}/\pi$ modes).

### 8.3 "Good lattice cross sections" and current–current correlators

Beyond the Wilson-line bilinear, any Euclidean correlator with the right collinear divergences can be factorized (§4.4):
heavy-quark-mediated Compton tensor [Detmold:2005gg] *(abstract)* ("a fictitious heavy quark" avoids operator mixing for
higher moments), position-space current correlators for DAs at NNLO + twist 4 [Braun:2007wv] *(abstract)*, Compton
amplitude via the Feynman–Hellmann/OPE method [Chambers:2017dov] *(abstract)*, the Euclidean hadronic tensor
[Liu:1993cv] *(abstract)*, and LaMET with current–current correlators [Zhang:2026lle] *(read: abstract and setup)* — "simple
renormalization properties and [no] linear power divergences", at the price of four-point functions; "the universality of the
large momentum expansion allows computing PDFs starting from any Euclidean correlator with appropriate large momentum Fourier
components". **Grade:** factorization of current–current LCSs **[A]** (standard OPE of two local currents — here the OPE is
on firmer ground than for Wilson-line operators since only local operators of ordinary QCD are involved; Zimmermann-type
results apply in PT).

### 8.4 Critiques and their resolution

* *Power-divergent mixing* [Rossi:2017muf, Rossi:2018zkn] *(abstracts)*: on the lattice, moments of DIS operators mix with lower-
  dimensional operators with power-divergent coefficients; the authors argued that equal-time correlators, reduced ITDs, and
  current–current T-products inherit this obstruction. *Responses:* [Ji:2017rah] *(abstract)*: the only power divergence in the
  non-local operator is the Wilson-line self-energy, which factorizes (Thm 3.1); [Karpie:2018zaz] *(abstract)*: after the
  continuum limit at fixed $z\neq0$, the moments obtained from non-local matrix elements are finite and agree with local
  twist-2 moments (quenched test). **Status:** the order of limits ($a\to0$ at fixed $z$, then $z\to0$ within the OPE) avoids the
  problem; no published refutation of this resolution was found. **[A]**.
* *Euclidean vs Minkowski* [Briceno:2017cpo] *(abstract)*: equal-time matrix elements from Euclidean lattice QCD coincide with
  Minkowski LSZ matrix elements; uniqueness to all orders in PT. **[A]** (all orders in PT).
* *Inverse problem*: §7.3.

## 9. Off-forward and exclusive objects: GPDs, DAs, LFWFs, heavy-meson and baryon LCDAs, $\Delta G$, DPDs

### 9.1 Quasi-GPDs

**Definition.** $\tilde F(y,\xi,t,P^z)=P^z\int\frac{dz}{2\pi}e^{-iyP^zz}\langle P+\frac\Delta2|\bar\psi(-\frac z2)\gamma^tU(-\frac z2,\frac z2)\psi(\frac z2)|P-\frac\Delta2\rangle$
(convention of [Su:2026eta], *read*; symmetric frame; asymmetric frames with Lorentz-invariant amplitudes and a frame-independent
quasi-GPD definition "that may lead to smaller power corrections" [Bhattacharya:2022aob] *(abstract)*).
**Theorem 9.1 (quasi-GPD matching).** $\tilde F(y,\xi,t,P^z)=\int_{-1}^1\frac{dx}{|x|}\tilde C\big(\frac yx,\frac\xi x,\frac{\mu}{xP^z}\big)F(x,\xi,t,\mu)+$power corrections,
$\tilde C$ independent of $t$ (at least at one loop; [Liu:2019urm] *(abstract)*: "the matching coefficient is independent of the
momentum transfer squared"; at $\xi=0$ it equals the PDF kernel). One loop: unpolarized/helicity [Ji:2015qla] *(abstract: "no
matching is required for $E$ ($\tilde E$)" at one loop and leading power)*, transversity [Xiong:2015nua], RI/MOM [Liu:2019urm],
all quasi-GPDs incl. gluon contributions and arbitrary hadron spin by a different method [Ma:2022ggj] *(abstract)*; coordinate space
(pseudo) [Radyushkin:2019owq]; unified singlet/non-singlet forward/non-forward NLO kernels [Yao:2022vtp]; NNLO coordinate-space
kernel for non-singlet unpolarized and helicity GPDs [Ji:2025mvk] *(abstract)*. Proof architecture: the OPE route of §4.2 with
conformal/non-forward twist-2 operators (the support/analyticity Prop. 1.4 extends to non-forward kinematics). **Grade [A]**;
**[R]$_{1L}$**; **[R]$_{2L}$** (one group, coordinate space). Kinematic twist-3 (needed for translation invariance): tree-level
expressions "logarithmically enhanced as compared to the leading twist, and discontinuous at $x=\pm\xi$" [Braun:2023alc] *(abstract)*.

**The $x=\pm\xi$ question (open; graded [C]).** Generic EFT counting requires $2|x\pm\xi|P^z\gg\Lambda$, which would exclude windows
around $x=\pm\xi$. Two recent positions:
(i) [Braun:2024snf] *(abstract)*: renormalon analysis predicts that at $x=\pm\xi$ the non-perturbative corrections to quasi-GPDs are
suppressed by **only the first power** of the hard scale, from soft momenta, "nothing to do with the known UV renormalon";
(ii) [Su:2026eta] *(read)*: by analogy with DVCS at $x_B=\xi$ (where the soft region associated with the real photon is power
suppressed [Ji:1998xh, Collins:1998be, Radyushkin:1997ki], as cited there), the logarithms $\ln\frac{4P_z^2(x\pm\xi)^2}{\mu^2}$ in the
one-loop kernel come multiplied by $|x\pm\xi|$ and are suppressed at $x=\mp\xi$, "there is no leading-twist contribution from the
soft scales associated with $(x\pm\xi)P^z$"; reanalysis of four lattice data sets with one-loop matching (regulator hierarchy
$\epsilon_2\ll|\epsilon_1|\to0$) gives GPDs continuous at $x=\pm\xi$ with discontinuous derivatives; earlier apparent discontinuities
"may arise from regulator-related numerical artifacts"; required: $2(|x|+|\xi|)P^z\gg\Lambda$ and $2|1\pm x|P^z\gg\Lambda$.
*Assessment:* (i) and (ii) are not strictly contradictory — (ii) argues that no **leading-twist** soft contribution arises, (i) finds
a **power correction** enhanced from $1/P_z^2$ to $1/P^z$ at the point $x=\pm\xi$. A proof would require a power-counting analysis of
the soft-quark (zero-mode) region of the quasi-GPD at $x=\pm\xi$ to all orders, analogous to the DVCS analysis of
[Collins:1998be]; not available. Together with the kinematic twist-3 discontinuities [Braun:2023alc] the status is **[C]**.

### 9.2 Meson and baryon distribution amplitudes

Quasi-DA $\tilde\phi(x,P^z)$ from $\langle0|\bar\psi(z)\gamma^z\gamma_5W\psi(0)|\pi(P)\rangle$; matching
$\tilde\phi(x)=\int dy\,C(x,y,\mu/P^z)\phi(y)$ — one loop [Ji:2015qla] (with the GPD paper), RI/MOM [Liu:2018tox] (the quasi-PDF
renormalization factor can be used "provided that they are implemented on lattice and in perturbation theory in the same manner"),
pseudo-ITD [Radyushkin:2019owq], NNLO coordinate and momentum space in a "state-of-the-art scheme" (hybrid-type) [Ji:2025mvk];
lattice: [Zhang:2017bzy, LatticeParton:2022zqc, Holligan:2023rex]. Baryon LCDAs: one-loop factorization of quasi-DAs
"explicitly demonstrated at the one-loop level", $\overline{\rm MS}$ and RI/MOM kernels [Deng:2023csv] *(abstract)*. **Grades:**
meson DA matching **[A]**, **[R]$_{1L}$**, **[R]$_{2L}$** (single group); baryon **[R]$_{1L}$** (one group); all-order baryon
factorization **[P]** (three-quark operators: renormalization of the multi-link operator assumed).

### 9.3 Light-front wave functions

[Ji:2021znw] *(read)*: light-front quantization as an effective theory with small-$k^+$ modes integrated out; LFWF amplitudes from
the large-momentum expansion of equal-time correlators with staple links (quasi-TMDWFs), with the **same heavy–light Sudakov kernel**
$H^\pm_{\rm LFWF}$ (§6.1) and the reduced soft function $S_r$ (§10.4); the imaginary parts of the two Sudakov factors add (they cancel
for quasi-TMD PDFs). Pseudoscalar-meson example. **Grade [A]** (one-loop explicit; same structure as Thm 10.1).

### 9.4 Heavy-meson LCDAs

Two-step factorization: QCD LCDA of a heavy meson = perturbative jet-like function ⊗ HQET LCDA (removing the $m_Q$ scale)
[Beneke:2023nmj] *(abstract)*; LaMET access to the HQET LCDA via equal-time correlators with "a dynamic quark field for a fast
moving heavy quark", first lattice test giving $\lambda_B^{-1}$ consistent with $B\to\gamma\ell\nu$ constraints [Han:2024fkr]
*(abstract)*. Hierarchy required: $\Lambda\ll m_Q\ll P^z$ (or $P^z/m_Q$ fixed with boosted HQET). **Grade [A]** for each step
(one loop explicit), combined chain **[P]**.

### 9.5 Gluon helicity $\Delta G$

## 10. Transverse-momentum-dependent distributions from LaMET

### 10.1 Definitions *(read: JLL PLB 2020, NPB 2020; ESSZ 2022)*

Physical TMD (Collins/JLL notation): $f^{\rm TMD}(x,b_\perp,\mu,\zeta)=\lim_{\delta\to0}f(x,b_\perp,\mu,\delta)/\sqrt{S(b_\perp,\mu,\delta e^{2y_n},\delta)}$,
with the un-subtracted correlator built with a staple of light-like links $\mathcal W_{\pm n}$ (− for DY, + for SIDIS) and the soft
function $S$ (two light-like directions, $\mathcal T/\bar{\mathcal T}$ ordered); $\zeta=2(xP^+)^2e^{2y_n}$;
$\mu\frac{d\ln f^{\rm TMD}}{d\mu}=\Gamma_{\rm cusp}\ln\frac{\mu^2}{\zeta}-2\gamma_H$, $2\zeta\frac{d\ln f^{\rm TMD}}{d\zeta}=K(b_\perp,\mu)$,
$\frac{dK}{d\ln\mu}=-2\Gamma_{\rm cusp}$ (Collins' $K$ = ESSZ's $\gamma_\zeta$; [Vladimirov:2020umg] *(abstract)* gives a process-independent
non-perturbative definition).

**Quasi-TMD** [Ji:2014hxa, Ji:2018hvs, Ebert:2018gzl, Ebert:2019okf, Ji:2019ewn]:
$$
\tilde f(\lambda,b_\perp,\mu,P^z)=\lim_{L\to\infty}\frac{\langle P|\bar\psi(\frac{\lambda n_z}{2}+\vec b_\perp)\Gamma\,\mathcal W_z(\frac{\lambda n_z}{2}+\vec b_\perp;L)\psi(-\frac{\lambda n_z}{2})|P\rangle}{\sqrt{Z_E(2L,b_\perp,\mu)}},
\qquad Z_E=\tfrac{1}{N_c}{\rm Tr}\langle0|W_\perp\mathcal W_z(\vec b_\perp;2L)|0\rangle,
$$
$\Gamma=\gamma^z$ or $\gamma^0$ (any combination except $\gamma^0-\gamma^z$), $\mathcal W_z$ a staple of space-like links of length $L$ closed by
$W_\perp$; $\sqrt{Z_E}$ (flat rectangular Euclidean Wilson loop $2L\times b_\perp$) removes the linear divergence, the cusp divergences
and the **pinch-pole singularity** of infinitely long links [Ji:2018hvs] *(abstract: "resolved by the finite length of the gauge link")*.
Fourier transform in $\lambda$ gives $\tilde f(x,b_\perp,\mu,\zeta_z)$, $\zeta_z=(2xP^z)^2$. The quasi-TMD has **no rapidity divergence**:
$P^z$ acts as an off-light-cone rapidity regulator. Momentum evolution: $2\zeta_z\frac{d\ln\tilde f}{d\zeta_z}=K(b_\perp,\mu)+G(\zeta_z,\mu)$,
$K^{(1)}=-\frac{\alpha_sC_F}{\pi}L_b$, $G^{(1)}=\frac{\alpha_sC_F}{\pi}(1-\ln\frac{\zeta_z}{\mu^2})$ [Ji:2014hxa, Ji:2019ewn]; $\mu\frac{d\ln\tilde f}{d\mu}=2\gamma_F$
(heavy–light current AD; cusp ADs cancelled by $\sqrt{Z_E}$). ESSZ use a **proper** quasi-TMD
$\tilde f=\tilde f^{\rm naive}\sqrt{\tilde S^R_{\rm naive}/S^R_C}$ and a Lorentz-invariant correlator $\Omega(b,P,\epsilon,\eta v,\delta)$
covering quasi, Collins, LR (large-rapidity), JMY and MHENS schemes (their Tables).
**Off-light-cone soft function** and its **reduced** part: $S(b_\perp,\mu,Y,Y')=e^{(Y+Y')K(b_\perp,\mu)}S_r^{-1}(b_\perp,\mu)+\dots$,
$\mu^2\frac{d\ln S_r}{d\mu^2}=\Gamma_S$ (constant part of the cusp AD at large **hyperbolic** angle); $S^{(1)}=\frac{\alpha_sC_F}{2\pi}[2-2(Y+Y')]L_b$.

### 10.2 Theorem 10.1 (quasi-TMD factorization)

**Statement (corrected form).** For $b_\perp\gg1/(xP^z)$, $\zeta_z\gg\Lambda^2$, at leading power:
$$
\tilde f(x,b_\perp,\mu,\zeta_z)\sqrt{S_r(b_\perp,\mu)}=H\Big(\frac{\zeta_z}{\mu^2}\Big)\,
\exp\Big[\tfrac12K(b_\perp,\mu)\ln\frac{\zeta_z}{\zeta}\Big]\,f^{\rm TMD}(x,b_\perp,\mu,\zeta)
\Big\{1+O\Big(\frac{\Lambda^2}{\zeta_z},\frac{M^2}{\zeta_z},\frac{1}{b_\perp^2\zeta_z}\Big)\Big\},
$$
**multiplicative in $x$** (no convolution), flavour diagonal (no quark–gluon or flavour mixing), spin independent ($H$ depends
only on the colour representation), valid for quarks and gluons (with $C_g$). Equivalent ESSZ form:
$\tilde f^{[s]}_{i/h}(x,\vec b_T,\mu,\tilde\zeta,x\tilde P^z)=C_i(x\tilde P^z,\mu)\exp[\frac12\gamma^i_\zeta\ln\frac{\tilde\zeta}{\zeta}]f^{[s]}_{i/h}(x,\vec b_T,\mu,\zeta)\{1+O[\frac{1}{(x\tilde P^zb_T)^2},\frac{\Lambda^2}{(x\tilde P^z)^2}]\}$
[Ebert:2022fmh] *(read)*. One loop: $H^{(1)}=C_q^{(1)}=\frac{\alpha_sC_F}{2\pi}(-2+\frac{\pi^2}{12}-\frac{L_z^2}{2}+L_z)$, $\gamma_C^q=\frac{d\ln C_q}{d\ln(2x\tilde P^z)}=\frac{\alpha_sC_F}{\pi}(1-L_z)$,
$\frac{d\gamma_C}{d\ln\mu}=2\Gamma_{\rm cusp}$; NNLO: [delRio:2023pse] *(read)*, agreeing with the two-loop heavy–light Sudakov kernel of
[Ji:2023pba]; NLL/NNLL resummed $C_q$ in ESSZ and lattice applications.

**Correction (this work, `s4`).** [Ji:2019ewn] eq. (factorization) and [Ji:2020ect] print $e^{K\ln(\zeta_z/\zeta)}$ although both define
$2\zeta\,d\ln f/d\zeta=K$; with the one-loop expressions quoted in the RMP itself, the $L_b\ln(\zeta_z/\zeta)$ terms cancel only for
$e^{\frac12K\ln(\zeta_z/\zeta)}$, which is also the ESZ/ESSZ form and is consistent with $2\zeta_z\,d\ln\tilde f/d\zeta_z=K+G$. Verified symbolically:
quasi-TMD $\tilde f^{(1)}=a[-\frac12L_b^2+L_b(\frac52-L_z)-\frac32-\frac12L_z^2+L_z]$, $f^{\rm TMD(1)}=a[-\frac12L_b^2+(\frac32-\ln\frac{\zeta}{\mu^2})L_b+\frac12-\frac{\pi^2}{12}]$,
$S_r^{(1)}=-2aL_b$, $K^{(1)}=-2aL_b$ ⇒ coefficient $\frac12$ and $H^{(1)}$ as above. **Grade of the printed form [V]** (typo-level).

**Hypotheses.** (T1) multiplicative renormalizability of staple operators with $\sqrt{Z_E}$ (auxiliary-field argument with cusps;
lattice: [Zhang:2022xuw] *(abstract)*: $\sqrt{Z_E}$ + short-distance hadron matrix element removes all UV divergences while RI/MOM
fails to remove all linear divergences; one-loop staple renormalization incl. mixing [Constantinou:2019vyb, Alexandrou:2023ucc,
Spanoudes:2024kpb], auxiliary-field improvement [Green:2020xco]) — **[A]**; (T2) leading-region/power-counting analysis with staple
links — **[A]**; (T3) commutation of $\epsilon\to0$ with $L(\tilde\eta)\to\infty$ — **[A]** (argued, checked at one loop);
(T4) "exchanging $\epsilon\to0$ and $\zeta_{\rm LR}\to\infty$ only affects the UV" (asymptotic freedom; LaMET principle) — **[A]** in
PT, the non-perturbative statement is Conj. 2.1; (T5) the non-perturbative soft function $S_r$ is obtainable (Prop. 10.2).

**Proof architectures.**
*(A) Leading regions (JLL 2020, sketch; "details to a future publication")*: collinear subgraphs identical to the light-front TMD
(obtained by $P^z\to\infty$); soft radiation between the fast colour charge and the staple captured by the off-light-cone soft
function; hard subgraphs at $\zeta_z$, confined to the vicinities of the two quark–link vertices because any hard momentum flowing
between $0$ and $b_\perp$ costs a power of $1/P^z$ ⇒ no convolution — "in sharp contrast to the $k_\perp$ integrated quasi-PDFs".
*(B) ESSZ 2022 (all orders, unpolarized quark in detail; gluons and spin by "minor, straightforward modifications")*:
(1) write quasi, LR and Collins beam functions as Fourier transforms of one Lorentz-invariant correlator $\Omega$; leading power keeps
only the $P^\mu\Omega$ structure; (2) boost the quasi parameters by rapidity $y_B$: $\tilde b^+=-b^-e^{2y_B}\to0$, $\tilde b^z\to0$,
$y_{\tilde P}=y_P-y_B\to\infty$, so the quasi-correlator equals the LR correlator with finite-length links up to terms suppressed at large
proton rapidity; (3) $\epsilon\to0$ and $\tilde\eta\to\infty$ commute: self-energies cancel in $\Omega/\sqrt S$ (the staple of $\Omega$ is
half that of $S$; exchanges between the halves are exponentially suppressed), and the eikonal poles $1/(n_B\cdot k)$ give the same
result with either regulator (verified at one loop); (4) LR vs Collins differ only in the order of $\epsilon\to0$ and $y_B\to-\infty$;
by asymptotic freedom this is a UV effect, so $f^{\rm LR}=C_q(\sqrt{\zeta_{\rm LR}}/2,\mu)f+O(y_B^ke^{y_B})$, and $C_q$ cannot depend on
$y_n-y_B$ because that would bring in the non-perturbative CS kernel ⇒ $C_q=C_q(\zeta_{\rm LR}/\mu^2,\alpha_s)$; (5) **no mixing**: the
quark fields stay space-like separated, so the $y_B\to-\infty$ limit cannot generate a UV counterterm contracting flavour indices (for the
collinear quasi-PDF the fields approach the light cone and mixing does occur); cross-check through the JMY scheme (analytic continuation
space-like ↔ time-like) and the absence of mixing in TMD factorization of DY/SIDIS; one-loop checks in their appendices.
*(C) SCET derivation* [Vladimirov:2020ofp] *(abstract)*: factorized expression in terms of the physical TMD and a "nonperturbative lattice
related factor" which cancels in suitably constructed ratios; one-loop coefficient and anomalous dimensions. *(D) Coulomb-gauge quasi-TMDs*
[Zhao:2023ptv] *(abstract)*: Coulomb-gauge-dressed fields reduce to standard TMD correlators under a principal-value prescription in the
infinite boost limit; factorization "derived using SCET and verified at one-loop order"; no Wilson lines, simpler renormalization for
T-even TMDs (T-odd ones, e.g. Sivers, need the staple).
**Grade:** perturbative all-order statement **[A]** (three independent arguments; the decisive step (T4) is an order-of-limits argument,
not a diagram-by-diagram subtraction proof); $H$ **[R]$_{1L}$** (≥4 groups), **[R]$_{2L}$** (dRV, JLS); hadronic statement **[C]**.
Glauber/soft-exchange issues specific to the staple at finite $L$: no dedicated published analysis found (§13).

### 10.4 The soft function

**Proposition 10.4.**
(a) *[JLL 2020, NPB 955 (read)]* The off-light-cone soft function equals a **form factor of a pair of colour sources** (HQET fields) moving
with nearly light-like velocities $v,v'$: $S(b_\perp,\mu,Y,Y')={}_{v'}\langle Q\bar Q|J(v,v',\vec b_\perp)|Q\bar Q\rangle_v$,
$J=\bar Q^\dagger_{v'}(\vec b_\perp)\bar Q_v(\vec b_\perp)Q^\dagger_{v'}(0)Q_v(0)$ — a time-independent formulation. **[A]** (HQET propagator =
Wilson line).
(b) *[same]* The **intrinsic soft function** $S_I=S_r$ (in the off-light-cone scheme $S_I=e^{-\mathcal D}$) follows from a large-momentum
pseudoscalar-meson form factor $F(b_\perp,P\cdot P')=\langle P'|\bar\eta\Gamma'\eta(\vec b_\perp)\bar\psi\Gamma\psi(0)|P\rangle$ and the quasi-TMD
wave functions: $\frac{F}{\int H\,\tilde\phi\,\tilde\phi^\dagger}=S_I$. The factorizations of $F$ and $\tilde\phi$ used are stated with details
"in a future publication"; the relation $2\mathcal D_1-\mathcal D_2=\mathcal D$ and the master formula are verified at one loop. **[P]**.
First lattice computation [LatticeParton:2020uhz] *(abstract)*; improved with Fierz rearrangement to suppress higher twist and applied to
pion TMDWFs and proton TMDs [LatticePartonLPC:2023pdv] *(abstract)*; one-loop TMDWFs and soft functions [Deng:2022gzi].
(c) *[bent soft functions]* Euclidean "bent" quasi-soft functions [Ji:2014hxa, Ebert:2019okf] reproduce the IR logarithms at one loop
(ESZ's "infrared consistency test", *read*: the naive quasi hadron matrix element, quasi soft function and quasi-TMD "all fail the
infrared consistency test" at one loop; the bent version passes at one loop), but their anomalous dimension is the cusp anomalous
dimension at a **Euclidean (circular) angle** $\pi/2$, which differs from the large-hyperbolic-angle $\Gamma_S$ beyond one loop
[Korchemsky:1987wg] ⇒ wrong $\ln\mu^2b_\perp^2$ structure from two loops [Ji:2019sxk] *(read)*; ESSZ concur ("will break down at two
loops"). **[A]** (based on the known two-loop cusp anomalous dimension).
(d) *[Liu 2024, JHEP 09 030 (read)]* **Theorem (time-ordering of TMD soft factors).** Using the causality-constrained coordinate-space
analyticity of gluonic Wightman functions in perturbation theory in linear covariant gauges, the Drell–Yan-shape TMD soft factor with the
**exponential regulator** [Li:2016axz] admits below-threshold (cut-free, Euclidean-type) parametric representations to all orders, and equals
the single-time-ordered soft factor $S_t$ (a space-like form factor); the relation extends to a family interpolating the off-light-cone,
finite light-front-length and exponential regulators. Caveats stated by the author: (i) a mathematically rigorous proof that the
analyticity holds for dimensionally regularized correlators at generic $\epsilon$ is still needed ("should be one of the most important
properties of the dimensional regularization"); (ii) $S_t\ne S_{\rm SIDIS}$ in general; equality DY = SIDIS for infinitely long light-like
links is argued separately by $\pm i\eta$ tracking in time-ordered PT (appendix), not by analyticity; (iii) the $e^+e^-$ soft factor is
"much less local". **Grade [A]** (all orders in PT, conditional on (i)); the DY=SIDIS statement **[P]**.

model results and do not transfer automatically to 4D QCD (marginal coupling, confinement), but they make concrete which hypotheses are
plausible: convergence/asymptoticity of the *coordinate* expansion and Borel-level manipulations are on firm ground; term-by-term
statements about momentum-space power corrections are not.

## 13. Open problems (precise formulations)

1. **OPE theorem for Wilson-line operators (node N4).** Prove, at the level of Zimmermann's forest formula, the short-distance
   expansion of $\bar J(z)J(0)$ in QCD + space-like auxiliary field, with the anisotropic power counting of the propagator
   $\theta(x^z)\delta(x^0)\delta^2(x_\perp)$; equivalently a BPHZ-level version of the IMQY "3-D/4-D" counting including the OPE.
2. **Renormalizability of QCD + space-like static source (node N1)** written out (BRST, $\delta m$ counterterm, $\det(in\cdot D)$).
3. **Uniform twist expansion in Ioffe time (H3).** Even in a solvable model, prove a remainder bound uniform for **real** $\lambda$
   (Liu's GN convergence theorem covers $\lambda\in i\mathbb R_{\ge0}$); then attempt QCD at large $\beta_0$.
4. **Large-distance control (H5) in QCD.** Prove exponential decay of hadronic Wilson-line correlators in a gauge theory (needs a mass
   gap with a static colour source, i.e. confinement input); obtain usable constants $(A,m)$ for Prop. 7.3.
5. **Endpoint behaviour of twist-4 distributions** ($\delta$ in Cor. 5.3): decide between $\Lambda^2/((1-x)P_z^2)$ and
   $\Lambda^2/((1-x)^2P_z^2)$ (scheme dependent: normalization introduces $\delta=0$ terms, §5.3).
6. **GPDs at $x=\pm\xi$.** All-order power counting of the soft-quark (zero-mode) region of quasi-GPDs; reconcile the linear power
   correction of [Braun:2024snf] with the leading-twist computability claim of [Su:2026eta] and the kinematic twist-3 discontinuities of
   [Braun:2023alc].
7. **Quasi-TMD factorization beyond arguments:** a Collins-style diagrammatic proof incl. (T3) beyond one loop, the order-of-limits step
   (T4), a Glauber/soft-exchange analysis for finite-length staples (none published, title searches Oct 2026), and the factorization of
   the large-momentum meson form factor used for $S_I$ ("future publication" in [Ji:2019sxk]).
8. **Coordinate-space analyticity of dimensionally regularized Wightman functions** (needed for [Liu:2023pjb]); DY = SIDIS soft factor by
   analyticity.
9. **Higher orders:** N$^3$LO non-singlet quasi-PDF kernel; NNLO singlet/gluon kernels; NNLO momentum-space quasi-GPD kernels; three-loop
   heavy–light Sudakov kernel.
10. **Twist-3 factorization to all orders** including zero modes $\propto\delta(x)$.
11. **Renormalon prescriptions:** universality of the PV-defined twist-3 parameter $m_0(\tau)$ across matrix elements (it depends on external
    states [Zhang:2023bxs]); relation to the heavy-quark residual mass; renormalon structure of hybrid kernels beyond large $\beta_0$.
12. **Audit of higher-order hybrid kernels** for the δ-term issue found in §3.7 (the one-loop printed form fails $\int C=1$ by
    $3a\ln(\lambda_Se^{\gamma_E})$; NNLO hybrid kernels used in lattice analyses should be checked against vector-current conservation
    $\int d\xi\,C_{\rm hyb}=1$).
13. **Asymptotic nature of the momentum-space LaMET expansion** in QCD (Liu's O(N) result suggests factorial divergence of momentum-space
    power expansions; the Weinberg-type expansion of [Ji:2024oka] should be formulated as an asymptotic expansion with
    renormalon-consistent subtractions).
14. **Colour-non-singlet DPDs** and heavy-meson LCDA chains: all-order soft/rapidity structure.
