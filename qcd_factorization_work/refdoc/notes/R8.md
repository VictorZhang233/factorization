## PART I. Faithful account of the segment

### p. 197 — end of Prop. E.31(iv); proof of Prop. E.31; Rem. E.32 (pp. 197–198)

**Prop. E.31(iv), end.** If the core graph $\Gamma_0$ already has an SA or AS localized insertion on a bystander parton, a
single SS line attached to that parton already makes the pattern coincident (order of $t_i^a,t_i^b$ undetermined; the side
sum leaves terms $\propto[t_i^a,t_i^b]$). So in the non-abelian theory (E.33) holds only if all patterns of the family are
non-coincident (independently of Ass. E.33, still under E.18, E.34) — e.g. at an order with a single SS line and no localized
insertion of the core graph on the two partons it joins. Otherwise (E.33) fails in general (inside $\mathcal G_0$ the residual
is not expected to vanish, Rem. E.32); the residual of coincident patterns is only *assumed* power suppressed in the larger
sum of Ass. E.33(c2) (complete graph set $\mathfrak G$ plus adjacent near-on-shell soft regions and real emission).

**Proof of Prop. E.31.** (i) single line: (E.30), (E.31); non-coincident patterns: all localized insertions commute, proceed
line by line. (ii) Lemma E.28(i). (iii) Fix a non-coincident pattern $p$, lines $l=1..N$, $X_l=t^a_{i_l}\otimes t^a_{j_l}$,
$G_l$ the (real) localized transverse kernel. *Caveat stated in the proof*: the algebra needs $G_l$ to depend only on
$k_{Tl}$, but $\Theta_l$ depends through $\nu_A,\nu_B$ on jet momenta, graph and side assignment; at points where all
$\Theta_l=1$ for all side assignments the dependence disappears; the difference ("window-edge term") is **not estimated**.
With window factors 1, after $\mathfrak P$ integrates out the SS momenta, the CTP matrix element (E.25) for side assignment
$s$ is
$$\Big\langle\Psi'\Big|\prod_{l:s_l=\text{conj}}(+iG_lX_l)\prod_{l:s_l=\text{amp}}(-iG_lX_l)\Big|\Psi\Big\rangle,\qquad
|\Psi\rangle=\big(V_H\,T\{\cdots\}\big)_A\otimes\big(V_H\,T\{\cdots\}\big)_B|P_A\rangle\otimes|P_B\rangle .$$
Used: the cuts of $J_A$ and $J_B$ move independently (window Glauber lines are never cut), both final states unconstrained
(Def. E.6) ⇒ identity on $\mathcal H_A\otimes\mathcal H_B$ between the two sides; $\tau>0$ evolution removed by Lemma E.26(b);
symmetry factors of identical lines cancel the label sums. Since $p$ is non-coincident all $X_l,G_l$ commute (also with the
core's $\tau=0$ insertions):
$$\sum_s\prod_{l:s_l=\text{conj}}(+iG_lX_l)\prod_{l:s_l=\text{amp}}(-iG_lX_l)=\prod_{l=1}^N\big(-iG_lX_l+iG_lX_l\big)=0\qquad(N\ge1).$$
Abelian: every pattern commutes, only $N=0$ survives = (E.33); equivalently $\langle\Psi'|e^{iV_G}e^{-iV_G}|\Psi\rangle$ (not
needed). (iv) $O(\chi^2)$: Ex. E.38, Lemma E.48(d).

**Rem. E.32 (origin of the non-abelian cancellation).** Prop. E.31 generalizes Lemma 9.2/Prop. 9.3 (eikonal bystander
approximation replaced by Lemma E.28; arbitrary $J_A,J_B$, any number of bystanders); (iii) uses only commutativity and
completeness. For non-abelian multiple exchanges on one parton the cancellation needs *compatible equal-time orderings in
both jets*. The exact theory has one time and unitary final-state evolution, so after summing all final states all FSI
(incl. crossed multiple exchanges) cancel; but $J_A$ uses $x^+$ and $J_B$ uses $x^-$ as time, "which exchange is latest" can
differ (crossed configurations), so Lemma E.26(c)'s maximal-time pairing cannot be done in both jets simultaneously. With exact
connecting propagators the two orders are correlated; the correlation comes from near-mass-shell points
($|k^+k^-|\sim k_T^2$) and is lost by the strict approximation inside the window (Lemma E.48(d)). The residual
$\propto C_AX$ has the colour structure of Glauber gluons emitting a soft gluon through the three-gluon vertex (Ex. E.38(4)),
so QCD unitarity can guarantee cancellation only in the complete set including those graphs and real emission: inside
$\mathcal G_0$ the $O(\chi^2)$ residual should not be expected to vanish — hence Ass. E.33(c) is formulated for "all graphs of
the given order with the given $J_A,J_B$". The §9 statement attributing the non-abelian cancellation to Lemma E.26 + Prop. E.31
is to be read in this sense (theorem for non-coincident patterns; conditional on E.33(c) otherwise) — an explicit
self-correction of the main text.

### E.4.5 One-sidedness after the cut sum (pp. 198–202)

Intro: for general $S$, Glauber momentum can flow through internal vertices of $S$ ("Glauber–soft mixing", Lipatov-vertex-type);
the external factor $R$ then contains soft internal propagators whose $k^-$ dependence is not controlled by Lemma E.19(c);
$S$ has its own real emission and cut sums. The needed properties are isolated as an assumption.

**Assumption E.33 (Glauber–soft mixing).** $\Gamma$ a DY forward graph, $R$ a leading region, $\Theta_j,\nu_A,\nu_B$ as in
Def. E.5, $\hat\Phi_R$ as in Ass. E.34.
* (a) *(smoothness of external factors)* For every localizable endpoint $j$ in $U_R$ (also if attached to an internal vertex of
  $S$), at $\Theta_j>0$ with the other variables fixed, the factor outside $J_A$, $R_j(k_j^-)$ (after integrating $S$'s internal
  loop momenta), splits as $R_j=R_{j\downarrow}+R_{j\uparrow}$, $R_{j\downarrow}$ ($R_{j\uparrow}$) analytic in the upper (lower)
  half plane, both analytic in $|k_j^-|<\Lambda_j$ and obeying there and on the real axis the bounds used in the proof of
  Lemma E.28(iii), and $\nu_A/\Lambda_j\le\lambda^{a'}$ ($a'>0$) — except on a set $E\subset U_R$ with
  $\int_E\hat\Phi_Rd\mu\le C\lambda^aL^K$. Same for $J_B$, $k_j^+$.
* (b) *(cut compatibility of $S$)* after the localization of (a) the cut sum of $J_A\otimes J_B\otimes S$ takes the form (E.25)
  with $S$'s fields as $\langle0|\bar T\{\cdots\}T\{\cdots\}|0\rangle$, and the $\tau=0$ insertions with both ends on bystanders
  (also through $S$) assemble into operators on $\mathcal H_A\otimes\mathcal H_B\otimes\mathcal H_S$.
* (c) *(compatibility of equal-time orderings, for the complete set)* $\mathfrak G$ = all forward graphs of the given order
  with the given $J_A,J_B$ (and their connections to $H$); closed under adding soft 3- and 4-gluon vertices between Glauber
  and soft lines, contains real soft emission through Lipatov-type vertices. **(c1)** for non-commuting localized insertions
  on the same bystander parton on the same side (SS, SA, AS, through $S$) *there is a fixed localization order for which the
  per-graph weak equivalence (E.35) holds*; **(c2)** summed over all graphs of $\mathfrak G$, all coincident patterns and all
  side assignments, combining the window with the adjacent near-on-shell soft region of $U_R$ (incl. real emission), the
  contribution after $\mathfrak P$ integrates the window SS momenta is $O(\lambda^aL^K)$ weakly; equivalently
  $\mathcal W'^\dagger\mathcal W=1+O(\lambda^a)$ on bystander states at $\tau=0$ ($\mathcal W$ = sum of all such insertions on the
  amplitude side).

*Status of E.33 (p. 198–199).* For $\mathcal G_0$: (a) holds by Lemma E.19(b)(c) + Def. E.5 (only the connecting line's own
pole; $\nu_A/\Lambda_j\le\lambda^{a_G}$ in the window; $E=\varnothing$) — except regions with H-emission endpoints (E.44(d));
(b) empty; (c) holds in the abelian theory and at orders with only non-coincident patterns (Prop. E.31(iii)); for non-abelian
coincident patterns it is not a property of the window-localized expression (Lemma E.48(d)) and not expected inside
$\mathcal G_0$ (Rem. E.32) ⇒ even for $\mathcal G_0$ an assumption about $\mathfrak G$. General graphs: (a) holds if $S$'s internal
lines are far off shell and the other soft momenta through them have minus components $\gg\nu_A$; may fail when a "mixing"
line carries the difference of two comparable soft momenta near shell (same configurations as E.44). (b) = joint form of soft
and jet unitarity used in Prop. 8.4. Literature: (b)(c) only in outline (CSS [4]; Collins [1] ch. 14); RS Glauber EFT [69]
organizes operators but proves nothing for arbitrary graphs (Rem. E.56). Ex. E.38 reduces (c) at $O(\alpha_s^2)$ to an explicit
condition (${\rm Re}\,p_{\rm opp}=0$ plus the sum of $C_AX$-type real–virtual soft emission) and checks (a) in one
Lipatov-vertex graph; not a complete verification at that order.

**Rewriting construction (p. 199).** Fix $R$. Carrier $\phi(x)=2\sqrt\pi\,e^{-x^2}$, $\int\frac{dx}{2\pi}\phi=1$, entire,
$|\phi(x+iy)|\le2\sqrt\pi\,e^{y^2-x^2}$ (verified K7). The map $\mathfrak r_R$ acts on the cut-summed integrand (also after
App. D's approximators, Rem. E.36(3)):
* **(R1)** $J_A,J_B$ in the CTP form of Lemma E.26 (exact in the sense of Ass. E.18(i): the jets' minus components and
  $\mathcal E$ integrated over the whole real axis).
* **(R2)** For each localizable unit $u$ a *unit localization operator* $\mathfrak l_u$: for a $J_A$ endpoint $j$ replace the
  jet factor $\mathcal J(k_j^-)$ by $\bar{\mathcal J}_j\,\phi(k_j^-/w_j)/w_j$ and set $k_j^-=0$ in the external factor;
  bystanders: $\bar{\mathcal J}_j=\mathcal J(0)$; active lines emitted by $V_H$: $\bar{\mathcal J}_j=c_j\mathcal J_+$,
  $c_j\equiv R_{j\downarrow}(0)/R_j(0)$ (full-axis Lemma E.28(iii); for $\mathcal G_0$: $c_j=\theta(k_j^+)$). $J_B$/$k_j^+$ likewise; SS
  lines: both ends replaced. Endpoints on active lines *absorbed* by $V_H$ unchanged. Then
  $$\mathfrak r_RG\equiv\prod_u\big[\Theta_u\mathfrak l_u+(1-\Theta_u)\big]G=\sum_S\prod_{u\in S}\Theta_u\prod_{u\notin S}(1-\Theta_u)\,\mathfrak l_SG,\qquad\text{(E.34)}$$
  $\mathfrak l_S$ = the $\mathfrak l_u$ ($u\in S$) in a fixed order; windows are independent of soft $\pm$ components, so they commute
  with $\mathfrak l_u$.

$\mathfrak r_R$ is linear; commuting case order-independent; non-commuting case: arbitrary fixed order, legitimacy = Ass.
E.33(c1). $\tilde F_{\Gamma,R}\equiv\mathfrak r_RF_\Gamma$, $\tilde\Gamma_{R,C}$ its decomposition by cuts (localization changes jet
factors, not the set of cuts). SS lines stay in $\tilde F_{\Gamma,R}$ (in no denominator); their cancellation is a family
statement (Prop. E.31(iii), Thm. E.8(iv)). Width $w_j=\lambda^{a_G}|k_{Tj}|$ is a choice (any width preserves the fibre
integral); $w_j\ll|k_{Tj}|$ keeps the carrier inside fibres of $\mathfrak B$-sets; $w_j\gg\nu_A$ not necessary; price: the
localized component may be deformed only by $\le\frac12w_j$.

**H-emission endpoints (pp. 199–200).** Localizable endpoints on active lines *emitted* by $V_H$. Such lines exist only at
$\tau>0$: $J=J^{\rm fi}$, $\mathcal J_-=0$; the localized value $c_j\mathcal J_+R_j(0)=\mathcal J_+R_{j\downarrow}(0)$ depends on the
half plane of the external pole; for $\mathcal G_0$, $c_j=\theta(k_j^+)$ — non-analytic in the component seen by the other jet
(reflects the mass-shell pole at $|k_j^-|\sim k_{Tj}^2/|k_j^+|$, outside the window). Consequences: (1) if the other end is also
localizable, localizing it meets an external factor containing $\theta(k_j^+)$ (violates Lemma E.28's analyticity); localizing
the other end first makes $\int J^{\rm fi}dk_j^-$ only conditionally convergent, its value (the actual $c_j$) fixed by the exact
integrand outside the window — not computed. (2) If the other end sits on an active line absorbed by $V_H$ (to be deformed
along the Glauber direction), $\theta(k_j^+)$ blocks analytic continuation in $k_j^+$. Alternative: do not localize; then $k_j^-$
appears only in final-state denominators of $J_A$ (not pinched in $J_A$), deform $k_j^-\mapsto k_j^--i\sigma_j\kappa|k_{Tj}|$; with
the other end's deformation the connecting line's real part changes by $\pm2\kappa^2\psi^2k_T^2$ (no crossing by Lemma E.41(b)'s
computation — verified algebraically in these notes); but then $k_j^-$ is "final-state one-sided", the eikonal factor is
*future-pointing*, and its combination with the Ward identity (Prop. E.46) is not analysed. Hence such regions are outside the
proven scope → Ass. E.44(d). They occur only if $H$ emits collinear lines into $J_A$ ($J_B$) with uncut soft lines attached.

**Assumption E.34 (power-counting envelope for fibre integrals).** For each leading region $R$ there is a non-negative
$\hat\Phi_R$ on $U_R$, independent of the localized soft components, $\int_{U_R}\hat\Phi_Rd\hat\mu\le CL^K$ (uniform in
$\epsilon\in(0,\epsilon_0]$ and in the $i0$ regulator $\eta_0$), such that for $\Gamma$ and every chain term $T_c\Gamma$: (i) the
fibre-integration error of each unit localization $\mathfrak l_u$ — (E.30), (E.31), the remainder of Lemma E.28(iii), and the
truncation terms at fibre endpoints — after replacing the other $i0$ by $i\eta_0$, is bounded pointwise by $C\lambda^a\hat\Phi_R$;
(ii) on boundary layers of relative width $\lambda^a$, $\int\hat\Phi_R\le C\lambda^aL^K$. Comment: fibre-integral version of
Ass. D.7(A3); must handle pinches remaining in the other variables; unproven at any order; Glauber-specific only in that the
integrated variable is a soft light-cone energy.

**Proposition E.35 (one-sidedness after the cut sum).** Assume E.18, E.34; integral form of Lemma E.19's approximations by
D.7(A3) (chain terms (A4)(ii)). Let $(\Gamma,R)\in\mathcal G_0$, and either the theory is abelian or on each side of each jet each
bystander parton carries at most one localizable insertion (else E.33(c1)); $R$ without H-emission endpoints (else E.44(d));
general $\Gamma$: also E.33(a)(b). Then for all $V\in\mathfrak B$, $V\subset U_R$ and all chain terms $T_c$ (incl. $T_\varnothing=1$)
$$\int_V\big(T_cF_\Gamma-\mathfrak r_RT_cF_\Gamma\big)\,d\mu=O(\lambda^aL^K),\qquad\text{(E.35)}$$
in particular $F_\Gamma\doteq_R\tilde F_{\Gamma,R}$ ($C_{R'}\tilde F_{\Gamma,R}:=\mathfrak r_RC_{R'}F_\Gamma$). In $\tilde F_{\Gamma,R}$ and every
$\mathfrak r_RT_cF_\Gamma$: (a) where $\Theta_u=1$, each uncut soft $k_j^-$ appears in $J_A$ either only in initial-state denominators
(endpoint on an active line absorbed by $V_H$) or in no denominator (only in $\phi$); never in cut $\delta$'s; same for $k_j^+$
in $J_B$; in particular on $U_R\setminus K_R$ = Thm. E.8(ii). (b) On $U_R\setminus K_R$ no Glauber-pinched momentum; for any
deformation with $|{\rm Im}\,k_j^-|\le\frac12w_j$, $|\phi(k_j^-/w_j)|\le2\sqrt\pi e^{1/4}$ and its $k_j^-$ integral is unchanged.

*Proof structure.* (R1) = Lemma E.26 applied after Lemma E.19(a)(d) (their errors: scale estimates into (A3)), exact in the sense

#### E.5.1 Structure of the rewritten integrand; deformation map (pp. 205–207)

$F\equiv\sum_{C\in\mathcal C(\Gamma)}\tilde\Gamma_{R,C}$. Only properties used:
* **(F1) Jets.** On $K_R$ the deformation moves only single-orientation components; Def. E.7(b) not required there (E.44(c)).
  $C$ = connected component of the uncut soft subgraph on side $\sigma$; its lines to $J_A$ ($J_B$) = A-legs (B-legs); leg
  momentum $q_e$ into the jet (direct line: $q_a=k$, $q_b=-k$). $J_A$ depends on soft momenta only through
  $$D=\mathcal A_D+\sum_{a\in I(D)}q_a^-+i\sigma0\qquad\text{(E.37)}$$
  ($\mathcal A_D$ independent of all soft minus components) and polynomial numerators; localized A-legs' $q_a^-$ only in
  $\phi(q_a^-/w_a)$ with coefficient $\mathcal J(0)$ or $c_a\mathcal J_+$; $q_a^+$ absent; $q_{Ta}$ a real parameter. $J_B$:
  $D=\mathcal B_D+\sum q_b^++i\sigma0$. Cut $\delta$'s free of uncut soft light-cone components.
* **(F2) Hard part and measurement**: after Lemma E.19(d) $H,H^\dagger$ depend only on $l_A^+,l_B^-,q$ (soft $\mp$ dependence relative
  $O(\lambda^2)$, transverse $O(\lambda)$; transverse components are never deformed). Measurement depends only on $q$ (TMD: also
  transverse momenta).
* **(F3) Soft subgraph**: uncut soft lines carry exact Feynman propagators $i/(l^2-m_l^2+i\sigma0)$ — except localized units
  (external factor at $k_j^\mp=0$; SS: $-1/k_{Tj}^2$ × carrier). Cut soft lines on shell and real.

**Glauber window and direction.** $\rho$ = twice the soft-neighbourhood radius (e.g. $\rho=2\lambda^{\beta_S}$, Def. D.5);
$$\psi(q)=\vartheta\Big(\frac{|q^+q^-|}{\delta\,q_T^2}\Big)\,\vartheta\Big(\frac{\|q\|}{\rho}\Big)\quad(\psi=0\text{ at }q_T=0),\qquad\text{(E.38)}$$
zero outside $W_\delta=\{|q^+q^-|\le\delta q_T^2,\|q\|\le\rho\}$, one on the core $W_\delta^c=\{|q^+q^-|\le\frac12\delta q_T^2,\|q\|\le\frac12\rho\}$;
$q\mapsto\psi(q)|q_T|$ continuous, smooth on $\{q_T\ne0\}$, derivative may grow like $\rho/(\delta|q_T|)$ (only locally Lipschitz,
locally integrable derivative — enough for Cauchy). Glauber direction
$$e_G\equiv\bar n-n,\quad(e_G^+,e_G^-,e_{G,T})=(-1,+1,0_T),\quad e_G^2=-2,\quad n\cdot e_G=1,\quad\bar n\cdot e_G=-1\qquad\text{(E.39)}$$
(here $n=(1,0,0_T)$, $\bar n=(0,1,0_T)$ in $(+,-,T)$ components, $n\cdot k=k^-$, $n\cdot\bar n=1$; verified K8. *Our SCET
convention differs: $n\cdot\bar n=2$*.)

**Definition E.39 (deformation map).** $0<\kappa\le\frac12$.
(i) *Direct lines* (side $\sigma_j$, momentum $k_j$ into $J_A$):
$$k_j^-\mapsto k_j^-+i\sigma_j\kappa\,\psi_As_A|k_{Tj}|,\qquad k_j^+\mapsto k_j^+-i\sigma_j\kappa\,\psi_Bs_B|k_{Tj}|,\qquad k_{Tj}\ \text{fixed}\qquad\text{(E.40)}$$
* AA: $\psi_A=\psi_B=\psi(k_j)$, $s_A=s_B=1$, i.e. $k_j\mapsto k_j+i\sigma_j\kappa\psi(k_j)|k_{Tj}|e_G$.
* SA (A end localizable): $\psi_A=\psi(k_j)\Theta'_j$, $s_A=\lambda^{a_G}$; $\psi_B=\psi(k_j)$, $s_B=1+(\lambda^{-a_G}-1)\Theta'_j$. At
  $\Theta'_j=1$: $k_j^-$ moves $\kappa\lambda^{a_G}|k_{Tj}|=\kappa w_j\le\frac12w_j$, $k_j^+$ moves $\kappa\lambda^{-a_G}|k_{Tj}|$, product
  $\kappa^2k_T^2$. "At $\Theta'_j<1$ $k_j^-$ does not move" (see E6), only $k_j^+$ moves $\kappa\psi|k_{Tj}|$. AS: $A\leftrightarrow B$.
* SS: not deformed; at $\Theta'_j=1$ its $k_j^\pm$ appear only in entire carriers; at $\Theta'_j<1$ soft-angle point.
* H-emission endpoints: definition not applicable ($c_j=\theta(k_j^+)$ non-analytic); → E.44(d).

(ii) *General soft subgraph — Glauber flow.* $C$ a $\sigma$-side uncut soft component with both A- and B-legs and no localized
legs. A Glauber flow assigns real $f_l$ to each line (incl. legs) with: conservation at internal vertices of $C$; $f=0$ on cut
lines; $f_a\ge\kappa\psi(q_a)|q_{Ta}|$ on A-legs; $-f_b\ge\kappa\psi(q_b)|q_{Tb}|$ on B-legs. Deformation
$$l\mapsto l+it\sigma f_le_G\qquad(\text{every line of }C,\ t\in[0,1]),\qquad\text{(E.41)}$$
realized by loop-momentum deformations $k\mapsto k+itv(k)$, closed via $J_A\to H\to J_B$; $f$ from the routing of Prop. E.43:
continuous, piecewise smooth, compactly supported. AA = special case ($f=\kappa\psi|k_T|$). $\Omega_t$, $\Omega\equiv\Omega_1$. Not
treated: (1) components with localized legs *and* internal vertices (need two separately conserved flows) and (2) components
with only one kind of legs (e.g. a soft line with both ends on $J_A$: opposite connecting momenta, $\kappa_j\ge0$ impossible at
both ends; not Glauber-pinched; a single such line can move only $k^+$, limited by its own pole $\sim k_T^2/|k^-|$) → E.44(a).
$\mathcal G_0$ has neither.

**Remark E.40.** By $e_G^2=-2$,
$$(l+it\sigma f_le_G)^2-m_l^2=\big(l^2-m_l^2+2t^2f_l^2\big)+2it\sigma f_l(l^+-l^-)\qquad\text{(E.42)}$$
(verified symbolically, K8): the deformation only pushes real parts towards timelike; timelike/on-shell lines are never crossed;
danger only from spacelike lines with $0<m_l^2-l^2\le2f_l^2$. Contrast with Lemma D.20(b): deforming only $k^-$ ($v^2=0$, real
part unchanged) gives ${\rm Im}\,k^2=2\kappa k^+$ at the on-shell point, forcing $\kappa\sim k_T^2/|k^+|$; here both components move
along $e_G$, and only inside the window where $k^2\le-(1-2\delta)k_T^2$ (distance $\sim k_T^2$ from shell), so magnitude
$\kappa|k_T|$ is allowed. Outside the window $\psi=0$; outside the core $|k^+k^-|\ge\frac12\delta k_T^2$ (not Glauber anyway).

#### E.5.2 Lemma E.41 (no singularity crossed) (pp. 207–209)

**Lemma E.41.** $0<\kappa\le\frac12$, $0<\delta\le\frac18$, deformation of Def. E.39. For all $t\in[0,1]$ and all points of $\Omega_t$:
(a) every $J_A,J_B$ energy denominator of $F$ obeys $\sigma{\rm Im}\,D\ge\sigma{\rm Im}\,D|_{t=0}>0$; (b) each deformed direct line
at $\psi>0$ has ${\rm Re}(k_t^2)\le-\frac14k_T^2$; carriers on localized legs have $|{\rm Im}\,k_j^\mp|\le\frac12w_j$, so
$|\phi|\le2\sqrt\pi e^{1/4}$; (c) every other uncut soft line $l$ with non-zero flow is non-singular on $\Omega_t$ provided at the
real point
$$(N_l)\qquad l^2-m_l^2\ge0\quad\text{or}\quad m_l^2-l^2>2f_l^2;\qquad\text{(E.43)}$$
(d) $H,H^\dagger$, collinear lines without soft momentum, cut $\delta$'s, measurement unaffected; (e) approximator-generated
denominators of $C_{R'}F$ are non-zero *provided the eikonal denominators take the past-pointing prescription*: soft-approximated
jet denominators (Def. D.19) keep the form (E.37); eikonal $n\cdot K+i\sigma0$, $\bar n\cdot K'+i\sigma0$ (or with the spacelike
$n_A(y_A)$, $n_B(y_B)$ of Def. D.21) satisfy $\sigma{\rm Im}\ge0$; collinear approximations act only on $H$. Hence if every
flow-carrying soft internal line satisfies $(N_l)$ (vacuous for $\mathcal G_0$), $\int_{\mathbb R}F=\int_\Omega F$ (sense of D.7(A1):
deform at $i\eta_0$, then $\eta_0\to0$).
*Proof.* (a) ${\rm Im}\,q_a^-=t\sigma f_a$ ⇒ $\sigma{\rm Im}\,D=\eta_0+t\sum_{I(D)}f_a\ge\eta_0$; $J_B$: ${\rm Im}\,q_b^+=-t\sigma f_b$,
$\sigma{\rm Im}\,D=\eta_0+t\sum(-f_b)\ge\eta_0$; relies on Prop. E.35 (only initial denominators) and the sign coherence of Lemma
E.22; a final denominator would give $\eta_0-t\sum f_a$, crossing zero = the single-cut-graph Glauber pinch. (b)
${\rm Re}(k_t^2)=k^2+2t^2\kappa^2\psi_A\psi_Bs_As_Bk_T^2\le(2\delta-1+2\kappa^2)k_T^2\le-\frac14k_T^2$, using $\psi_A\psi_Bs_As_B\le1$
(scan K9: max $=-0.388\,k_T^2$). (c) from (E.42). (d) from (F2). (e) eikonal: ${\rm Im}(n\cdot K)=t\sum f_a$,
${\rm Im}(\bar n\cdot K')=-t\sum f_b$ "times $\sigma$ non-negative" (σ missing in print, E5); $n_A(y_A)=(1,-e^{-2y_A},0_T)$:
$n_A\cdot e_G=1+e^{-2y_A}>0$; $n_B\cdot e_G=-(1+e^{2y_B})$ (K8); the opposite prescription $-i\sigma0$ would put the pole on the swept
side (Fig. 65(b)). Holomorphy for each $\eta_0>0$; deformation compactly supported, Lipschitz on $\{|k_{Tj}|\ge\gamma\}$; Stokes with
boundary faces $|k_{Tj}|=\gamma$ where the amplitude $\le\kappa\lambda^{-a_G}\gamma\to0$; same integrability near $k_T\to0$ as on the
real contour ⇒ boundary terms and thin tubes vanish as $\gamma\to0$.

#### E.5.3 Soft-scale hierarchy and deformation target (pp. 209–212)

Lemma E.41 reduces the problem to combinatorial geometry: on each two-leg-type component find a flow such that all
flow-carrying spacelike lines satisfy $(N_l)$; flow travels only along $C$'s lines from B-legs to A-legs; danger: a softer or
near-shell spacelike internal line forced to carry the flow of a harder leg.

**Definition E.42 (graded configuration).** $C$ as above, $N_C$ its number of legs, $E$ an open set of real soft momenta; $C$
is graded on $E$ (constant $K\ge1$) if there are positive functions $s_1\ge\dots\ge s_m$ on $E$ and levels $i(l)$ with:
(S1) $\|l\|\le s_{i(l)}$; (S2) $l$ spacelike ⇒ $m_l^2-l^2\ge s_{i(l)}^2/K$; (S3) each leg $e$ with $\psi(q_e)>0$ has a path $\pi_e$ in
$C$ to a leg of the other jet through lines with $i(l)\le i(e)$. $m=1$: single soft scale ((S3) automatic); $s_{i+1}\le\theta s_i$:
strong ordering. (Commentary: on window legs (S2) ⇔ $|q_{Te}|\gtrsim s_{i(e)}$; in strong ordering $l^2=p_i^2+O(s_is_{i+1})$; (S2)
fails at near-shell soft lines, (S3) when a window leg connects at its own scale only to same-jet legs or the cut.)

**Proposition E.43.** $0<\delta\le\frac18$, $0<\kappa\le\frac12$, $\rho$ = twice the soft-neighbourhood radius.
(i) $\Gamma\in\mathcal G_0$ without H-emission endpoints: $\Omega$ of Def. E.39(i) is a deformation target (Def. E.7(a)–(c)); on
$U_R\setminus K_R$ at $\|k\|\le\rho/2$ every deformed soft momentum satisfies $|k^+||k^-|\ge c\,k_T^2$, $c=\min(\kappa^2,\delta/2)$
(scan K10: min ratio 1.0006). (ii) General $\Gamma$: if every two-leg-type component without localized legs is graded on $E_C$ and
$\kappa^2<1/(2KN_C^2)$, there is a flow such that every line satisfies $(N_l)$ on $E_C$ and every leg $|q_e^+||q_e^-|\ge cq_{Te}^2$.
(iii) gluing over a finite cover $\{E_\alpha\}$ by a partition of unity, for $\kappa^2<\min_\alpha1/(2K_\alpha N_C^2)$, provided the two
excluded component types are absent.
*Proof.* (i) direct lines with independent loop momenta; Lemma E.41(a)(b)(d)(e); if $|k^+k^-|\le\frac12\delta k_T^2$ then $\psi=1$ and

#### E.5.7 Non-abelian operator structure of bystander–bystander Glauber exchange (pp. 217–218)

After Lemma E.28, $\mathcal G_0$ bystander insertions sit at $x^+=0$ in $J_A$ and $x^-=0$ in $J_B$; $V_G=\sum_{i,j}\chi_{ij}\mathbf T_i\cdot
\mathbf T'_j$, $\chi_{ij}=\chi(b_i-b'_j)$ (only "$\chi$ real and even" used). An ordering convention ("regularization") assigns weights
$w(\pi_A,\pi_B)\in\mathbb C$, $\sum w=1$; then
$$\mathcal A_N=\frac{(-i)^N}{N!}\sum_{(i_e,j_e)_{e=1}^N}\prod_e\chi_{i_ej_e}\sum_{\pi_A,\pi_B}w(\pi_A,\pi_B)\Big[\prod_e^{\pi_A}T_{i_e}^{a_e}\Big]\Big[\prod_e^{\pi_B}T_{j_e}^{\prime a_e}\Big],\qquad\text{(E.50)}$$
$\mathcal A_1=-iV_G$.

**Lemma E.48.** (a) $V_G$ hermitian; $\mathcal U_G=e^{-iV_G}$ unitary (formal power series). (b) abelian: for any weights
$\mathcal A_N=(-iV_G)^N/N!$, $\sum_N\mathcal A_N=\mathcal U_G$. (c) common ordering ($w$ supported on $\pi_A=\pi_B$, generated by a common
non-negative time distribution $\prod_eh_e(t_e)dt_e$): $\sum_N\mathcal A_N=T\exp(-i\int dt\,V(t))$, $V(t)=\sum_{ij}h_{ij}(t)\chi_{ij}\mathbf T_i
\cdot\mathbf T'_j$ hermitian ⇒ unitary; equal $h_{ij}$ ⇒ $\mathcal U_G$; one A and one B bystander: $\exp(-i\chi\mathbf T\cdot\mathbf T')$ =
meaning of the "colour ordering" of (9.2). (d) independent orderings (strict Glauber propagator $-1/k_T^2$, independent symmetric
cutoffs): $N=2$ gives $w=\frac14$ for all four ordering pairs (a property of the regulator; RS's $|2k^z|^{-\eta}$ gives (c));
non-abelian sum not unitary:
$$\mathcal A_2=\tfrac12(-i\chi)^2\big(X^2+\tfrac14C_AX\big),\qquad(1+\mathcal A_1+\mathcal A_2)^\dagger(1+\mathcal A_1+\mathcal A_2)=1-\tfrac14C_A\chi^2X+O(\chi^3).\qquad\text{(E.51)}$$
*Proof.* (a) trivial (N-th order of $e^{iV}e^{-iV}$ is $(iV-iV)^N/N!=0$). (b) colour factors are numbers. (c) A and B colour matrices
commute and pair; Dyson series. (d) in Lemma 9.2's eikonal bystander model the two orderings in $J_A$ give $i/(\pm2p^+k^-+i0)$; over a
symmetric window the principal value vanishes and each $\delta$ part gives $\frac12$ (verified K5: each $=1/(4p^+)$); independently in
$J_B$ ⇒ $w=\frac12\cdot\frac12$; $\mathcal A_2=\frac{(-i\chi)^2}{2}\cdot\frac14\sum_{a,b}\{T^a,T^b\}\{T'^a,T'^b\}$,

with two A and two B bystanders and different time profiles is unitary at $O(\chi^2)$ to machine precision, the
independent-ordering one is not (C7).

**Remark E.49.** (d) shows that strict Glauber + independent symmetric cutoffs neither organize into $(-iV_G)^N/N!$ nor are unitary:

## PART III. ERRORS (verified; page; correction)

**E1 (p. 198, Ass. E.33(c1) — logically inconsistent with the paper's own analysis; moderate–serious).** (c1) asserts that for
non-commuting localized insertions on the same bystander parton (same side) *there is a fixed localization order for which the
per-graph weak equivalence (E.35) holds*. But the paper's own joint-localization analysis (p. 195–196, "不对易情形") shows that the
equal-time limit of two such insertions is a *weighted* sum of the two orders, $w_{12}O_2O_1+w_{21}O_1O_2$, with weights fixed by the
external factors: for single poles $z_j=k_{Tj}^2/(2k_j^+)$ in the same half plane $w_{12}=z_1/(z_1+z_2)$ (re-derived symbolically, K12),
and for $k_1^+k_2^+<0$ (poles in opposite half planes) the order is fixed by the signs of $k_j^+$. A fixed order differs from the true
value by $w_{21}\,[O_1,O_2]$, which is $O(1)$ (a colour commutator times a non-small weight), not $O(\lambda^a)$; integrating over the
other jet's variables does not make it small in general (otherwise ${\rm Re}\,p_{\rm opp}$ would be 0 or 1 graph by graph, contradicting
Ex. E.38(3) and Lemma E.48(d)). The paper itself says (c) "is not expected to hold inside $\mathcal G_0$", yet (c1) is formulated per
graph, where the complete set $\mathfrak G$ cannot help. Consequence: for graphs with coincident insertions Thm. E.8(i)(ii) and
Prop. E.35 rest on a hypothesis that is generically false (the conditional statements become vacuous). **Correction**: replace (c1)
by a *lemma* (multi-insertion version of Lemma E.28): joint localization with ordering weights $w_\pi$ determined by the external
factors; define $\mathfrak r_R$ with these weights (then (E.35) can hold per graph); keep only the complete-set statement (c2) as the
assumption. Then note the new difficulty: the weights depend on $k_j^+$ (the other jet's variables), so the rewritten $J_A$ is no
longer independent of $k^+$ and the deformation of Sect. E.5 (which moves $k^+$ of SA/AS lines by $\kappa\lambda^{-a_G}|k_T|$) must be
re-examined: the weights are *piecewise* in $k^+$ ($z_1/(z_1+z_2)$ for $k_1^+k_2^+>0$, $0$ or $1$ for $k_1^+k_2^+<0$, since the two
external poles then lie in opposite half planes), i.e. non-analytic at $k_j^+=0$ exactly like the coefficient $c_j=\theta(k_j^+)$ that
made the paper exclude H-emission endpoints (E.44(d)). The same obstruction therefore reappears for coincident SA/AS insertions.

## PART V. GAPS (missing or weak; what our monograph must do better)

**G1 (non-abelian coincident insertions, E.33(c)).** The decisive non-abelian step is an assumption; even at $O(\alpha_s^2)$ only
reduced to "${\rm Re}\,p_{\rm opp}=0$ + $C_AX$ real–virtual sum". Literature evidence the paper does not use: Lindsay–Ross–Sachrajda
1982/83 (all two-loop virtual spectator graphs: "the sum of all these non-factorising terms is zero"), Brandt–Frenkel–Taylor 1989
(explicit two-loop cancellation of spectator interactions in an eikonal-meson model), Rothstein–Stewart 2016 (with $|2k_z|^{-\eta}$
crossed Glauber boxes vanish, ladders exponentiate to a hermitian colour-matrix phase; non-abelian crossed-box part carried by the soft
region — our c_glauber §1.6), Schwartz–Yan–Zhu 2018 (pure Glauber ladders cancel for single-scale observables via scale invariance),
Aybat–Sterman 2009 (outline of non-abelian phase cancellation). Our Ch11 should (i) collect this evidence, (ii) attempt the explicit
$O(\alpha_s^2)$ check (two Glauber exchanges + Lipatov real/virtual graphs in a bystander model with a common regulator), and grade
the step accordingly.

**G2 (joint localization).** Fix E1: prove a multi-insertion Lemma E.28 with ordering weights; analyse the (piecewise,
non-analytic at $k_j^+=0$) $k^+$ dependence of the weights in the deformation step — the same mechanism as the H-emission obstruction,
so a common treatment of E.44(d) and coincident SA/AS insertions is needed.

**G3 (Ass. E.44 is needed even for abelian $\mathcal G_0$).** (b) $\mathfrak B$-compatibility and (c) soft-angle region (incl. bystander
couplings at the genuine soft scale $\lambda^2(1,1,1)$) are assumed in all cases; (d) H-emission endpoints even in $\mathcal G_0$. Hence
**no part of the DY Glauber cancellation is unconditional at the cross-section level**, not even the abelian single-exchange family
for arbitrary graphs. Our grading must say so ([A] overall; [R]-conditional sub-lemmas). A cleaner route for (b) might be to choose
$\mathfrak B$ adapted to the windows (sets whose boundaries avoid ${\rm supp}\,\psi$ in the moved directions) — worth exploring.

**G4 (E.18, E.34).** LCOPT zero modes, instantaneous loops with UV renormalization, hermiticity of cut rules for ghosts/three-gluon
vertices; fibre-integral envelope — all unproven; the paper offers no strategy. At least state which parts are known in the light-front
literature (BPP review) and test LCOPT = covariant on a two-loop example.

**G5 (Ward identity / prescription).** The prescription argument (Prop. E.46 step 2) applies the Ward identity to unrewritten
integrands and the deformation to rewritten ones; the reconciliation is relegated to (H1)(H2). For TMD this is the substance of the
past-pointing staple. Our TMD chapter should give an independent one-loop and two-loop check of the past-pointing prescription for
DY (and the future one for SIDIS) — we already have one-loop material in c_tmd.

**G6 (no complete explicit higher-order example).** Examples stop at $O(g^2)$ kinematics and $O(\chi^2)$ colour algebra in the strict
window approximation; no fully worked two-loop example of the whole chain (rewriting → deformation → Wilson lines) for a non-abelian
bystander model. Also only scalar bystanders; no fermion/gluon bystanders (good components $\gamma^+$, Rem. E.17 asserted only).

**G7 (literature comparison).** No comparison with Diehl–Gaunt–Ostermeier–Plößl–Schäfer 2016, who used light-cone perturbation theory
for single and double DY and "revisit and clarify some issues regarding the Glauber cancellation argument … for the single Drell–Yan
process" — the closest existing work to the paper's method; no Aybat–Sterman 2009, LRS, BFT, Zeng 2015, Collins 2011 §14.4.8 "issues
remaining" details.

**G8 (DFT literature and power corrections).** Missing Di'Lieto–Gendron–Halliday–Sachrajda 1981 (agree on non-cancellation, disagree on
intermediate results), Andrási et al. 1981 (bound-state size replaces the IR divergence — exactly Rem. E.53(3)'s heuristic),
Frenkel–Gatheral–Taylor 1984 (power suppression explained, argued to all orders), Catani–Ciafaloni–Marchesini 1986 ("higher-twist
type"); and Basu–Ramalho–Sterman 1984 / CSS 1989 §10.2 (factorization of power corrections itself fails at $m^4/s^2$) — relevant because
Rem. E.53(3) moves DFT into "power corrections" without saying that power corrections are themselves not factorizable beyond $1/Q^2$.

**G9 (TMD details).** No discussion of rapidity-regulator dependence of the past-pointing soft factor vs the SIDIS one, nor of the
time-like vs space-like non-light-like Wilson-line subtlety (our c_tmd §3.4: for time-like past-pointing lines path order is
anti-time order; PT relation fails at finite rapidity). The paper uses Collins' space-like lines, so it is safe, but should say why.

**G10 (window parameters).** No statement that the final result is independent of the auxiliary choices ($a_G,\delta,\kappa,\rho,\vartheta$);
it should be (the integral does not depend on the contour, and $\mathfrak r_R$ only rewrites), but the error constants depend on them.

**G11 (active–active Glauber).** AA lines are simply deformed; no remark that their Glauber part is the Coulomb phase of the soft factor
(RS §10.1: Glauber = zero-bin of the soft graph for active–active), which is what makes TMD soft factors complex in DY vs SIDIS.

**G12 (precision of Rem. E.54(b) and E.55).** See E8, E3.
