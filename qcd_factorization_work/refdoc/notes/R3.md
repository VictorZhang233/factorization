### 1.1 §8 «B 级结果 II：e⁺e⁻→hX 与软因子的幺正性» (pp. 51–55)

Intro (p. 51): simplest factorization problem containing a soft subgraph (several final-state jets with soft exchanges, all jets final
state); the argument "soft factor = 1 by unitarity" is the standard soft-factor elimination of all collinear proofs; "proof" = B level;
in App. D's strict sense conditional on Assumptions D.7, D.14, the cut-sum representation D.27 and the Ward-identity product structure
D.37 (Prop. D.29, Thm. D.46(a)).

**Theorem 8.1 (e⁺e⁻ single-inclusive hadron production, under App. D assumptions)** [grade B]. Assume D.7, D.14, D.27, D.37 (UV
subdivergences: also D.41; MS-bar renormalization of FFs: a multiplicative-renormalization assumption analogous to D.43 — *"FF moments are
not matrix elements of local operators, so (Z1) is an independent input there"*). Then to any order of PT, at leading power,
$$\frac{d\sigma^{e^+e^-\to hX}}{dz}=\sum_i\int_z^1\frac{d\zeta}{\zeta}\,\frac{d\hat\sigma_i}{d\zeta}\Big(\zeta,\frac{Q}{\mu},\alpha_s(\mu)\Big)\,d_{h/i}\Big(\frac z\zeta,\mu\Big)+\text{power-suppressed},\tag{8.1}$$
$d_{h/i}$ the FF of Def. 2.2, $\hat\sigma_i$ the IR-finite partonic cross section. "Any order, leading power" means: partonic, order by
order, remainder $O(\lambda^aL^K)$ (Thm. D.46(a)); reading $d_{h/i}$ as a hadronic FF uses the bridging hypothesis of Def. 1.1.
Proof in four steps: (1) soft gluons not pinched in the Glauber region; (2) eikonal approximation + Ward identities turn the soft
subgraph into a Wilson-line matrix element; (3) unitarity sets the soft factor to 1; (4) unobserved jets go into the hard part, the
observed jet gives the FF.

**§8.1 Lemma 8.2 (final-state lines: poles on one side)** [elementary; proof complete]. Let soft momentum $k$ leave $H$, flow along a
final-state jet $J_B$ (moving in the − direction) and finally exit through the final state. Then, on a given side of the cut, all
$k$-dependent propagators of $J_B$ have their $k^+$ poles in the same half plane.
*Proof:* a line carrying $l+k$, $l$ n̄-collinear near shell, $l^->0$:
$(l+k)^2-m^2+i0=l^2-m^2+2l^-k^++2l^+k^--2\boldsymbol l_T\!\cdot\!\boldsymbol k_T+k^2+i0$, linear in $k^+$ with coefficient
$2(l^-+k^-)\simeq2l^->0$ ⇒ pole at $k^+=(\dots)/(2(l^-+k^-))-i0$ (LHP); $k$ is parallel to $l$ on every line ⇒ all poles in the LHP
(UHP on the conjugate side).

**Corollary 8.3** [partial: one soft momentum]. In e⁺e⁻, for a single soft $k$ joining final-state jets $J_A,J_B$, the $k^+$ integral
(for $J_B$) and the $k^-$ integral (for $J_A$) can be deformed off the real axis on each side of the cut so that $|k^+k^-|\gtrsim\boldsymbol k_T^2$
(simultaneous deformation of several soft momenta not included). Hence the Glauber region $|k^+k^-|\ll\boldsymbol k_T^2$ can be avoided and
the jet-line $i0$'s do not obstruct the eikonal approximation (3.11).
*Proof:* by L8.2 the deformation $k^+\to k^++i\kappa$ crosses no $J_B$ pole (likewise $J_A$, $k^-$; Lemma D.20(a)); but the soft gluon's own
pole $1/(2k^+k^--\boldsymbol k_T^2+i0)$ may lie on the deformation side, so the contour must return to the real axis near it; generically
$\kappa\sim\boldsymbol k_T^2/|k^-|$ (resp. $\boldsymbol k_T^2/|k^+|$), **not** $\sim|\boldsymbol k_T|$ (Lemma D.20(b)) — enough for
$|k^+k^-|\gtrsim\boldsymbol k_T^2$. Multiple soft momenta/general graphs = Assumption D.7(A1) and the first half of (A4)(iii); cf. [1].
*Caveat stated:* leaving the Glauber region does not by itself give the soft-approximation error bound; for $|k^+|\gg|\boldsymbol k_T|$ the
lower bound on the soft-approximated jet denominators is the second half of D.7(A4)(iii), an input.

**§8.2** On the deformed contour the soft approximation (3.11) holds at leading power (error estimate conditional on D.7(A3)(A4),
Lemma D.25). By Cor. 3.17 the sum over all attachments of a soft gluon to jet $J_i$ (direction $v_i$) equals attachment to a
future-pointing Wilson line $W^{(+)}_{v_i}$ from the hard vertex; the $i0$ direction comes from L8.2. Reduced graphs:
$$\sum_{\rm graphs}\simeq H\otimes\Big[\prod_iJ_i\Big]\otimes S,\qquad
S(k_{\rm tot})=\sum_{X_s}\langle0|\,\bar T\Big[\Big(\prod_iW^{(+)}_{v_i}\Big)^\dagger\Big]|X_s\rangle\langle X_s|\,T\Big[\prod_iW^{(+)}_{v_i}\Big]|0\rangle\,\delta^4(k_{\rm tot}-p_{X_s}),\tag{8.2}$$
$J_i$ with soft attachments removed (their longitudinal collinear gluons collected by Thm. 3.14 into Wilson lines along $\bar n_i$,
part of $J_i$); colour indices contracted between $H$ and $S$.

**§8.3 Proposition 8.4 (unitarity of the soft factor)** ["formal proof" + DR-verifiable content]. If the measured quantity does not
depend on $k_{\rm tot}$ at leading power,
$$\int d^4k_{\rm tot}\,S(k_{\rm tot})=\langle0|\,\mathcal U^\dagger\mathcal U\,|0\rangle=\mathbb 1_{\rm color},\qquad \mathcal U\equiv T\Big[\prod_iW^{(+)}_{v_i}(0)\Big].\tag{8.3}$$
*Formal proof:* integrating $k_{\rm tot}$ removes the δ; $\sum_{X_s}|X_s\rangle\langle X_s|=1$ gives $\langle0|\mathcal U^\dagger\mathcal U|0\rangle$ (Fig. 32).
$\mathcal U$ is the time-evolution operator of classical colour sources leaving the origin along time-/light-like $v_i$ (rescaling
invariance, $v_i^0=1$): $\mathcal U=T\exp\big(ig\int_0^\infty dt\sum_iv_i^\mu A^a_\mu(v_it)T^a_i\big)$; path order = time order;
$B(t)\equiv\sum_iv_i\!\cdot\!A^a(v_it)T^a_i$ Hermitian; $\mathcal U(t)=T\exp(ig\int_0^tB)$ obeys $d\mathcal U/dt=igB(t)\mathcal U$ ⇒
$d(\mathcal U^\dagger\mathcal U)/dt=0$ ⇒ unitary on (gluon space ⊗ source colour space). Graphically: Wilson line = propagator of an
infinitely heavy coloured particle; same cutting rule $\frac{i}{v\cdot k+i0}-\frac{i}{v\cdot k-i0}=2\pi\delta(v\cdot k)$.
*Caveats stated (p. 53):* $\mathcal U$ needs regularization (cusp UV divergences; rapidity divergences for light-like lines); in covariant
gauges completeness/Hermiticity hold in the BRST sense. In DR PT (8.3) is directly checkable: after the $k_{\rm tot}$ integral every real
and virtual soft cut graph is scaleless ⇒ 0 (Assumption D.17 (D2)); only the zeroth-order 1 survives; Thm. D.46 uses Prop. 8.4 in this form.
*Check:* cutting identity and $d(\mathcal U^\dagger\mathcal U)/dt=0$ correct; DR-scalelessness correct for both time- and light-like $v_i$.

**Fig. 32** (p. 53): cut soft function; amplitude side (left) and conjugate (right) each with future-pointing Wilson lines from the hard
vertex towards the cut; soft gluons cross the cut (real) or stay on one side (virtual); summed over cuts ⇒ $\langle0|\mathcal U^\dagger\mathcal U|0\rangle=1$.

**Remark 8.5 (when S ≠ 1)**: (8.3) needs full inclusivity in soft momenta; fails for (i) measured hadron $p_T$ relative to the jet axis or
jet-pair imbalance (⇒ TMD soft factor, §10); (ii) event shapes (thrust) as $\tau\to0$; (iii) gaps (no radiation above $E_0$ in an angular
region) ⇒ non-global logarithms. Then factorization (if valid) contains a nontrivial soft function.

**§8.4 Lemma 8.6 (spectral sum of an unobserved jet)** [formal nonperturbatively; order by order in DR PT]. For an unobserved final-state
jet $J_j$ the hard part depends at leading power only on the large component $l^-$. Integrating over $l^+,\boldsymbol l_T$ and summing over
cuts gives the integral of the spectral function of the Wilson-line-gauge-invariant quark propagator, equal to its free value. Hence no
nonperturbative factor: merge into the hard part as a zeroth-order on-shell parton.
*Argument:* $J_j(l)=\sum_X\int d^4w\,e^{il\cdot w}\langle0|W^\dagger\psi(w)|X\rangle\langle X|\bar\psi W(0)|0\rangle$ = spectral function $\rho(l)$; Wilson
line along the light-like direction conjugate to the jet ($n$, coupling $n\cdot A=A^-$, for a jet with large $l^-$); Lorentz covariance ⇒
leading Dirac structure $\slashed l\,\rho_1(l^2,\dots)$ (an $\slashed n$ structure does not contribute in the leading projection);
$\int dl^+d^2l_T\to\int dl^2\rho_1(l^2)$; canonical anticommutator of good components at equal light-cone time (δ function, interaction
independent) ⇒ $=1$ for unrenormalized fields (field renormalization cancels against UV renormalization of $H$): light-cone
Källén–Lehmann sum rule. Formal (light-cone canonical structure, zero modes; no rigorous proof in interacting 4D QCD). In PT: after the
$l^+,\boldsymbol l_T$ integrals $J_j$ depends only on $l^-$; boost invariance (rescale $l^-$ and $n$ together; rescaling $n$ leaves $W$
unchanged) ⇒ no dimensionful scale ⇒ every order beyond zero is scaleless ⇒ 0 in DR; equivalently deform the $l^2$ contour to
$|l^2|\sim Q^2$ where the jet is no longer collinear and can be expanded into $H$.
Observed jet $J_h$: like the DIS target jet; L3.7 + Thm. 3.14: one parton + longitudinal gluons into $H$ → future-pointing Wilson line
along $\bar n$; hadron along $+z$ (large $l^+$); integrating $l^-$, $\boldsymbol l_T$ (hadron $p_T$ relative to the parton) gives the FF (2.13).

**Fig. 33** (p. 54): (a) factorized cut graph: $\gamma^*\to H$, observed hadron jet → $d_{h/i}$, $S=1$, unobserved jet as an on-shell line
$\bar\imath$; red part = $d\hat\sigma_i/d\zeta$; (b) cut-graph form of the FF (2.13): operator vertex ⊗, future-pointing $W^{(+)}_{\bar n}$ double
lines with longitudinal gluons of $J_h$; (c) sum rule for $J_j$: $\int dl^+d^2l_T\sum_X(\dots)=$ zeroth-order value (interaction independent).

**Proof of Thm. 8.1** (p. 54–55): steps 1–3 ⇒ $S=1$; L8.6 ⇒ unobserved jets into $H$; observed jet ⇒ FF; overlaps by the subtraction
method (Thm. 6.3) as in DIS; (H1)–(H3) = Assumption D.14; cut sums D.27; recombination of subtractions D.37; extra issues of several jet
directions in the proof of Prop. D.29; IR finiteness of $H$ from the subtraction construction.

**Remark 8.7 (universality of FFs)** (p. 55): FF Wilson lines are future-pointing; in SIDIS and $pp\to hX$ the observed hadron also comes
from a final-state jet. Collins–Metz [36] argued (graphically) that even for TMD FFs (incl. the T-odd Collins function) reversing the
Wilson-line direction does not change the value — in FFs the eikonal $i0$ does not pinch with other singularities. So FFs are universal
between e⁺e⁻ and SIDIS; in $pp\to hX$ universality is usually assumed (factorization itself unproven, §12; TMD factorization in
$pp\to h_1h_2X$ is broken, Thm. 12.3). Contrast with PDFs (§10.4).

### 1.2 §9 «B′ 级结果：Drell–Yan 过程与 Glauber 区» (pp. 55–62)

**Theorem 9.1 (DY collinear factorization, under App. D & E assumptions)** [grade B′]. Assume D.7, D.14, D.27 (D.14 in the one-sided form of
Prop. D.32; UV subdivergences, renormalization and factor products also need D.37, D.41, D.43, Thm. D.46(c)), and the Glauber input:
either Assumption D.31 directly, or (colour-singlet external states) Assumptions E.18, E.33, E.34, E.44 plus the convergence condition of
Cor. E.10(d), which by Thm. E.8 and Cor. E.10 give the (D.19) needed in Prop. D.32. Then for $A+B\to\gamma^*(q)+X$, to any order, at leading power,
$$\frac{d\sigma}{dQ^2dy}=\sum_{i,j}\int_{x_A}^1d\xi_A\int_{x_B}^1d\xi_B\,f_{i/A}(\xi_A,\mu)f_{j/B}(\xi_B,\mu)\,\frac{d\hat\sigma_{ij}}{dQ^2dy}\Big(\xi_A,\xi_B,\frac Q\mu,\alpha_s(\mu)\Big)+\text{power-suppressed},\tag{9.1}$$
with $f_{i/A}$ the DIS PDFs. Meaning as for Thm. 6.6, here with the integrated form of (D.19); bridging hypothesis for hadrons. Proof
structure (G1)–(G6) "by the CSS standard"; which parts are fully proven: §9.5. The $O(\alpha_s)$ partonic cross section from Fig. 34; its
collinear poles are absorbed by the DIS PDFs (lowest-order universality test; no hadron spectators involved). Plan: solvable model →
general structure → completeness.

**§9.1 The problem** (p. 56). Fig. 35's black gluon; $k$ flows from $s_B$ to $s_A$, through A's active parton into $H$ and back along B's active
parton. In the Glauber region the $k^-$-dependent denominators of $J_A$:
$$\text{spectator } s_A-k:\ (s_A-k)^2-m^2+i0\simeq-2s_A^+k^-+2\boldsymbol s_{AT}\!\cdot\!\boldsymbol k_T-\boldsymbol k_T^2+i0,\qquad
\text{active } a_A+k:\ (a_A+k)^2-m_q^2+i0\simeq a_A^2-m_q^2+2a_A^+k^-+\dots+i0;$$
UHP vs LHP poles ($s_A$ on shell on the cut, $s_A^+,a_A^+>0$) ⇒ $k^-$ pinched; same for $k^+$ in $J_B$. In a single cut graph the Glauber
region is leading and the eikonal approximation fails there (Remark 3.11). Uncancelled, it would make A's spectator transverse-momentum
distribution depend on B (non-universality). *Check:* agrees with our c_glauber §1.2 four-pole table.

**§9.2 Lemma 9.2 (eikonal phase)** [model statement; Abelian proved, non-Abelian single exchange only]. Treat the spectators as on-shell fast
particles propagating after the hard scattering (eikonal spectator approximation: the exchanged gluons' longitudinal momenta are absorbed by
relabelling the final-state phase space through the on-shell conditions; active-parton denominators no longer depend on $k^\pm$; general graphs
in [1] ch. 14). Then in the Glauber region the sum of $N$ exchanges (incl. all crossed graphs) between fast spectators $s_A,s_B$ becomes, in
transverse impact-parameter space,
$$\mathcal M(\boldsymbol b)=\mathcal M^{(0)}(\boldsymbol b)\,\mathcal V(\boldsymbol b),\qquad \mathcal V(\boldsymbol b)=\mathcal P\exp\big(i\chi(\boldsymbol b)\,T^a_A\otimes T^a_B\big)\Big|_{\text{色排序}},\qquad
\chi(\boldsymbol b)\propto g^2\!\int\!\frac{d^2k_T}{(2\pi)^2}\frac{e^{i\boldsymbol k_T\cdot\boldsymbol b}}{\boldsymbol k_T^2},\tag{9.2}$$
$\boldsymbol b$ = transverse separation of $s_A,s_B$ (conjugate to the difference of their final transverse momenta). Abelian: $\mathcal V=e^{i\chi}$, χ
real, unitary. Non-Abelian: A-side and B-side Glauber vertices are ordered in $x^+$ and $x^-$ respectively; "colour ordering" = a
*regularization convention*: a common ordering ("common collision time") on both sides, under which $\mathcal V$ is a time-ordered exponential
of Hermitian generators, hence unitary (Lemma E.48(c)). "It is not a property of the sum of graphs in the strict instantaneous limit: if the
orderings on the two sides are weighted independently in the strict instantaneous Glauber approximation, then at $N=2$ $\mathcal V^\dagger\mathcal V\neq1$,
residual $\propto C_A\chi^2\,\boldsymbol T_A\!\cdot\!\boldsymbol T_B$ (Lemma E.48(d), Remark E.49); whether the missing part is supplied by regions outside
the Glauber window and real soft emission of the same colour structure is the content of Assumption E.33(c), unproven." (Sign convention of χ
opposite to App. E's $V_G$.)
*Proof:* in the Glauber region the $s_A$ propagator depends only on $k^-$ (and $\boldsymbol k_T$); in eikonal form $1/(-2s_A^+k^-+i0)$ the $k^\pm$
integrals separate; single exchange: $\int\frac{dk^-}{2\pi}\frac{1}{-2s_A^+k^-+i0}$ and $\int\frac{dk^+}{2\pi}\frac{1}{2s_B^-k^++i0}$; with a **symmetric
cutoff only the δ parts survive** (the PV part is odd), giving $-i/(4s_A^+)$ and $-i/(4s_B^-)$; with vertex factors $(2s_A^+)(2s_B^-)$ an
energy-independent, purely imaginary correction $i\chi(\boldsymbol k_T)$, $\chi\propto g^2/\boldsymbol k_T^2$ real. For $N$ exchanges summed over crossings
the δ's set all longitudinal momenta to zero and the exchanges multiply in b space: $\frac1{N!}(i\chi)^N$. Non-Abelian: only the single exchange;
ordering and unitarity: Lemma E.48, Remark E.49.
*Checks:* $-i/(4s^\pm)$ values correct. χ as written is IR divergent (needs a regulator; harmless constant phase) — not mentioned.

**Proposition 9.3 (cancellation of spectator–spectator final-state interactions)** [model; Abelian + common-ordering]. In the model of Fig. 35
with the approximation of L9.2, if the spectators' final transverse momenta are integrated, then in the Abelian case (and in the non-Abelian
case under the common-ordering convention) all spectator–spectator Glauber exchanges cancel:
$$\int d^2s_{TA}d^2s_{TB}|\mathcal M|^2=\int d^2b\,{\rm Tr}_c\big[\mathcal M^{(0)\dagger}(\boldsymbol b)\mathcal V^\dagger(\boldsymbol b)\mathcal V(\boldsymbol b)\mathcal M^{(0)}(\boldsymbol b)\big]=\int d^2s_{TA}d^2s_{TB}|\mathcal M^{(0)}|^2.\tag{9.3}$$
Non-Abelian: the $O(\chi)$ exchange cancels in the same approximation; "$N\ge2$ exchanges necessarily attach to the same pair of spectators
(coincident attachment, Def. E.30) and **do not cancel within the graphs of this model**; Assumption E.33(c) assumes that the residual together
with graphs outside $\mathcal G_0$ (Glauber–soft mixing) and real emission is power suppressed (Prop. E.31(iv)); unproven."
*Proof:* Parseval (integrating the momenta conjugate to $\boldsymbol b$ = integrating $\boldsymbol b$; amplitude and conjugate at the same $\boldsymbol b$); Abelian/common
ordering: L9.2 + $\mathcal V^\dagger\mathcal V=1$; non-Abelian $O(\chi)$: ${\rm Tr}_c[\mathcal M^{(0)\dagger}(i\chi X-i\chi X)\mathcal M^{(0)}]=0$, $X=T^a_A\otimes T^a_B$; $N\ge2$:
cannot rest on unitarity of $\mathcal V$ (ordering-convention dependent, Remark E.49); general graphs: App. E replaces the eikonal spectator
approximation by light-cone unitarity of cut sums (L. E.26, P. E.31, under E.18, E.34): Abelian and non-coincident attachments cancel; multiple
non-commuting insertions on the same spectator parton = E.33(c), unproven (Remark E.32). If spectator $k_T$ is measured (or a rapidity gap
required), amplitude and conjugate sit at different $\boldsymbol b$, $\mathcal V^\dagger(\boldsymbol b')\mathcal V(\boldsymbol b)\neq1$: no cancellation.
(See error E1: the "do not cancel within these graphs" claim is an artefact of the strict Glauber truncation.)

**§9.4 General structure (G1)–(G6)** (pp. 59–60), "CSS [3,4], Bodwin [6], organized as Collins [1] ch. 14":

$$F_\pi(Q^2)=\int_0^1dx\int_0^1dy\,\phi_\pi^*(x,\mu)T_H(x,y,Q^2,\mu)\phi_\pi(y,\mu)+O\Big(\frac{\Lambda^2}{Q^4}\Big),\tag{11.2}$$
$$T_H=\frac{16\pi C_F\alpha_s}{Q^2}\frac{f_\pi^2}{8N_c}\frac{1}{\bar x\bar y}\big[1+O(\alpha_s)\big],\tag{11.3}$$
(photon couplings to $u$ and $\bar d$ summed, the latter brought to the former's form by isospin $\phi(x)=\phi(\bar x)$, charge combination $e_u-e_d=1$). For
$\phi=6x\bar x$, $\int_0^1\phi/\bar x=3$:
$$Q^2F_\pi(Q^2)\xrightarrow{Q^2\to\infty}8\pi\alpha_s(Q^2)f_\pi^2\approx0.15\ {\rm GeV}^2\quad(\alpha_s\approx0.35).\tag{11.4}$$
*Proof (CSS standard):* leading region (Thm. 3.2): incoming π jet $J_A$ (+), outgoing π jet $J_B$ (−), hard, soft; boost–dimension argument (as L3.7): each pion jet is
leading with two good-component valence quarks + longitudinal gluons ⇒ $F_\pi\sim1/Q^2$; each extra parton costs at least $1/Q$; longitudinal gluons → Wilson
line (Thm. 3.14). Soft region: soft gluons between $J_A$, $J_B$ cancel at leading power (L11.3); soft-quark (Feynman) mechanism ↔ endpoint $\bar x\to0$; with $\phi\sim\bar x$
it scales as $1/Q^4$ ((3.9)-type counting), i.e. suppressed by $\Lambda^2/Q^2$ and further by Sudakov factors. Endpoint convergence: $\int\phi/\bar x$ converges if
$\phi=O(\bar x^\epsilon)$; power counting uses $\phi=O(\bar x)$; each Gegenbauer term (and any finite sum) vanishes linearly, **but for a nonperturbative DA the endpoint
behaviour of the full series cannot be inferred termwise — $\phi_\pi=O(\bar x)$ is an assumption** (perturbative analogue D.39 (E1)); self-consistency: a divergent
endpoint integral would signal inseparable soft/hard overlap (§11.3). Hard kernel: tree graphs (Fig. 43): gluon $1/(\bar x\bar yQ^2)$, quark $1/(\bar yQ^2)$ or
$1/(\bar xQ^2)$; four graphs + $\gamma^+\gamma_5$ projection ⇒ $T_H$ (**stated, not derived**); $\phi=6x\bar x$ ⇒ (11.4). Precise version (soft region cancels after summing
attachments, endpoint region power suppressed; conditional on endpoint condition, general power counting, D.37 (W1)): P. D.40(a).
*Checks:* (11.3)–(11.4) identical to our c_exclusive result $T_H=\frac{2\pi C_F\alpha_sf_\pi^2}{N_cQ^2}\big[\frac{e_u}{\bar x\bar y}+\frac{e_{\bar d}}{xy}\big]$ (s4: 20/20 PASS); numbers

**Lemma 11.3 (colour-singlet cancellation, eikonal approximation)** [estimate; precise version P. D.40(a)(i)(ii)]. A colour-singlet $q\bar q$ pair, both along $n$,
transverse positions $\boldsymbol b_1,\boldsymbol b_2$; coupling to a soft gluon ($k\sim\Lambda$ in all components) summed over both lines:

process has $|\boldsymbol b_1-\boldsymbol b_2|\sim1/Q$ ⇒ soft coupling suppressed by $\Lambda/Q$.
*Proof:* quark coupling $gn^\mu t^a/(n\cdot k)$, antiquark $-gn^\mu t^a/(n\cdot k)$ (on $\delta_{ij}$ the antiquark generator $-t^{aT}$ acts as $-t^a$ from the right); on the singlet

matrix elements suppressed by $|\boldsymbol b|\Lambda$ (scaling estimate; in PT, after summing all soft attachments to the meson jets, the approximated soft coupling on the
singlet vanishes exactly).

## 3. ERRORS (verified; most severe first)

**E1 (substantive; pp. 57, 58 [Prop. 9.3 + Fig. 36 caption], 61 [§9.5]; also Remark 9.4): the non-Abelian two-Glauber "residual" is a truncation artefact, not
an open obstruction "within these graphs".** The colour algebra is right (verified): with box and crossed box each weighted by half of the δδ term (strict
instantaneous Glauber propagator, independent symmetric cut-offs in $k^\pm$), $\mathcal V^\dagger\mathcal V=1-\frac14C_A\chi^2\boldsymbol T_A\cdot\boldsymbol T_B+O(\chi^3)$. But evaluating
**the same two graphs** exactly for eikonal lines (infinite time-like Wilson lines, rapidity difference $Y$; $k^+k^-$ kept in the gluon propagators — this is the model
L9.2 effectively uses: its $\delta(k^\mp)$ evaluation of the spectator propagators is that of through-going $-\infty\to+\infty$ lines, the active-parton $k$-dependence being
dropped) gives, at fixed transverse momenta,
$$J_{\rm box}=\frac{2\pi}{ab}\,(\pi+iY),\qquad J_{\rm cross}=-\frac{2\pi i\,Y}{ab}\qquad(a=\boldsymbol k_T^2+m^2,\ b=(\boldsymbol K-\boldsymbol k)_T^2+m^2),$$
i.e. ${\rm Re}\,J_{\rm cross}=0$: the whole s-channel (δδ) weight sits in the uncrossed graph (the only one with an s-channel cut). In colour space
$J_{\rm box}L+J_{\rm cross}X=\frac{2\pi}{ab}\big[\pi(\boldsymbol T_A\cdot\boldsymbol T_B)^2-iY\frac{C_A}{2}\boldsymbol T_A\cdot\boldsymbol T_B\big]$: the first term is exactly the common-ordering
$\frac12(i\chi)^2(\boldsymbol T_A\cdot\boldsymbol T_B)^2$, the second is $i\times$real$\times$Hermitian — a one-loop Regge (gluon-reggeization) **phase** $\propto C_AY$ that drops out of
$\mathcal V^\dagger\mathcal V$. So box + crossed box *alone* already give $\mathcal V^\dagger\mathcal V=1+O(\chi^3)$: no real emission is needed (for straight infinite time-like lines the
$O(g)$ one-gluon emission amplitude $\propto\delta(v\cdot k)$ vanishes on shell, so real-emission cuts first enter $\mathcal V^\dagger\mathcal V$ at $O(g^6)$), and — by unitarity of the
full eikonal system, argued but not computed here — the remaining $O(g^4)$ graphs (one-loop corrections of the single exchange, three-gluon "Y" graphs) can only
contribute phases at this order.
The "missing piece" is the principal-value part of the *same* graphs outside the Glauber window, which the symmetric cut-off discards. The refdoc's own App. E
(Lemma E.48(d), Remark E.49, p. ~217–218) concedes that a $k^+$–$k^-$-coupled regulator (Rothstein–Stewart $|2k^z|^{-\eta}$) yields the common ordering and that "in
the exact graphs the s-channel anti-Hermitian part comes only from the uncrossed graph", yet the main text calls the common ordering a mere "regularization
convention", states that the $N\ge2$ terms "do not cancel within the graphs of this model", and requires Assumption E.33(c) "including real emission". Our
c_glauber §1.6 also records that with the R–S regulator every crossed Glauber diagram vanishes and the ladders exponentiate to a Hermitian colour phase
(RS eqs. (9.1), (9.18), (9.19)). **Correction for our text:** (i) present the common (time-)ordering as the physical result; (ii) show the exact $O(g^4)$ eikonal
computation above (it explains the reggeization log and why the instantaneous approximation misassigns the imaginary part); (iii) note that, to all orders in the
eikonal-spectator model, unitarity follows at the *same* formal level as Prop. 8.4 from the unitarity of the evolution operator of two classical sources,
$T\exp\{ig\int dt[v_A\!\cdot\!A(v_At)T_A+v_B\!\cdot\!A(v_Bt+\boldsymbol b)T_B]\}$ — the refdoc applies a double standard (Prop. 8.4 accepted as a formal proof, the analogous
spectator statement labelled an unproven assumption); (iv) locate the genuine open problem elsewhere: all-order interplay of Glauber with soft/collinear modes
for non-eikonal (composite, interacting) spectators and with active partons. Consequence: the refdoc's main justification for grading DY B′ rather than B is
weaker than claimed (a gap box for DY [A] remains appropriate — critic REVIEW B12).

**E2 (p. 64, proof of Thm. 10.3, step (iv)): degenerate subtraction formula.** Printed:
$\tilde f^{\rm unsub}_A\tilde f^{\rm unsub}_B\,\tilde S(+\infty,-\infty)/[\tilde S(+\infty,-\infty)\tilde S(+\infty,-\infty)]=\tilde f_A(\zeta_A)\tilde f_B(\zeta_B)$. With identical arguments the
