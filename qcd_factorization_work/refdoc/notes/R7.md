## 0. One-paragraph summary

App. E gives a *conditional theorem*: Glauber-region cancellation for inclusive Drell–Yan (collinear and
TMD), at every order of non-abelian perturbation theory, for colour-singlet external "hadrons" (point-like
colour-singlet splitting vertices). The argument has four links.

1. Write each collinear subgraph in **light-cone-ordered perturbation theory (LCOPT)**. $J_A$ uses time $x^+$
   and energy $l^-$; $J_B$ uses time $x^-$ and energy $l^+$ ("hybrid" LCPT, one time per jet). The hard vertex
   sits at light-cone time 0 on both sides of the cut, and the total final-state energy $\mathcal E$ of $J_A$ is
   an unweighted variable.
2. In a fixed routing ("standard flow"), every soft momentum enters initial-state energy denominators with
   coefficient $+k^-$ and final-state denominators with $-k^-$. The Glauber pinch is therefore an
   "initial–final" pinch, either within one LCOPT term or between terms.
3. **Sum over all measurement-compatible cuts.** An exact algebraic identity for cut positions (a light-cone
   form of Veltman's largest-time equation) and a closed-time-path representation remove all final-state
   interactions internal to $J_A$. The remaining soft insertions from outside $J_A$ are localized at equal
   light-cone time $\tau=0$ by an exact complex-analysis lemma (Lemma E.28), within scale-adapted windows. This
   holds only in a *weak* (after-integration) sense.
4. Spectator–spectator (SS) Glauber exchange becomes the insertion of a Hermitian operator $V_G$ at $\tau=0$.
   For non-coincident connections the side sum cancels. Multiple non-commuting insertions on the same parton
   in the non-abelian theory do **not** cancel in the strict window approximation; this is left as an
   assumption.

What remains (Part 2, App. E.5–E.6, not in this segment): simultaneous contour deformation, eikonal
approximation, Ward identities giving past-pointing Wilson lines, and soft factor $=1$. The result is
explicitly conditional on four assumptions: E.18 (LCOPT validity: zero modes, instantaneous loops, Hermiticity
of cut rules), E.33 (Glauber–soft mixing and ordering of non-commuting equal-time insertions), E.34 (integrable
envelopes for fibre-integrated bounds) and E.44 (deformation in general configurations, compatibility with the
set algebra, the soft-angle region, H-emission endpoints). It also uses App. D's D.7(A3), D.7(A4)(ii), D.14 and
D.27. This is by far the most careful treatment of the DY Glauber problem I know of in a review-type document,
and it is honest about what is proven. The individual lemmas E.15, E.16, E.22, E.24 and E.28 are genuinely
rigorous mathematical statements. I verified them independently; two proofs have small fixable gaps (§8).

## 1. App. E introduction (p. 162)

* Purpose: the Glauber part of steps (G1)–(G6) of main-text §9, "under explicitly listed assumptions". The
  conclusion is a **conditional theorem, not an unconditional proof of DY factorization** (status in App. E.6,
  Tables 7–8). Scope: inclusive DY at any order and any graph of non-abelian PT, for both collinear and TMD
  factorization (the latter measures only the pair $q_T$).
* Four links: LCOPT for collinear subgraphs (E.3); cut sums with largest-time pairing and equal-time
  localization in windows (E.4); simultaneous contour deformation with eikonal approximation and Ward
  identities giving past-pointing Wilson lines (E.5); assembly of the main theorem with the four remaining
  assumptions (E.6).
* The rewrite holds only after integration ("weak equivalence"). Explicitly **conditional**:
  - several non-commuting localized insertions on the same spectator parton (including multiple spectator
    exchanges) in the non-abelian theory;
  - soft endpoints on collinear lines emitted by the hard vertex;
  - general soft subgraphs and general soft configurations;
  - compatibility of deformation with neighbourhood boundaries;
  - spectator couplings at the soft scale (the "soft-angle region").
* Theorems E.8/E.9 give, through Cor. E.10, the Glauber part of the input to Prop. D.32.
* **Symbol conventions (p. 162):** $\epsilon$ only for DR ($d=4-2\epsilon$). Glauber window width parameter
  $\delta\in(0,\tfrac18]$. Finite $i0$ is written $i\eta_0$, with $\eta_0\to0^+$. $\eta=\pm1$ only for
  orientation. (Small slip: $\delta$ is reused on p. 182 for $|l^+|\ge\delta$; see §8 E6.)

## 2. E.1 Overview and main results (pp. 162–165)

### E.1.1 The problem: the Glauber pinch in a single cut graph (pp. 162–163)
Spectator model of §9 (Fig. 35). The soft gluon $k$ flows from $B$'s spectator $s_B$ to $A$'s spectator $s_A$,
then along $A$'s active parton into the hard vertex. In the Glauber region $|k^+k^-|\ll k_T^2$ the two
$k^-$-dependent Feynman denominators in $J_A$ are
$$-2s_A^+k^-+2\mathbf s_{TA}\!\cdot\!\mathbf k_T-\mathbf k_T^2+i0,\qquad a_A^2-m_q^2+2a_A^+k^-+\dots+i0.\tag{E.1}$$
With $s_A^+>0$ and $a_A^+>0$, their poles lie in the upper and lower half $k^-$-planes, separated by
$O(\lambda^2)$. $k^+$ is trapped in $J_B$ in the same way. (I checked the signs: the first comes from
$(s-k)^2-m_s^2$ with the final spectator $s$ on shell; the second from $(P_A-s+k)^2-m_q^2$.)

(i) In a single cut graph the $k$ contour cannot leave the Glauber region, where the soft approximation (3.11)
fails (Note 3.11). The contour clauses of Assumption D.7, (A1)(a) and the first part of (A4)(iii), fail for
single cut graphs.
(ii) The pinch arises because the same soft momentum flows through lines both before (initial) and after
(final) the collision. If $k$ is trapped in only one jet (spectator of A to active parton of B, the blue gluon
in Fig. 35), the other jet's component can still be deformed. The real difficulty is trapping in both jets
(spectator–spectator).
(iii) In the §9 model the SS exchange cancels after integration over the spectators' final transverse momenta
(Prop. 9.3). In general graphs, Glauber gluons can be many, interleaved with soft and collinear gluons, can
connect final spectators with initial active lines, and carry non-abelian colour. Doria–Frenkel–Taylor [51]
shows that the cancellation fails for coloured external states (§9.5(c), Note E.53). So a general proof must say
precisely after which sums, in which sense (pointwise, integral norm, after integration only), on which
configurations and to which power accuracy the cancellation holds, and which steps remain assumptions.

### E.1.2 Structure of the proof (pp. 163–164), with Fig. 71
(a) Leading regions (Thm 3.12(c), Lemma 8.6): $H$ (including unobserved final jets), $J_A$, $J_B$ and $S$, with
soft momenta $k_1\dots k_s$ connecting $S$ to $J_{A,B}$ (Def. E.1, Fig. 72).
(b) Glauber power counting: $J_A$ sees $k_j$ only through $(k_j^-,\mathbf k_{Tj})$ and $J_B$ only through
$(k_j^+,\mathbf k_{Tj})$; $H$ depends only on $l_A^+,l_B^-$ (Lemma E.19). This is a scaling statement; its
integral form is Assumption D.7(A3).
(c) Light-cone ordering (Lemmas E.15, E.16; Assumption E.18). $V_H$ sits at $\tau=0$ on both sides, and
$\mathcal E$ is an unweighted variable (Lemma E.21). Under standard flow (Def. E.3), initial-state denominators
have poles only at $\operatorname{Im}k_j^-<0$ and final-state ones only at $\operatorname{Im}k_j^->0$ (Lemma
E.22). Hence the pinch is "initial–final" (Def. E.4).
(d) Cut sum and equal-time rewrite. The cut sum of $J_A$ becomes a closed-time-path matrix element. Final-state
interactions internal to $J_A$ cancel exactly by largest-time pairing (Lemmas E.24, E.26). Soft insertions from
outside $J_A$ localize at $\tau=0$ after the $k^-$ integral where their external factors are $k^-$-independent
on $J_A$'s energy scale, i.e. in the scale-adapted windows of Def. E.5 (Lemma E.28). Insertions on spectators
become equal-time insertions commuting with $V_H$. Insertions on active lines emitted by $V_H$ give contact
terms. The rewrites (R1)(R2) (Prop. E.35) hold only in the weak sense, with relative error after fibre
integration over the soft light-cone energy. Integrating over the remaining variables needs Assumption E.34.
The "soft-angle region" outside the windows (including the genuine soft scale $k\sim\lambda^2(1,1,1)$) is not
rewritten.
(e) SS exchange: an insertion of the Hermitian operator $V_G$ at $\tau=0$. Non-coincident connections (each
spectator parton carries at most one localized insertion) cancel by commuting colour matrices. In the abelian
case this is $e^{iV_G}e^{-iV_G}=1$. In the non-abelian theory, multiple exchanges on the same parton do **not**
cancel in the strict window approximation (Lemma E.48(d)); their cancellation is Assumption E.33(c), a
statement about the full graph set including graphs outside $\mathcal G_0$ and real emission (Prop. E.31(iv),
Note E.32).
(f) Simultaneous deformation of uncut soft momenta along Glauber directions (Def. E.39, Lemma E.41). Localized
endpoints move by no more than the carrier width. For $\mathcal G_0$ and graded configurations this gives the
deformation target (Prop. E.43). General configurations, compatibility with $\mathfrak B$, the soft-angle region
and H-emission endpoints form Assumption E.44.
(g) Eikonal approximation on active lines on the deformed contour (Prop. E.45). Ward identities act on App. D's
approximated integrands (subtraction terms) and give Wilson lines; the compatible prescription is past-pointing
(Prop. E.46). The collinear soft factor is 1 by "past" unitarity (Prop. E.47). TMD gives past-pointing staples
and $\tilde S(\mathbf b_T)$ (Prop. E.50).

### E.1.3 Main results and status (pp. 164–165)
The main theorem (E.8) in four sentences:
(i) For every leading region $R$, after the measurement-compatible cut sum, the integrand on $U_R$ equals a
LCOPT-constructed rewritten form $\tilde F_{\Gamma,R}$ **only after integration** (weak equivalence (E.6)),
uniformly for all chain terms of App. D.
(ii) In the rewritten form, inside the windows, each uncut soft momentum through $J_A$ ($J_B$) appears only in
initial-state denominators or only in an entire function.
(iii) Hence, outside the soft-angle region, a simultaneous deformation out of the Glauber region exists. The
eikonal approximation holds on active lines, Ward identities give past-pointing Wilson lines, the collinear soft
factor is 1 and the collinear factors are DIS PDFs.
(iv) Summed over a spectator-exchange family, SS Glauber exchanges of non-coincident connections cancel (all
connections in the abelian case). Non-abelian same-parton multiple exchanges cancel only in the full graph set,
by Assumption E.33(c).
The TMD version (E.9) keeps (i), (ii) and (iv) (soft-scale errors become relative $O(\lambda)$) and replaces
(iii) by past-pointing staples and $\tilde S(\mathbf b_T)$.

Three points on the form of errors:
* The rewrite error is not pointwise. A pinched pole is replaced by a smooth carrier; in a window of width
  $\sim\lambda^2$ they differ pointwise by a relative $O(1)$. $F_\Gamma$ carries pinched $i0$ distributions and
  is not $L^1$, so the integral norm (D.3) is meaningless. What is proved is a relative bound after fibre
  integration over each localized soft light-cone energy (Lemma E.28, (E.31)), pointwise in the other
  variables.
* Integrating these bounds over the remaining variables (still carrying $i0$ distributions) needs an
  integrable envelope: Assumption E.34, the fibre-integrated analogue of D.7(A3).
* Uniformity over chain terms uses (A4)(ii).

The four assumptions are listed. For the class $\mathcal G_0$ (only soft lines directly connecting the two
jets; no H-emission endpoints, otherwise all of E.44(d)): E.33(a)(b) are not needed. E.33(c) is needed only in
the non-abelian theory when two non-commuting localized insertions sit on the same spectator parton. For
$\mathcal G_0$ and graded configurations without localized legs (Def. E.42), Def. E.7(a)–(c) is proved (Prop.
E.43), so E.44(a) is not needed. E.44(b),(c) (and (d) for H-emission) are always needed to go from the main
theorem to Prop. D.32. The last step to (9.1) uses Assumptions D.14 and D.27 as for $e^+e^-$.
Relation to the main text: the steps of §9.5(a)(b) "published only in outline form" are exactly those needing
E.33 and E.44 (Note E.52). The argument follows CSS [135, 3, 4], Bodwin [6] and Collins [1] ch. 14. LCOPT rules
come from Kogut–Soper [131] and Brodsky–Pauli–Pinsky [132]; the cut-sum pairing is Veltman's largest-time
equation [133] plus Cutkosky [134] in light-cone time. Comparison with Rothstein–Stewart [69] is in Note E.56.
(All bibliography entries checked; they are correct. **Aybat–Sterman, PLB 671 (2009) 46**, which introduced
the hybrid LCPT form of exactly this argument, is **not cited anywhere** in the reference document; see §10.)

## 3. E.2 Notation and definitions (pp. 165–176)

Conventions as in §3.3/(3.7): $P_A^+$ and $P_B^-$ are large, $\lambda$ is the IR scale over $Q$ ($q_T/Q$ for
TMD), and $Q=1$ when convenient. App. D's setup ($\mathcal R$, partial order, $U_R$, $\mathfrak S(A)$, $T_R$,
$C_R$) is used unchanged.

### Definition E.1 (data of a leading region) (p. 166)
$\Gamma$ is a forward graph for $A(P_A)+B(P_B)\to\gamma^*(q)+X$ (amplitude and conjugate glued along the final
state, cut not yet specified). $\Gamma_C$ is the cut graph for cut $C$. $R\in\mathcal R$ is a leading region
(Thm 3.12(c); a mode assignment, Def. D.3), with unobserved final jets merged into $H$ (Lemma 8.6).
(a) **Subgraphs.** $H$ on the amplitude side and $H^\dagger$ on the conjugate side (between them the final
state of $\gamma^*$ and the merged jets). $J_A$ and $J_B$ both span the cut, and their final lines may be cut.
$S$ also spans the cut. $J_A$ connects to $H$ by one physical parton and any number of longitudinally
polarized gluons on each side; likewise $J_B$. By (3.9) soft lines do not attach to $H,H^\dagger$.
(b) **Hard vertex:** for $J_A$, $H$ ($H^\dagger$) is a single vertex $V_H$ ($V_H^\dagger$); likewise for $J_B$.
(c) **Active and spectator lines:** lines of $J_A$ with an endpoint on $V_H$ or $V_H^\dagger$ are active. All
others (reaching the cut, or internal) are spectators. Total active momentum $l_A$ ($l'_A$ on the conjugate
side), and $l_B,l'_B$.
(d) **Soft momenta and sides.** Soft (connecting) momenta $k_1\dots k_s$ are oriented *into* the jet they
attach to. A direct $J_A$–$J_B$ line is counted once, with $k_j$ into $J_A$ (so $-k_j$ into $J_B$).
$\sigma_j=\pm1$ according as the attachment vertex is on the amplitude or the conjugate side. An uncut soft line
lies entirely on one side, which is $\sigma_j$. $\mathcal A$ ($\mathcal B$) is the index set of soft momenta
attached to $J_A$ ($J_B$); direct lines belong to both.
(e) **Graph class:** $S_{\rm dir}$ is the set of direct lines. $(\Gamma,R)\in\mathcal G_0$ iff $S=S_{\rm dir}$
(the soft subgraph has no vertices of its own; any number of direct lines, possibly cut, any colour structure).
Lines with both ends on the same jet are not in $S_{\rm dir}$. General $(\Gamma,R)$ allows Glauber momentum
through internal $S$ vertices ("Glauber–soft mixing", E.33).

LCOPT structure used (rules in E.3): each $J_A$ term is labelled by an $x^+$ ordering of the vertices on each
side of the cut. The amplitude side gives $i/(E_{\rm in}(s)-\sum_{l\in s}E(l)+i0)$ per intermediate state, the
conjugate side its complex conjugate, and the cut state $2\pi\delta(E_{\rm in}-\sum_lE(l))$, with
$E(l)=(\mathbf l_T^2+m^2-i0)/(2l^+)$. By Lemma E.21, $V_H$ and $V_H^\dagger$ sit at $\tau\equiv x^+=0$, and
$\mathcal E\equiv\sum_{p\in C\cap J_A}E(p)=P_A^--\hat l_A^-$ is an unweighted variable (E.18).

### Definition E.2 (initial/final state, orientation) (pp. 166–167)
(a) Amplitude-side intermediate states at $\tau<0$ (between the hadron and $V_H$) are *initial*; those at
$\tau>0$ (between $V_H$ and the cut) are *final*. The conjugate side is mirrored.
(b) If $k_j^-$ appears in an initial (final) energy denominator of a term, $k_j$ appears there with orientation
$\eta=-1$ ($\eta=+1$).
(c) "$k_j$ flows only through $\eta=-1$ lines" means: in every LCOPT term $k_j^-$ appears only in initial
denominators, never in final denominators or in the cut $\delta$.
Remark (p. 167): orientation is a property of LCOPT intermediate states, **not of Feynman lines**. A spectator
line can belong to both initial and final states. What decides is whether the attachment vertex is before or
after $V_H$ in light-cone time, plus the routing.

**Fig. 72 (p. 167):** a DY cut graph with leading region. Red dashed cut, amplitude left ($\sigma=+1$),
conjugate right ($\sigma=-1$), hadrons as double lines. $J_A$ (top) and $J_B$ (bottom) span the cut, with one
active parton (arrow) into $H$ or $H^\dagger$ and spectators crossing the cut. The green $S$ also spans the cut.
$k_1$ attaches to A's spectator after the hard scattering; $k_2$ to B's active line before it; $k'_1$ is on the
conjugate side; the orange $k_3\in S_{\rm dir}$ connects the two jets directly. Brackets mark the initial
($\tau<0$) and final ($\tau>0$) segments. The caption repeats that horizontal position is schematic and that
orientation belongs to intermediate states.

### Definition E.3 (standard flow) (pp. 167–168)
For each soft $k_j$ attached to $J_A$ at $v_j$ on side $\sigma_j$, route $J_A$'s loop momenta so that $k_j$
enters at $v_j$, flows along a same-side path to $V_H$ ($\sigma_j=+1$) or $V_H^\dagger$ ($\sigma_j=-1$), and
exits through the active lines:
$$l_A=\hat l_A+\sum_{j\in\mathcal A,\sigma_j=+1}k_j,\qquad l'_A=\hat l'_A+\sum_{j\in\mathcal A,\sigma_j=-1}k'_j,\qquad
\hat l_A=\hat l'_A=P_A-\sum_{c\in C\cap J_A}p_c\tag{E.2}$$
Cut momenta $p_c$ are soft-independent. For $J_B$ the incoming connection momenta are used ($-k_j$ for
$S_{\rm dir}$), so a direct line forms a closed loop through $J_A$, $V_H$, $H$, $J_B$'s active line.
**Existence:** $J_A$'s amplitude part together with $V_H$ is connected (each component carries $O(1)$ positive
plus momentum, which can only come from the hadron or via active lines from $H$). Adding $k_j$ along a path is a
unimodular loop-momentum substitution, an exact identity. The content: soft minus components enter factors
outside $J_A$ only through $l_A^-$, which $H$ and the measurement do not see at leading power (Lemma E.19(d)).
So $\hat l_A^-$ is unweighted and routing soft momenta "out through $V_H$" changes nothing outside $J_A$ (Lemma
E.21).
Consequence: in an amplitude-side LCOPT term,
$$\text{initial denominator}=A_D+\sum_{j\in I(D)}k_j^-+i0,\qquad\text{final denominator}=A'_D-\sum_{j\in F(D)}k_j^-+i0,\tag{E.3}$$
where $A_D,A'_D$ are independent of all soft minus components and $I(D)$ ($F(D)$) is the set injected before
(after, but before the cut). The cut $\delta$ argument $\mathcal E-\sum_cE(p_c)$ is soft-independent. This
generalizes Lemmas 8.2 and 9.5.

### Definition E.4 (Glauber region and Glauber pinch) (p. 168)
Fix $0<\delta\le\frac18$ (the same $\delta$ as the switch function in E.5).
(i) $G_\delta=\{k:\mathbf k_T\neq0,\ |k^+k^-|\le\delta\,\mathbf k_T^2\}$ (for complex $k$, use the moduli of
the components).
(ii) $k_j$ is *pinched in $J_A$* if $k_j^-$ appears in an initial denominator of some term and in a final
denominator of the same or another term: an *intra-term* pinch (same term) or an *inter-term* pinch. Likewise
for $J_B$ with $k_j^+$.
(iii) A direct line is *Glauber-pinched* if pinched in both $J_A$ and $J_B$. For general $S$, a soft loop
momentum is Glauber-pinched if one of the connection momenta it flows through is pinched in $J_A$ and another
in $J_B$.
Three remarks:
1. Cut lines are never in the Glauber region: $k^2=m^2\ge0$ with $\mathbf k_T\neq0$ gives
   $2|k^+k^-|=\mathbf k_T^2+m^2>2\delta\mathbf k_T^2$. Hence Glauber momenta live on uncut lines and $\sigma_j$
   is well defined. ✔
2. Inter-term pinches are also obstructions. Ward identities (Thm 3.14, Cor. 3.17) hold only after summing all
   attachments, and all terms must use the same $k^-$ contour. If some terms want up and others down, the pole
   of the K-term $1/k^-$ at $k^-=0$ lies between the contours; the difference is the Glauber-window residue
   (origin of the imaginary phase of Lemma 9.2). In the Feynman integral, inter-term pinches appear as genuine
   pinches like (E.1) (Ex. E.23).
3. If $k_j$ is pinched in only one jet (A-spectator to B-active), $k_j^+$ can be deformed to
   $|k_j^+|\gtrsim|\mathbf k_{Tj}|$, but this **does not leave $G_\delta$**: with $k^-\sim\lambda^2$ and
   $|\mathbf k_T|\sim\lambda$, $|k^+k^-|\sim\lambda^3\ll\mathbf k_T^2$. It only makes the soft approximation
   valid on the $J_B$ side. To leave $G_\delta$, the $J_A$ end must first be rewritten by Lemma E.28 (Prop.
   E.35, Note E.36(2)). (This corrects a naive reading of (G3); see also Note E.13.)

### E.2.2 Scale-adapted windows and the soft-angle region (p. 169)
Motivation: Lemma E.28 localizes a spectator insertion at $\tau=0$ to accuracy $\lambda_J/\Lambda$, with
$\lambda_J$ the energy scale of $J_A$ and $\Lambda$ the scale on which the external factor varies in $k^-$
($\Lambda=\mathbf k_T^2/(4|k^+|)$ for a direct line). At the Glauber scale $(\lambda^2,\lambda^2,\lambda)$,
$\Lambda\sim1\gg\lambda_J\sim\lambda^2$. At the soft scale $\lambda^2(1,1,1)$, $\Lambda\sim\lambda_J$ and the
localization error is $O(1)$. The soft-scale part is leading power (direct SS line:
$d^4k\cdot\lambda^{-4}\cdot\lambda^{-2}\cdot\lambda^{-2}\sim\lambda^0$, with $d^4k\sim\lambda^8$; checked ✔)
and intersects $G_\delta$, so the rewrite must be restricted to where the scales separate.

**Definition E.5 (localizable endpoints, scale-adapted windows, soft-angle region)** ($\beta_R$ from Def. D.5;
fix $0<a_G<\min_{R'}\beta_{R'}$):
(a) An endpoint $j$ of an uncut soft line in $J_A$ is *localizable* if it sits on a spectator line of $J_A$, or
on an active line emitted by $V_H$ (one with $V_H$ as its earlier endpoint), and the other end of the soft line
is not in $J_A$. A *localizable unit* is a localizable endpoint, or an SS line (direct line with both ends
localizable spectator endpoints, treated as one unit).
(b) Jet energy scale: $\nu_A\equiv P_A^-+\sum_{l\in J_A}E(l)$, summed over all lines of $J_A$ (both sides and on
the cut, oriented $l^+>0$), with $E(l)\ge0$. After Lemma E.19(a), $\nu_A$ depends only on plus and transverse
components (and soft $\mathbf k_T$), not on soft $\pm$ components. In $R$'s scaling region
$\nu_A,\nu_B\sim\lambda^2$.
(c) With $\vartheta\in C^\infty(\mathbb R)$, $0\le\vartheta\le1$, $\vartheta=1$ on $(-\infty,\tfrac12]$ and
$\vartheta=0$ on $[1,\infty)$: for a localizable endpoint $j$ in $J_A$, let $\mu_j\equiv\lambda^{\beta_R}$ (the
bound on $|k_j^+|$ on $U_R$); for an SS line, $\mu_j\equiv\nu_B$. Then
$$\Theta_j\equiv\vartheta\Big(\frac{4\nu_A\mu_j}{\lambda^{a_G}\mathbf k_{Tj}^2}\Big),\qquad
\Theta'_j\equiv\vartheta\Big(\frac{8\nu_A\mu_j}{\lambda^{a_G}\mathbf k_{Tj}^2}\Big),\qquad w_j\equiv\lambda^{a_G}|\mathbf k_{Tj}|.\tag{E.4}$$
$J_B$ follows by $A\leftrightarrow B$; both ends of an SS line use the same symmetric $\Theta_j$. Since
$\operatorname{supp}\Theta'_j\subset\{\Theta_j=1\}$: $\{\Theta_j>0\}$ is the window and $w_j$ the carrier width
of the rewrite.
(d) Soft-angle region: $K_R\equiv\{x\in U_R:\prod_u\Theta'_u(x)<1\}$ (product over all localizable units).
Design remark: the windows depend only on soft $\mathbf k_T$ and on jet plus and transverse components, never on
soft $\pm$ components. This lets them commute with fibre integration in $k_j^-$ (and then $k_j^+$ for SS lines)
and with the deformations of E.5. A $k^+$-dependent window would bring a non-analytic factor into $J_B$'s $k^+$
fibre. The price is that $\mu_j$ replaces $|k_j^+|$ (for SS lines $J_B$'s tail effectively restricts
$|k_j^+|\lesssim\nu_B$). In the scaling region, Glauber $|\mathbf k_T|\sim\lambda$ is inside the window and
soft $|\mathbf k_T|\sim\lambda^2$ is in $K_R$. (Checked: $\Theta_j=1$ requires
$|\mathbf k_T|\gtrsim\lambda^{1+(\beta_R-a_G)/2}$ for $\nu_A\sim\lambda^2$. ✔)

### E.2.3 Cut sums, weak equivalence, deformation targets (pp. 170–171)
**Definition E.6 (measurement-compatible cut sum).**
(a) Collinear case: $\mathcal C(\Gamma)$ is the set of final cuts $C$ through $\gamma^*$ with given $Q^2$ and
$y$, with no other restriction (spectators, unobserved jets and soft radiation are integrated, $\mathbf q_T$
too):
$$F_\Gamma\equiv\sum_{C\in\mathcal C(\Gamma)}\Gamma_C\quad(\text{"cut sum"}).\tag{E.5}$$
It is understood as the common integrand of D.27(X1), defined on the **LCOPT integration domain**. The minus
components of $J_A$'s internal loop momenta (with $\mathcal E$) and the plus components of $J_B$'s are
integrated over the whole real line. The remaining variables (jet plus and transverse components, all four
components of uncut soft lines) are kept. $U_R$, $N_{R'}$ and $\mathfrak B$ are their projections (the conditions
on $J_A$ minus components in Def. D.5 are dropped). Legitimacy at leading power is part of Assumption E.18(i).
(b) TMD: add $\delta^{(2)}(\mathbf q_T-\mathbf q_T(\gamma^*))$. Only the pair's $\mathbf q_T$ is fixed; spectator
transverse momenta stay integrated.
(c) **Weak equivalence.** $\mathfrak B$ is the set algebra generated (finite unions, intersections,
complements) by the $N_{R'}(\lambda)$; in particular $U_{R'}\in\mathfrak B$. $F\doteq_RF'$ iff there are
$C,a>0,K$ such that for all $V\in\mathfrak B$, $V\subset U_R$, and all
$\Phi\in\{1\}\cup\{C_{R'}:R'\in\mathcal R\}$ ($C_{R'}$ the linear maps (6.3); for rewritten forms per Note
E.36(3)), the integrals exist (with each denominator's own $i\eta_0\to0^+$, on real space) and
$$\Big|\int_V\Phi\,(F-F')\,d\mu\Big|\le C\lambda^aL^K\quad\text{uniformly for }\lambda\in(0,\lambda_0],\ \epsilon\in(0,\epsilon_0].\tag{E.6}$$
Remarks: "compatible with measurement" means nothing but the lepton pair is measured. Once spectator transverse
momenta, rapidity gaps or observed hadrons enter, the completeness used in Lemma E.26 fails (Notes E.54,
E.55). Under standard flow the measurement depends on line momenta only through $q$ (out of $V_H$), and at
leading power only through $l_A^+$, $l_B^-$ and transverse components. That is why TMD keeps (i)(ii).
On (c): weak equivalence is the only provable form, since the rewrite preserves $k^-$ integrals and
$\int|F_\Gamma|$ is undefined. By Lemma D.11, $C_{R'}=\sum_{\max c=R'}(-1)^{|c|-1}T_c$, so (E.6) for all chain
terms implies (E.6) for all $C_{R'}$.

**Definition E.7 (deformation target).** Let $G$ be an integrand (e.g. $\tilde F_{\Gamma,R}$). Consider a
continuous homotopy $h:[0,1]\times\mathbb R^N\to\mathbb C^N$, $h_0=\mathrm{id}$, moving only the $\pm$
components of $R$'s uncut soft loop momenta (collinear and hard momenta and all transverse components stay
real), with $\Omega=h_1(\mathbb R^N)$. $\Omega$ is a *deformation target* for $G$ if:
(a) $h_t(x)=x$ when $\|k\|$ is large, or when every soft momentum of $R$ is outside a fixed neighbourhood of
$G_\delta$;
(b) on $h_1(U_R\setminus K_R)$ every uncut soft momentum appearing in a denominator of $G$ has
$|k_j^+||k_j^-|\ge c|\mathbf k_{Tj}|^2$ ($c$ independent of $\lambda$);
(c) for all $t$, $h_t(\mathbb R^N)$ contains no singularity of $G$ or of $C_{R'}G$, so integrals on $\Omega$
equal real-space integrals with each denominator's $i0$.
If moreover (d) $\int_VG'd\mu=\int_{h_1(V)}G'd\mu_\Omega$ for all $V\in\mathfrak B$, $V\subset U_R$ and
$G'\in\{G\}\cup\{C_{R'}G\}$, then $\Omega$ is *compatible with $\mathfrak B$*.
Remarks: (a)–(c) are the Glauber part of D.7's contour clauses. (c) is (A1)(a) for deformations localized near
$G_\delta$. (b) is the first part of (A4)(iii), but only outside $K_R$ and only for soft momenta in denominators
(those only in entire carriers, e.g. localized SS lines, have nothing to avoid). The second part of (A4)(iii) is
not a Glauber issue and stays in D.7. On $K_R$, (b) is not provided: there the integrand is not rewritten and
any deformation with $|k^+k^-|\gtrsim\mathbf k_T^2$ crosses final-state poles (E.44(c)). (d) is unnecessary in
D.7 (one contour); here the rewrite is region-dependent. A sufficient condition for (d): each moved
component's fibre of $V$ is a finite union of intervals at whose endpoints $h_t$ is the identity (then Cauchy
on each fibre). General $V$ fail this, so **(d) is an assumption even for $\mathcal G_0$**.

### E.2.4 Main theorem (pp. 171–173)
**Theorem E.8 (main theorem, collinear DY)** (boxed). $\Gamma$ is any DY forward graph (non-abelian QCD, any
order). The external $A,B$ are **colour singlets** (e.g. split into partons by colour-singlet point vertices,
Fig. 35); coloured external partons are excluded (Note E.53). $R$ is a leading region, $U_R$ its dominated set
(Def. D.5, on the LCOPT domain), $K_R\subset U_R$ the soft-angle region, and $F_\Gamma$ the collinear cut sum.
Assume E.18, E.33, E.34, and for (iii) also E.44(a). If $R$ has H-emission endpoints (uncut soft lines on active
lines emitted by $V_H$ or $V_H^\dagger$, App. E.4.5), the corresponding parts of (i)–(iii) are replaced by
E.44(d). Then:
(i) **Rewrite.** $\tilde F_{\Gamma,R}\equiv\mathfrak r_RF_\Gamma=\sum_{C\in\mathcal C(\Gamma)}\tilde\Gamma_{R,C}$
(E.34) satisfies
$$F_\Gamma=\sum_{C\in\mathcal C(\Gamma)}\Gamma_C\doteq_R\tilde F_{\Gamma,R},\tag{E.7}$$
i.e. $\int_V(T_cF_\Gamma-\mathfrak r_RT_cF_\Gamma)\,d\mu=O(\lambda^aL^K)$ for every $V\in\mathfrak B$,
$V\subset U_R$ and every chain term $T_c$, hence (E.6) for every subtraction term
($C_{R'}\tilde F_{\Gamma,R}\equiv\mathfrak r_RC_{R'}F_\Gamma$). What is *proved* is the relative bound after
fibre integration over the localized soft light-cone energies (pointwise elsewhere). The integral form uses
E.34; the integral form of Lemma E.19 uses D.7(A3), and chain uniformity uses (A4)(ii).
(ii) **One-sidedness.** In $\tilde F_{\Gamma,R}$ and each $\mathfrak r_RT_cF_\Gamma$, on $U_R\setminus K_R$ (more
generally, where the window functions equal 1), under standard flow, each uncut soft $k_j$ through $J_A$ has
$k_j^-$ either only in initial ($\eta=-1$) denominators or only in an entire carrier $\phi(k_j^-/w_j)$, never in
the cut $\delta$. Likewise for plus components into $J_B$. (Soft branches with both ends on one jet are
excluded; see after Def. E.39(ii).)
(iii) **Deformation and factorized structure.** A deformation target $\Omega_R$ (Def. E.7(a)–(c)) exists. On
$\Omega_R$ the soft approximation (3.11) for couplings of Glauber-core soft momenta to active lines holds with
relative error $O(\lambda^a)$ (in the scaling sense; Prop. E.45). Off-core momenta have no Glauber-specific
difficulty and are controlled by the second part of D.7(A4)(iii). After summing all graphs of a given order and
all attachments of soft and longitudinal collinear gluons, Ward identities turn the approximated couplings into
Wilson lines. The unique prescription compatible with $\Omega_R$ is past-pointing, $W_n^{(-)}$ and
$W_{\bar n}^{(-)}$ (except localized spectator terms, handled by (iv) and Prop. E.46(c); determining the
prescription involves a term-by-term check assigned to App. D's (H1)(H2), see the proof of E.46). The soft
factor equals 1 and the collinear factors are the DIS PDFs.
(iv) **SS exchange.** For $(\Gamma_0,R_0)\in\mathcal G_0$, summed over its spectator-exchange family
$\mathfrak F(\Gamma_0)$ (Def. E.30), the in-window SS exchanges of each non-coincident connection cancel after
integrating their momenta (Prop. E.31(iii)). If all connections in the family are non-coincident (in particular
in the abelian theory), (E.33) holds. The cancellation is on the window core. Window functions depend on the
side assignment through $\nu_A,\nu_B$, so at window edges the side sum need not factorize; these "window-edge
terms" lie in the soft-angle region and are not estimated. Non-abelian coincident connections leave residues
that generally do not cancel within the family (Prop. E.31(iv), Note E.32). E.33(c2) only asserts that,
combined with the rest of the full graph set $\mathfrak G$ and neighbouring soft regions (incl. real emission),
they are $O(\lambda^aL^K)$ in the weak sense.
Paragraph on $\mathcal G_0$ (no H-emission endpoints): E.33(a)(b) are not needed. (c) is needed only in the
non-abelian theory when some spectator parton carries at least two non-commuting localized insertions on the
same side (for (i)(ii)) or on both sides combined (for (iv)), of SS, SA or AS type. A single SS line does not
automatically exempt from (c) if the core has an SA or AS localized insertion on the same parton (that makes the
connection coincident). For $\mathcal G_0$ and graded configurations without localized legs (Def. E.42), the
target in (iii) is given by Prop. E.43 and E.44(a) is not needed.
Proof pointer (E.6.1): (i)(ii) follow from Lemmas E.15, E.21, E.22 and Prop. E.35; (iii) from Prop. E.43 (or
E.44(a)), Props. E.45–E.47 and Note 3.15; (iv) is Prop. E.31. The remark that "(i) is stated only weakly, the
strongest form that can hold" is somewhat rhetorical (§8 E9). "Soft factor 1, PDFs" refers to the approximated
integrand; assembly into (9.1) needs the subtraction method (Cor. E.10) and always E.44(b)(c).

## 4. E.3 Light-cone-ordered perturbation theory (pp. 176–187)

Intro (p. 176): (1) at fixed plus and transverse components, the full minus-component integral of $J$ equals a
sum over vertex orderings with energy denominators (E.15); numerator $l^-$-dependence becomes contact vertices,
polynomial in external energies, and non-absolutely-convergent "instantaneous loops" are independent of all
minus energies (E.16); only zero modes and instantaneous-loop values remain unprovable (E.18). (2) In the
Glauber region $J_A$ sees soft momenta only via $(k^-,\mathbf k_T)$ and $H$ ignores active minus components
(E.19), so $V_H$ is at $\tau=0$ and $\mathcal E$ is unweighted (E.21). (3) Under standard flow, initial
denominators contain $+k_j^-$ and final ones $-k_j^-$, so co-directional imaginary shifts only increase initial
imaginary parts (E.22). The spectator model is made explicit with the pinch (E.23). It uses Lemmas 3.7, 3.9,
3.10 and Def. 2.1.

### E.3.1 Light-cone time, intermediate states, energy denominators (pp. 176–178)
For a line of mass $m_l$ ($m_l=0$ for gluons and ghosts):
$$l^2-m_l^2+i0=2l^+\big(l^--E(l)\big),\qquad E(l)\equiv\frac{\mathbf l_T^2+m_l^2-i0}{2l^+}\quad(l^+\neq0).\tag{E.9}$$
$E(l)$ lies in the lower half-plane for $l^+>0$ and the upper for $l^+<0$. Fourier transform at fixed
$l^+,\mathbf l_T$:
$$\int\frac{dl^-}{2\pi}e^{-il^-\tau}\frac{i}{2l^+(l^--E(l))}=\frac{\theta(l^+)\theta(\tau)+\theta(-l^+)\theta(-\tau)}{2|l^+|}\,e^{-iE(l)\tau}.\tag{E.10}$$
(Checked by residues, all four sign cases ✔.) Positive-$l^+$ lines propagate only to larger $x^+$; negative-$l^+$
lines are reversed antiparticles. This is the origin of the light-front Hamiltonian formulation [131, 132].

**Fig. 73 (p. 177):** (a) $l^-$ plane: one pole, below for $l^+>0$ and above for $l^+<0$; for $\tau>0$ close in
the LHP (dashed arc), picking only the $l^+>0$ pole. (b) $k^-$ plane of Ex. E.23: initial-ordering pole
$\alpha-i0$ (blue, below), final-ordering pole $\beta+i0$ (red, above), pale crosses for more poles at higher
order; the real contour (dark blue) is trapped inside the orange window $|k^-|\lesssim\lambda^2$.

**Definition E.14 (light-cone time and energy).** $J$ is connected (vertex set $V$, $N=|V|\ge2$, internal lines
$L$), all plus and transverse components given, $l^+\neq0$.
(i) For $J_A$: $\tau_v\equiv x^+_v$, line energy $l^-$, on-shell energy $E(l)$ of (E.9). For $J_B$, swap
$+\leftrightarrow-$.
(ii) Each internal line is oriented along $l^+>0$ (reversing labels if needed; for fermions this is the
antiparticle interpretation), with tail $t(l)$ and head $h(l)$. $q_v^-$ is the minus momentum flowing into $J$
at $v$ (hadron or parton, soft $k_j$, outgoing momenta with a minus sign).
(iii) A compatible ordering is a total order $\pi:\{1..N\}\to V$ with
$\pi^{-1}(t(l))<\pi^{-1}(h(l))$; $\mathrm{Ord}(J)$ is their set. The $i$-th intermediate state is
$S_i^\pi=\{l\in L:\pi^{-1}(t(l))\le i<\pi^{-1}(h(l))\}$, with injected energy
$Q_i^\pi=\sum_{j\le i}q^-_{\pi(j)}$ and denominator
$$D_i^\pi=Q_i^\pi-\sum_{l\in S_i^\pi}E(l)\qquad(\text{each }-E(l)\text{ contributes }+i0/(2l^+)).\tag{E.11}$$
(iv) When the amplitude part of $J_A$ contains $V_H$ (at $\tau=0$ by Lemma E.21), states before $V_H$ are
initial and after are final (conjugate mirrored); contact vertices are classified by their time.
Since $J$ is connected, no intermediate state is empty (otherwise the first $i$ vertices would be disconnected
from the rest). This is used for convergence.

### E.3.2 Deriving LCOPT from Feynman integrals (pp. 178–182)
**Lemma E.15 (LCOPT from Feynman integrals).** Let $J$ satisfy Def. E.14 and have **no self-loops**. Assume
line numerators $N_l$ independent of internal minus components ("reduced numerators"; the general case is E.16),
and drop vertex factors as overall constants. For $q^-=(q_v^-)_{v\in V}\in\mathbb R^N$ define
$$I_J(q^-)=\int\prod_{l\in L}\frac{dl^-}{2\pi}\prod_{v\in V}2\pi\delta\Big(q_v^-+\sum_{h(l)=v}l^--\sum_{t(l)=v}l^-\Big)\prod_{l\in L}\frac{iN_l}{2l^+(l^--E(l))}.\tag{E.12}$$
Then
$$I_J(q^-)=2\pi\delta\Big(\sum_{v\in V}q_v^-\Big)\prod_{l\in L}\frac{N_l}{2l^+}\sum_{\pi\in\mathrm{Ord}(J)}\prod_{i=1}^{N-1}\frac{i}{D_i^\pi}.\tag{E.13}$$
Precisely: with $i0\to i\eta_0$ ($E(l)_{\eta_0}=(\mathbf l_T^2+m_l^2-i\eta_0)/(2l^+)$), (E.12) without the overall
$\delta$ converges absolutely, and (E.13) holds pointwise for each $\eta_0>0$; the limit $\eta_0\to0^+$ is taken
in the sense of tempered distributions in $q^-$.
**Proof (5 steps; I checked each):**
1. Single-line time representation: $f_l(x)=iN_l/[2l^+(x-E(l)_{\eta_0})]$ and
   $g_l(\Delta)=N_l\theta(\Delta)e^{-iE(l)_{\eta_0}\Delta}/(2l^+)$ satisfy $\int d\Delta\,e^{ix\Delta}g_l=f_l$,
   with $\|g_l\|_{L^1}=\|N_l\|/\eta_0$ and $\sup|g_l|=\|N_l\|/(2l^+)$. ✔
2. Absolute convergence: eliminate $N-1$ minus components with the $\delta$'s, leaving $n=|L|-N+1$ loop
   variables $u$ and $l^-=\ell_l(u)+a_l$. Then $|f_l(\ell_l(u)+a_l)|\le C'_l(1+|\ell_l(u)|)^{-1}$, locally
   uniformly in $q^-$, and it suffices that $\int d^nu\prod_l(1+|\ell_l(u)|)^{-1}<\infty$. By Weinberg's theorem
   [76] (Hahn–Zimmermann form [140]) this holds if $|L_W|>\dim W$ for every non-zero subspace $W$, where $L_W$ is
   the set of forms not vanishing identically on $W$. Flows in $W$ are cycles of the subgraph
   $(V(L_W),L_W)$, so $\dim W\le|L_W|-|V(L_W)|+c_W$. With no self-loops each component has $\ge2$ vertices, so
   $\dim W\le|L_W|-c_W\le|L_W|-1$. ✔ (correct and neat)
3. Time representation: dominated convergence with $e^{-\gamma|u|^2}$, Fubini, then $\gamma\to0^+$ forces zero
   circulation of $\{\Delta_l\}$, i.e. $\Delta_l=\tau_{h(l)}-\tau_{t(l)}$:
   $$I_J(q^-)=\int\prod_{v\in V}d\tau_v\,e^{-i\sum_vq_v^-\tau_v}\prod_{l\in L}g_l\big(\tau_{h(l)}-\tau_{t(l)}\big).\tag{E.14}$$
   Relative times are absolutely integrable via a spanning tree (tree factors in $L^1$, others bounded); the
   section integral is continuous at zero circulation; the common translation gives $2\pi\delta(\sum q^-)$.
4. Sector decomposition: only compatible orderings contribute (since $g_l\propto\theta$). In sector $\pi$, with
   $\Delta_i=\tau_{\pi(i+1)}-\tau_{\pi(i)}\ge0$,
   $\sum_lE(l)(\tau_h-\tau_t)=\sum_i\Delta_i\sum_{S_i^\pi}E(l)$ and
   $\sum_vq_v^-\tau_v=\tau_{\pi(N)}\sum_vq_v^--\sum_i\Delta_iQ_i^\pi$ (summation by parts ✔). Each
   $\int_0^\infty d\Delta_i\exp[i\Delta_i(Q_i^\pi-\sum E(l)_{\eta_0})]=i/D_i^\pi$ converges since $S_i^\pi\neq\varnothing$
   and $\operatorname{Im}D_i^\pi=\sum_{S_i}\eta_0/(2l^+)>0$.
5. $\eta_0\to0^+$: $\prod_ii/D_i^\pi$ involves $N-1$ linearly independent affine forms (the partial sums
   $Q_i^\pi$), so the product of $i/(D+i0)$ is a well-defined tempered distribution.
Remark (p. 180): this agrees with Kogut–Soper (one $1/(2l^+)$ per line, one $i/(\text{injected}-\sum\text{on-shell}+i0)$
per state). No arc estimates are used; absolute convergence replaces them, which is the role of reduced
numerators and no self-loops. **Grade [R]** (complete). **Verified numerically** (§6, C1): 8 graphs, 1 and 2
loops, agreement to $10^{-30}$; a directed 3-cycle with $\mathrm{Ord}=\varnothing$ gives exactly 0.

**Lemma E.16 (numerators and instantaneous terms)**, Feynman gauge:
(i) Fermions: with $l_{\rm on}\equiv(l^+,E(l),\mathbf l_T)$, $\slashed l+m=(\slashed l_{\rm on}+m)+\gamma^+(l^--E(l))$, so
$$\frac{i(\slashed l+m)}{l^2-m^2+i0}=\frac{i(\slashed l_{\rm on}+m)}{2l^+(l^--E(l))}+\frac{i\gamma^+}{2l^+}.\tag{E.15}$$
The first term has a reduced numerator. The second (instantaneous fermion) is $l^-$-independent; its Fourier
transform is $\propto\delta(\tau_{h(l)}-\tau_{t(l)})$, placing both ends at equal time. (Checked symbolically
with explicit Dirac matrices ✔.)
(ii) Gluon $-ig^{\mu\nu}\delta^{ab}/(l^2+i0)$ and ghost numerators are $l^-$-independent: no instantaneous terms.
(iii) In general the full numerator $\mathcal N$ (fermion lines, three-gluon and ghost–gluon vertices) is a
polynomial in internal minus components and $q_v^-$, and
$$\mathcal N\prod_{l\in L}\frac{i}{l^2-m_l^2+i0}=\sum_{T\subseteq L}c_T\prod_{l\in L\setminus T}\frac{i}{l^2-m_l^2+i0},\tag{E.16}$$
where $c_T$ is a polynomial in $q_v^-$ and in minus components of loops entirely inside $T$ (coefficients
depend on plus and transverse components). $T$ = instantaneous lines; $J/T$ = contracted graph; glued vertices
= contact vertices.
(iv) If $T$ is a forest and $J/T$ has no self-loops, the term satisfies Lemma E.15 and equals $J/T$'s LCOPT sum
(no denominators if $J/T$ has one vertex). Contact vertices sit at one light-cone time, classified per E.14(iv).
(v) Otherwise (instantaneous loops: $T$ has a cycle or $J/T$ has a self-loop), the only non-absolutely-convergent
pieces are one-dimensional integrals $\int dx\,x^r$ and $\int dx\,[2l^+(x-E(l))]^{-1}$. They separate from the rest
and depend only on plus and transverse components of their lines. Removing them leaves a term satisfying (iv).
So for **any linear assignment** of values, each term depends on external minus energies as
"polynomial × product of LCOPT denominators".
Proof: (i) $\slashed l=\gamma^+l^-+\gamma^-l^+-\gamma_T\cdot\mathbf l_T$ and
$\int\frac{dl^-}{2\pi}e^{-il^-\tau}\frac{i\gamma^+}{2l^+}=\frac{i\gamma^+}{2l^+}\delta(\tau)$. (iii) Substitute
$l^-=E(l)+\Delta_l/(2l^+)$ with $\Delta_l=l^2-m_l^2$, expand, and let $\Delta_l^r\cdot i/\Delta_l=i\Delta_l^{r-1}$
cancel propagators into $T$. Bridges of $(V,T)$ are re-expressed by momentum conservation through external
injections and crossing $L\setminus T$ lines; lines on $T$-cycles get loop variables. Iterate; $T$ grows or the
process terminates. (iv) Forest: $\delta$'s glue $T$'s endpoints; Lemma E.15 applies to $J/T$. (v) Choose a loop
basis with variables private to each $T$-cycle and each self-loop of $J/T$; then the self-loop variable appears
only in its own propagator and $T$-cycle variables only in $c_T$. (I checked the bookkeeping: consistent.)
Comment: instantaneous-loop factors have no dependence on external minus energies (for any linear value), so
pole statements are unaffected; their values need covariant regularization of the whole integral (E.18(ii)). In
QED, (i) is Kogut–Soper's instantaneous fermion; light-cone gauge has gluon instantaneous terms, avoided by
using Feynman gauge throughout. **Grade [R]** for the algebra; the values of instantaneous loops are left free.

**Note E.17 (contact vertices coinciding with $V_H$)** (p. 181). Contracting an instantaneous fermion line
between a soft vertex $v$ (soft gluon on an active line) and $V_H$ gives a contact vertex at $\tau=0$, neither
initial nor final. This is power suppressed: (a) the leading soft or Glauber coupling on A-collinear fermions is
$\gamma^+$ ($\bar u\gamma^\mu u\simeq2p^\mu$; $\gamma_T$ and $\gamma^-$ relatively suppressed by $\lambda$ and
$\lambda^2$), and $\gamma^+\gamma^+=0$ kills instantaneous lines adjacent to a $\gamma^+$ vertex (checked ✔); (b)
after contraction the soft gluon effectively attaches to $H$, costing $\lambda^2$ by (3.9). So soft momenta never
enter contact vertices at leading power. Longitudinal collinear gluons attaching to $H$ ($J^+H^-$) are ordinary
active lines (Lemma 3.7). [Scaling argument, grade A-level.]

**Assumption E.18 (applicability of LCOPT).** $R$ is a DY leading region and $J=J_A$ (likewise $J_B$), with
$V_H$, $V_H^\dagger$ and the cut (Lemma E.21).
(i) (Zero modes, endpoints, truncation of minus components) On $U_R$ (integral norm (D.3)), the regularized
Feynman integral of $J$ equals, up to $O(\lambda^a)$: first all internal minus components over the whole real
line at fixed plus and transverse components (Lemmas E.15, E.16), then plus and transverse components over the
set where all $l^+\neq0$. Distributions concentrated at $l^+=0$ (zero modes) and endpoint contributions of plus
integrals are absent at leading power. The minus-component restriction of Def. D.5
($\sigma_A(l)=|l^-|+|\mathbf l_T|\le\lambda^\beta$, its $|l^-|$ part) can be dropped at leading power: the
removed tail $|l^-|\gtrsim\lambda^\beta$ is where denominators are far from zero, of size $\lambda_J/\lambda^\beta$
(e.g. $\int_{|\mathcal E|>\lambda^\beta}d\mathcal E\,H_m=O(\lambda_J/\lambda^\beta)$ for (E.23)). Used in Def. E.6.
(ii) (Instantaneous loops) There is an assignment of the 1D integrals in E.16(v) (depending only on plus and
transverse components; distributions at $l^+=0$ allowed) for which the minus integral equals the E.16 expansion.
(iii) (Hermiticity of cut rules) In Feynman-gauge LCOPT, every internal vertex of $J$ (including three-gluon,
ghost–gluon and E.16 contact vertices) has the same numerical factor (on-shell numerator × vertex factor) on both
sides of the cut: $-iv$ on the amplitude side and $+iv$ on the conjugate side, with the same $v$. This is the
$\alpha_i=\beta_i$ needed in Lemma E.24. It holds directly for quark–gluon and scalar vertices; for ghost and
three-gluon vertices it follows from Hermiticity of the gauge-fixed Lagrangian under suitable ghost conjugation
conventions ('t Hooft–Veltman [139]; Veltman [133]). It was not verified for LCOPT on-shell numerators, hence
listed.
**Status paragraph (p. 182):**
(1) The hyperplanes $l^+=0$ have measure zero; off them E.15 and E.16 are pointwise identities.
(2) For E.16(iv)-type terms, on compact sets with all $|l^+|\ge\delta>0$, the finite-$\eta_0$ integrand is jointly
absolutely integrable, so Fubini gives "minus first" = joint. The difficulty is only near $l^+\to0$, where
$l^-\to\infty$ is needed for finite virtuality, i.e. the line is no longer A-collinear. Those points are not in
$N_R$, or belong to neighbourhoods of smaller regions (D.7(A4)(ii)), so (i) is effectively a statement about
approximated integrands over all space.
(3) By Note E.17, adjacent instantaneous lines vanish for good-component couplings, and for good currents
$\bar\psi\gamma^+\psi$ zero modes ("Z graphs") do not contribute as $q^+\to0$ [132]. This suggests zero modes
can only come from subleading couplings and UV-divergent subgraphs, but it is not proved in general.
(4) The tail estimate needs a D.7(A3)-type envelope.
(5) Whatever the values in (ii), they are independent of all minus energies, so Lemma E.22 and all later pole
arguments do not depend on them.
Not proved: (i) and (ii) for arbitrary QCD graphs including UV renormalization, and (iii) term by term for LCOPT
on-shell numerators. Equivalence of LF and covariant PT has been checked in many cases (review [132]); no
general all-order QCD proof is known to the authors. **Grade:** assumption (I would tag (i)/(ii) [P] in the
literature sense, (iii) provable; see §8, §10).

### E.3.3 Glauber power counting; light-cone locality of the hard vertex (pp. 182–184)
Scaling region: $J_A$ lines $\sim(1,\lambda^2,\lambda)$, $J_B$ lines $\sim(\lambda^2,1,\lambda)$, $H$ lines with
virtuality $\sim1$ ($Q=1$), soft $k_j$ either soft $(\lambda^2,\lambda^2,\lambda^2)$ or Glauber
$(\lambda^2,\lambda^2,\lambda)$. "Relative $O(\lambda^p)$" means the ratio of dropped to kept terms in the same
factor (a scaling statement).

**Lemma E.19 (Glauber power counting).**
(a) In every denominator and numerator of $J_A$, terms containing $k_j^+$ are relatively $O(\lambda^2)$. So
$J_A(k)=J_A(k)|_{k_j^+\to0}[1+O(\lambda^2)]$ (soft plus components set to 0 inside $J_A$ only; in LCOPT, drop
soft parts of $l^+$ in $E(l)$ and numerators).
(b) $J_B$ likewise, with $k_j^-$ terms relatively $O(\lambda^2)$.
(c) In $G_\delta$ ($0<\delta\le\frac18$) the connecting denominator does not vanish and
$\frac{1}{2k^+k^--\mathbf k_T^2+i0}=-\frac{1}{\mathbf k_T^2}(1+r)$ with $|r|\le\frac{2\delta}{1-2\delta}$.
(d) $H$ (with measurement $Q^2$, $y$, and $\mathbf q_T$ for TMD) depends on $l_A^-$ and on soft minus components
routed through $V_H$ only at relative $O(\lambda^2)$; likewise $l_B^+$ and plus components; transverse
dependence is relative $O(\lambda)$, and for TMD transverse components enter only through
$\delta^{(2)}(\mathbf q_T-\dots)$. Hence $H=H(l_A^+,l_B^-;q)$ at leading power.
(e) (TMD scale) For soft $(\lambda,\lambda,\lambda)$ ($\lambda=q_T/Q$) the ratios in (a)(b) become relative
$O(\lambda)$ ($J_A$ keeps $2l^+c^-\sim\lambda$ and drops $2c^+c^-\sim\lambda^2$ and $2l^-c^+\sim\lambda^3$), and
likewise $H$ in (d). At the Glauber scale (a)–(d) are unchanged.
Proof:
* (a) $(l+c)^2-m^2=(l^2-m^2)+2l^+c^-+2l^-c^+-2\mathbf l_T\cdot\mathbf c_T+2c^+c^--\mathbf c_T^2$. Kept:
  $l^2-m^2$ and $2l^+c^-$ ($\sim\lambda^2$); $2\mathbf l_T\cdot\mathbf c_T$ and $\mathbf c_T^2$ are $\sim\lambda^2$
  at Glauber ($\lambda^3,\lambda^4$ soft, also kept). Dropped: $2l^-c^+\sim\lambda^4$ and $2c^+c^-\sim\lambda^4$.
  Numerators carry $c^+$ only as $l^++c^+$.
* (b) Same with $+\leftrightarrow-$.
* (c) $r=x/(1-x)$ with $x=2k^+k^-/\mathbf k_T^2$, $|x|\le2\delta$.
* (d) $(p+l_A+c)^2$: $2p^+(l_A^-+c^-)\sim\lambda^2$ and $2\mathbf p_T\cdot(\cdot)\lesssim\lambda$ against $\sim1$;
  $q^+=l_A^+[1+O(\lambda^2)]$, $q^-=l_B^-[1+O(\lambda^2)]$.
All checked ✔.

**Note E.20 (pointwise scaling vs integral norm).** Like Lemma 3.10, E.19 is a scaling statement. Near zeros of
jet denominators, "relative $O(\lambda^2)$" is not a pointwise bound; turning (a)(b)(d) into $O(\lambda^a)$ in the
integral norm on $U_R$ needs D.7(A3) (and (A4)(iii) after leaving the Glauber region). The LCOPT structure and
pole positions (E.21, E.22) hold before and after the approximation. (c) is a pointwise bound on $G_\delta$.
Three error types are distinguished in E.6.1: scaling, pointwise, and fibre-integrated (the rewrite error of
(R2), integral form E.34).

**Lemma E.21 (light-cone locality of the hard vertex).** Make the approximation E.19(d), so that $H$ and the
measurement ignore $J_A$'s active minus components. In the cut graph, consider $J_A$: amplitude-side vertices
(all endpoints of active lines into $H$, physical partons and longitudinal gluons, merged into one vertex $V_H$),
conjugate-side vertices (incl. $V_H^\dagger$), and lines crossing the final cut (set $X$, on shell,
$p^-=E(p)$). The amplitude part of $J_A$ with $V_H$ is connected (likewise conjugate): by plus-momentum
conservation, a component without the incoming hadron gets only $O(\lambda^2)$ plus momentum from soft lines yet
must send $O(1)$ to the cut or to $V_H$, unless it is a closed collinear subgraph attached only to soft lines,
which by Thm 3.2 is not on a pinch surface. For an amplitude-side ordering $\pi$, cut lines emitted at or before
vertex $i$ are counted in $S_i^\pi$ (they survive to the cut). $K^\pi_{\le i}$ ($K^\pi_{>i}$) is the sum of
amplitude-side soft minus components injected before (after) interval $i$; primes for the conjugate side. Then:
(i) Integration over active minus components (unweighted) fixes both $V_H$ and $V_H^\dagger$ at $\tau=0$.
(ii) After integration,
$$J_A^{\rm cut}=\sum_{\pi,\pi'}\mathcal F_{\pi\pi'}\prod_{i<i_H}\frac{i}{P_A^-+K^\pi_{\le i}-\sum_{S_i^\pi}E(l)}\prod_{i\ge i_H}\frac{i}{\mathcal E-K^\pi_{>i}-\sum_{S_i^\pi}E(l)}
\times\prod_{i'<i'_H}\Big[\frac{i}{P_A^-+K'^{\pi'}_{\le i'}-\sum_{S'^{\pi'}_{i'}}E(l)}\Big]^*\prod_{i'\ge i'_H}\Big[\frac{i}{\mathcal E-K'^{\pi'}_{>i'}-\sum_{S'^{\pi'}_{i'}}E(l)}\Big]^*,\tag{E.17}$$
with $i_H$ the position of $V_H$ in $\pi$ ($i<i_H$ initial, $i\ge i_H$ final). $[\cdot]^*$ means complex conjugation
at real external momenta, then analytic continuation. $\mathcal F_{\pi\pi'}$ is the product of line factors
$1/(2l^+)$, on-shell numerators and vertex factors, and E.16 contact factors (polynomial in $k^-$), independent
of all minus components, and
$$\mathcal E=\sum_{p\in X}E(p).\tag{E.18}$$
Equivalently, $\mathcal E$ is an unweighted variable fixed by the cut's $2\pi\delta(\mathcal E-\sum_XE(p))$.
Initial denominators do not depend on $\mathcal E$, final ones not on $P_A^-$.
(iii) Standard flow is legitimate: denominators depend only on injection vertices, not on internal paths;
different paths differ by translations of plus and transverse loop variables (exact) and by changes in $H$ and
the measurement (relative $O(\lambda^2)$ for minus/plus, $O(\lambda)$ for transverse).
(iv) In operator language (i) says the PDF fields sit at $x^+=0$ (Def. 2.1). For TMD the active transverse weight
$e^{i\mathbf b\cdot\mathbf l_{TA}}$ contains no minus component.
Proof: E.15 and E.16 on the amplitude side with injections $-u^-$ at $V_H$, $P_A^-$ at the hadron, $k_j^-$ at
soft vertices and $-E(p)$ at cut-emitting vertices give $2\pi\delta(P_A^-+K-u^--\mathcal E)$ times the ordering sum.
For $i\ge i_H$ use $u^-=P_A^-+K-\mathcal E$. Conjugate: $u'^-=u^--K+K'$ from forward-graph conservation.
* (i) Relative values of several active $u_r^-$ are unweighted loop variables; in (E.14) they appear as
  $e^{iu_r^-\tau_r}$, giving $\delta(\tau_r-\tau_{r'})$, i.e. one vertex $V_H$. $u^-$ and $u'^-$ appear only via
  $\exp[iu^-(\tau_{V_H}-\tau'_{V_H^\dagger})]$, giving equal times; translation invariance sets both to 0.
* (ii) $\int\frac{du^-}{2\pi}2\pi\delta(\dots)=1$.
* (iii) $D_i$ depends only on $Q_i$ and $S_i$.
* (iv) The $l_A^-$ integral in (2.11).
I checked the derivation of the final-state denominators: correct ✔. **Grade [R]** given E.18 and the
E.19(d) approximation.

### E.3.4 Pole positions of soft momenta in energy denominators (pp. 185–186)
**Lemma E.22 (pole positions in LCOPT).** In (E.17) with standard flow, $\mathcal J_A$ ($\mathcal J'_A$) is the
set of amplitude (conjugate) soft momenta attached to $J_A$, with $k_j$ ($k'_j$) flowing in at the attachment.
In each ordering term:
(i) each amplitude initial denominator is $D=A_D+\sum_{j\in I(D)}k_j^-+i0$ with $A_D=P_A^--\sum_{l\in S}E(l)$
($I(D)$ injected before), and each final one is $D=A'_D-\sum_{j\in F(D)}k_j^-+i0$ with
$A'_D=\mathcal E-\sum_{l\in S}E(l)$ ($F(D)$ injected after). $A_D$ and $A'_D$ are independent of soft minus
components.
(ii) Otherwise $k_j^-$ occurs only polynomially in vertex and contact factors, not in $1/(2l^+)$, on-shell
numerators or the cut condition.
(iii) As functions of $k_j^-$ (others real), initial zeros lie at $\operatorname{Im}k_j^-<0$ and final zeros at
$\operatorname{Im}k_j^->0$. For any $\kappa_j\ge0$, the simultaneous shift $k_j^-\to k_j^-+i\kappa_j$ gives every
initial denominator $\operatorname{Im}D=\sum_{j\in I(D)}\kappa_j+0>0$.
(iv) Conjugate side: $[D']^*$ has zeros at $\operatorname{Im}k'^-_j>0$; $k'^-_j\to k'^-_j-i\kappa'_j$ gives
$\operatorname{Im}[D']^*=-\sum\kappa'_j-0<0$. Both sides: $k^-\to k^-+i\sigma\kappa$.
(v) $J_B$: swap $+\leftrightarrow-$, $\tau=x^-$, energy $l^+$. With $p_m$ flowing into $J_B$, the amplitude
initial denominator is $A+\sum_{m\in I(D)}p_m^++i0$; for direct lines $p_m=-k_j$, so the compatible shift is
$k_j^+\to k_j^+-i\sigma_j\kappa_j^B$.
Proof direct from (E.17) ✔. Remark: this generalizes Lemmas 8.2 and 9.5 to any number of soft momenta and any
ordering, without tracking line directions. The coefficient is fixed by "injected before or after this state".
The key is the uniform $+$ sign in all initial denominators (basis of the simultaneous deformation of E.5, with
$\kappa_j=\kappa\,\psi(k_j)|\mathbf k_{Tj}|$, $\kappa\in(0,\frac12]$, $\psi$ the switch (E.38)). $\kappa_j\ge0$ is a
condition on connection momenta; whether it can be realized by soft-loop deformations (e.g. a soft line with
both ends on $J_A$ has opposite connection momenta) is treated in E.5. A $k_j$ appearing in initial
denominators in some terms and final in others is pinched in $J_A$. **Grade [R].**

**Example E.23 (LCOPT of the spectator model)** (pp. 186–187). Hadron $A$ splits at a point vertex into active
quark $a$ (mass $m_q$) and scalar spectator $s_A$ (mass $m_s$); a Glauber gluon $k$ is exchanged between $s_A$
and $s_B$ in the amplitude. Standard flow: $k$ enters $J_A$ at $v_G$ on $s_A$, runs back along the spectator to
$v_0$, then along $a$ into $V_H$. With final spectator $s$: $s_1=s-k$ (before exchange) and $a=P_A-s+k$. Three
amplitude vertices $v_0,v_G,V_H$, with $v_0$ first, give two orderings:
* (I) $v_0<v_G<V_H$ (initial-state interaction): states $\{a,s_1\}$ and $\{a,s\}$, with
  $D_1=P_A^--E(a)-E(s_1)$ and $D_2=P_A^-+k^--E(a)-E(s)=k^--\alpha+i0$, where $\alpha\equiv E(a)+E(s)-P_A^-$.
* (II) $v_0<V_H<v_G$ (final-state interaction): states $\{a,s_1\}$ (initial) and $\{s_1\}$ (final), with
  $\mathcal E=E(s)$, $D_1$ as before, and $D_3=\mathcal E-k^--E(s_1)=-(k^--\beta)+i0$, where
  $\beta\equiv E(s)-E(s_1)$.
$D_1$ is $k^-$-free; $\alpha$ and $\beta$ depend on $\mathbf k_T$ (and negligible $k^+$), not on $k^-$. Vertex and
on-shell numerators are $k^-$-independent at leading power. The active instantaneous term
$i\gamma^+/(2a^+)$ is a bad component into $H$ (Lemma 3.7). Then
$$\frac{i}{D_1}\Big[\frac{i}{k^--\alpha+i0}+\frac{i}{\beta-k^-+i0}\Big]=\frac{1}{(k^--\alpha+i0)(k^--\beta-i0)},\tag{E.19}$$
using $\beta-\alpha=D_1$. **(Correct sign: positive RHS. The pdftotext output shows a spurious leading "−"; the
image confirms "+". Verified with sympy ✔.)** Times $1/(2a^+2s_1^+)$ this is exactly the product of Feynman
propagators $\frac{i}{a^2-m_q^2+i0}\cdot\frac{i}{s_1^2-m_s^2+i0}$ with $a^-=P_A^--E(s)+k^-$ and
$s_1^-=E(s)-k^-$ (verified ✔). Read-offs:
(a) Orientation: $k$ appears only in initial $D_2$ ($+k^-$) in (I) and only in final $D_3$ ($-k^-$) in (II).
"Active pole below, spectator pole above" becomes "initial-ordering pole below, final-ordering pole above".
$s_1$ belongs both to $\{a,s_1\}$ (initial) and $\{s_1\}$ (final).
(b) Pinch: in the Glauber region $\alpha,\beta=O(\lambda^2)$, the contour is trapped between $\alpha-i0$ and
$\beta+i0$ (Fig. 73(b)) and cannot reach $|k^-|\sim|\mathbf k_T|$. For $J_B$ and $k^+$ the same holds with
initial $-k^+$ and final $+k^+$ ($k$ flows out of $J_B$). So $k$ is Glauber-pinched and the Glauber region is
leading in single cut graphs.
(c) $k^-$ integral: outside factors $R$ (Glauber propagator $\simeq-1/\mathbf k_T^2$ and $J_B$) are
$k^-$-independent on the scale $|k^-|\lesssim\lambda^2$ (E.19(b)(c)). Closing (E.19) in the UHP,
$$\int\frac{dk^-}{2\pi}\frac{1}{(k^--\alpha+i0)(k^--\beta-i0)}=\frac{i}{\beta-\alpha+i0}=\frac{i}{D_1+i0}.\tag{E.20}$$
(Verified numerically ✔.) So after the $k^-$ integral the exchange brings no energy denominator: $J_A$ has its
no-exchange form with the spectator at its pre-exchange momentum $s_1=s-k$ (only $\mathbf k_T$ transferred), and
the exchange effectively happens at $\tau=0$. Each ordering alone converges only conditionally ($\sim1/k^-$), and
its value depends on the regularization (symmetric truncation vs full connecting propagator give different
weights, Note E.29(2); **I verified: symmetric truncation gives each ordering exactly $\tfrac12\,i/D_1$**). The sum
does not: keeping $-1/(2k^+k^--\mathbf k_T^2+i0)$, Lemma E.28(iii) gives $R(0)\,i/D_1[1+O(\lambda_J/\Lambda)]$,
$\Lambda=\mathbf k_T^2/(4|k^+|)$. This is the simplest case of equal-time insertion and of the
regularization-dependent constant $c$.

## 5. E.4 Unitarity of cut sums (pp. 187–196)

Intro (p. 187):
(1) An exact algebraic cut-position identity for a final-state "chain" of $J_A$ (E.24): equal-weight vertices
cancel pairwise at the largest light-cone time.
(2) Closed-time-path representation of the cut sum of $J_A$ (E.26): internal final-state interactions vanish;
only soft insertions from outside remain.
(3) If an insertion's external factor is $k^-$-independent on $J_A$'s energy scale, the $k^-$ integral localizes
it at $\tau=0$ (E.28). Spectator insertions become equal-time insertions commuting with $V_H$; active-line
insertions are initial anyway or give contact terms.
(4) In the windows, localizable endpoints are rewritten via (R2), after which each soft momentum appears in
each jet only with initial orientation or in an entire function (E.35, weak sense, E.34).
(5) SS exchange becomes $V_G$ insertions at $\tau=0$, and non-coincident connections cancel in the family sum
(E.31; abelian and non-coincident cases need no E.33; non-abelian same-parton cases are under E.33(c)).

### E.4.1 The cut-position identity (pp. 187–189)
By E.21, initial denominators do not depend on $\mathcal E$, final ones not on $P_A^-$, and $\mathcal E$ is
unweighted (measure $d\mathcal E/2\pi$ from the active minus integral). So only the final part matters. Fix the
initial orderings on both sides. List the final-part vertices as amplitude $\tau>0$ vertices in increasing
$\tau$, then conjugate $\tau>0$ vertices in decreasing $\tau$: the closed-time-path order from $V_H$ through the
cut to $V_H^\dagger$ (Fig. 75). Call them $v_1\dots v_n$, with states $s_0\dots s_n$ between them ($s_0$ right
after $V_H$, $s_n$ right before $V_H^\dagger$): a **final-state chain**. The cut crosses some $s_j$, with
$v_1\dots v_j$ on the amplitude side and $v_{j+1}\dots v_n$ on the conjugate side. Conversely a chain plus $j$
gives an (amplitude, conjugate) pair of orderings of some cut graph, so summing over cut graphs and orderings
equals summing over chains and $j$. Internal $J_A$ vertices (incl. contact vertices) may sit on either side.
Amplitude (conjugate) insertions, i.e. attachment points of soft lines lying wholly on that side, only to the
left (right) of the cut.
Cut rules: amplitude vertex $-iv$, conjugate $+iv$ ($v$ the Dirac/Lorentz structure with on-shell numerators,
the same as on the amplitude side in the forward-graph trace). States: $i/(\mathcal E-E_i+i0)$ (amplitude) and
$-i/(\mathcal E-E_i-i0)$ (conjugate). Merge each amplitude vertex $v_i$ with the factor $i$ of its left
neighbour $s_{i-1}$, and each conjugate vertex with the $-i$ of its right neighbour $s_i$; the cut state is not
merged. Reduced weights: $\alpha_i=(-iv)\cdot i=v$ (amplitude) and $\beta_i=(+iv)(-i)=v$ (conjugate). Internal
vertices have $\alpha_i=\beta_i$ (E.18(iii)); amplitude insertions have $\beta_i=0$; conjugate insertions have
$\alpha_i=0$. Common factors $\mathcal F$ are dropped. State energies:
$$E_i=\sum_{l\in s_i}E(l)+\sum_{\substack{\text{amp. ins. }v_a\\a>i}}k_a^-+\sum_{\substack{\text{conj. ins. }v_b\\b\le i}}k_b'^-,\tag{E.21}$$
independent of $j$ (amplitude insertions are always left of the cut, etc.). So the term with the cut through
$s_j$ is
$$\mathcal T_j=W_j\int\frac{d\mathcal E}{2\pi}\prod_{i<j}\frac{1}{\mathcal E-E_i+i0}\,2\pi\delta(\mathcal E-E_j)\prod_{i>j}\frac{1}{\mathcal E-E_i-i0},\qquad
W_j\equiv\prod_{i\le j}\alpha_i\prod_{i>j}\beta_i.\tag{E.22}$$
$W_j$ automatically vanishes for forbidden cut positions, and $E_j$ is soft-free. (I checked (E.21) against
Lemma E.22, including that it is the same formula for both sides whenever $W_j\neq0$ ✔.)

**Lemma E.24 (algebraic cut-position identity).** $E_0\dots E_n$ real; $\alpha_i,\beta_i\in\mathbb C$
independent of $\mathcal E$; $\pm i0\to\pm i\eta_0$, $2\pi\delta(x)\to2\eta_0/(x^2+\eta_0^2)$, then
$\eta_0\to0^+$. With
$$H_m(\mathcal E)=\prod_{i<m}\frac{1}{\mathcal E-E_i+i0}\prod_{i\ge m}\frac{1}{\mathcal E-E_i-i0}\quad(1\le m\le n),\tag{E.23}$$
(states $s_0..s_{m-1}$ with amplitude $i0$ and $s_m..s_n$ with conjugate $i0$, no cut),
$$\sum_{j=0}^n\mathcal T_j=\delta_{n0}W_0-i\sum_{m=1}^n(\alpha_m-\beta_m)\prod_{i<m}\alpha_i\prod_{i>m}\beta_i\int\frac{d\mathcal E}{2\pi}H_m(\mathcal E).\tag{E.24}$$
(a) If $\alpha_i=\beta_i$ for all $i$, then $\sum_j\mathcal T_j=\delta_{n0}$: for $n\ge1$ all cut positions sum to
zero.
(b) Only vertices with $\alpha_m\neq\beta_m$ appear. An amplitude insertion contributes only if it is the last
amplitude insertion with no conjugate insertion before it; a conjugate insertion only if it is the first
conjugate insertion with no amplitude insertion after it. So there are at most two terms.
Proof: with $a_i=(\mathcal E-E_i+i\eta_0)^{-1}$ and $b_i=(\mathcal E-E_i-i\eta_0)^{-1}$,
$i(a_i-b_i)=2\eta_0/[(\mathcal E-E_i)^2+\eta_0^2]$. With $H_m=\prod_{i<m}a_i\prod_{i\ge m}b_i$ for
$0\le m\le n+1$: $\prod_{i<j}a_i\,i(a_j-b_j)\prod_{i>j}b_i=i(H_{j+1}-H_j)$. Abel summation gives
$\sum_jW_j(H_{j+1}-H_j)=W_nH_{n+1}-W_0H_0+\sum_m(W_{m-1}-W_m)H_m$ with
$W_{m-1}-W_m=(\beta_m-\alpha_m)\prod_{i<m}\alpha_i\prod_{i>m}\beta_i$. For $n\ge1$, $\int H_{n+1}=\int H_0=0$ (all
poles on one side, at least two factors). For $n=0$, $\int\frac{d\mathcal E}{2\pi}i(a_0-b_0)=1$. Exact for each
$\eta_0$. ✔ **Grade [R].** **Verified numerically** (§6, C2) to $10^{-39}$ for $n=0..6$ with random complex
weights, all-internal chains, and insertion patterns (e.g. "ICA", "CIA", "AICIA" give 0, matching (b)).

**Note E.25 (time representation: largest-time equation).** In (E.14), $\mathcal T_j$ integrates over
light-cone time configurations that increase then decrease along the chain, maximal at the cut; $v_i$ carries
$e^{i(E_i-E_{i-1})\tau_i}$ regardless of side. In any configuration the latest vertex $v_m$ is adjacent to the
cut; moving the cut across it ($j=m$ vs $j=m-1$) gives the same integrand with weights $\alpha_m$ vs $\beta_m$,
so the sum is $\propto\alpha_m-\beta_m$. This is Veltman's largest-time equation [133] in light-cone ordering;
$H_m$ is the energy representation of "$v_m$ at the largest time". The lemma is exact for any injected energies
and weights, so it applies to App. D chain terms $T_c\Gamma$ too (Note E.36(3)). Relation to Cutkosky [134]:
the full cut sum is the discontinuity, which here reduces to the insertion terms on the right of (E.24).

### E.4.2 Light-cone unitarity lemma (pp. 189–191)
$\hat P^-$ is the light-cone Hamiltonian of $J_A$'s A-collinear degrees of freedom (its perturbative expansion is
E.15/E.16 LCOPT, under E.18). $O(\tau)=e^{i\hat P^-\tau}Oe^{-i\hat P^-\tau}$; $\mathrm T$ puts later times
left, $\bar{\mathrm T}$ the reverse. An insertion $O_a$ is the local composite operator at a soft attachment
(leading power: the good component $J^{+a}$ of the colour current, integrated over $x^-$ and Fourier transformed
in $x_T$). It carries $k_a$ into $J_A$ (standard flow), with phase $e^{-ik_a^-\tau_a}$ as in (E.14).

**Lemma E.26 (light-cone unitarity lemma).** Assume E.18 and the E.19(a)(d) approximations, with $J_A$ as in
(E.17). Fix everything outside $J_A$: amplitude insertions $O_a$ ($a\in\mathcal J_A$), conjugate insertions
$O'_b$ ($b\in\mathcal J'_A$) and their external factors. Sum over all measurement-compatible cuts of $J_A$
(those keeping every insertion on its side) and integrate $\mathcal E$ unweighted. Then:
(a) (Closed-time-path representation)
$$\mathcal J_A=\int\prod_ad\tau_a\,e^{-ik_a^-\tau_a}\prod_bd\tau'_b\,e^{+ik_b'^-\tau'_b}\,\langle P_A|\bar{\mathrm T}\Big\{V_H^\dagger(0)\prod_bO_b'^\dagger(\tau'_b)\Big\}\,\mathrm T\Big\{V_H(0)\prod_aO_a(\tau_a)\Big\}|P_A\rangle.\tag{E.25}$$
Expanded in $J_A$'s forward graphs, the terms of a given forward graph equal the sum of all its side-preserving
cuts.
(b) (Disappearance of final-state interactions) In each chain, contributions of internal vertices later than
all insertions cancel in the cut sum. If all insertions are at $\tau\le\tau_*$, only Heisenberg operators at
$\tau\le\tau_*$ appear, and later evolution cancels by $U(\infty,\tau_*)^\dagger U(\infty,\tau_*)=1$. Without
$\tau>0$ insertions, $\mathcal J_A=\langle\Psi'|\Psi\rangle$ with $|\Psi\rangle=V_H(0)\mathrm T\{\prod_aO_a(\tau_a)\}|P_A\rangle$,
so final-state interactions of $J_A$ are completely absent.
(c) (Largest-time pairing) Two families of cut graphs differing only in the side of one insertion $v_m$, with
$v_m$ the latest vertex in the configuration considered, sum to $(\alpha_m-\beta_m)$ times a common factor
(weights including external factors). They cancel if the external factors agree on both sides (Hermitian
structure of cut rules).
Proof:
* (a) Gell-Mann–Low with adiabatic switching gives
  $\mathrm T\{V_H(0)\prod O_a\}|P_A\rangle=U(0,\infty)\mathrm T\{V_{H,I}(0)\prod O_{a,I}e^{-i\int V_I}\}|P_A\rangle_0$;
  the conjugate is the Hermitian conjugate, and $U^\dagger U=1$. Insert complete sets of free light-front Fock
  states between all vertices and integrate over times (E.14): each term is a pair of LCOPT orderings of a cut
  graph, with denominators referred to $E_X$, which is (E.17) at $\mathcal E=E_X$. The unrestricted sum over $X$
  is the unweighted $\mathcal E$ integral plus all cuts. Grouping by forward graph works because vertex and
  propagator factors depend only on the forward graph and the sides. The LF vacuum is trivial (E.18(i)), so no
  division by vacuum amplitudes. Equivalently, at graph level, apply E.24 to each chain: $H_m$ is the "$v_m$
  latest" term ($s_0..s_{m-1}$ in T, $s_m..s_n$ in $\bar{\rm T}$, no projection on $X$ in between).
* (b) From E.24(b).
* (c) Pairing of $j=m$ and $j=m-1$.
**Grade [R]** for (b),(c) as consequences of E.24; (a)'s operator form rests on E.18 (LF vacuum, zero modes).

**Fig. 75 (p. 190):** top row: one chain $V_H\to v_1\to v_2\to v_3\to V_H^\dagger$ in closed-time-path order
(horizontal) vs light-cone time (vertical), with the four cut positions $j=0..3$. Bottom row: for a fixed time
configuration whose latest vertex (orange circle, $v_2$) is internal, moving the cut across it gives the same

**Note E.27 (what the lemma uses)** (p. 191).
(1) Completeness and measurement: the only physical input is that the identity operator stands between cut
states, i.e. $J_A$'s final state is unconstrained and $\mathcal E$ is unweighted. The first is
"measurement-compatible" (Def. E.6). The second comes from E.21: the measurement fixes $q^-$, absorbed at leading
power by $J_B$'s active line. **If one orders the whole graph in a single $x^+$, $H$ depends on $l_B^-$ and the B
end of the hard vertex spreads over $O(1/Q)$ in $x^+$, exactly the Glauber range, so "which vertex is latest"
loses meaning. Hence one light-cone time per jet.** For TMD, $V_H^\dagger$ at $\mathbf b_T$ gives
$e^{i\mathbf b_T\cdot\hat{\mathbf P}_T}$, commuting with $\hat P^-$ (E.52). If a final hadron is measured, the
projector does not commute with evolution and (b)(c) fail (Notes E.54, E.55).
(2) Gauge: E.24 is algebraic and needs no positive metric; in Feynman gauge (a) is an identity between two
perturbative expansions including unphysical polarizations.
(3) What does not cancel: soft lines pin insertions to a side, so $\tau>0$ insertions cannot be paired within
$J_A$ alone (moving one requires moving the other end in $J_B$ or $S$). These are the source of the Glauber pinch
of Ex. E.23.

### E.4.3 Equal-light-cone-time insertions (pp. 191–194)
Why "equal time": in $G_\delta$ the connecting propagator $\simeq-1/\mathbf k_T^2$ is $k^\pm$-independent, so in
coordinate space it is $\propto\delta(x^+-y^+)\delta(x^--y^-)$. $J_A$ ignores $k^+$, so its attachment points
concentrate at $x^-\simeq0$ (within $O(1/Q)$); $J_B$'s concentrate at $y^+\simeq0$. So the Glauber exchange
happens at $x^+=y^+\simeq0$ and $x^-=y^-\simeq0$: instantaneous in both jets' times, exactly at the hard vertex's
time, when the two Lorentz-contracted hadrons pass through each other. "Final" vs "initial" Glauber insertions
differ only infinitesimally around $\tau=0$; the next lemma controls this. Fix one amplitude insertion $O$
(conjugate by complex conjugation) with injected energy $k\equiv k^-$, other variables real. With
$\mathcal J(\tau)$ the integrand of (E.25) as a function of $O$'s time,
$$J(k)=\int d\tau\,e^{-ik\tau}\mathcal J(\tau)=J^{\rm in}(k)+J^{\rm fi}(k),\qquad J^{\rm in}=\int_{-\infty}^0d\tau\,e^{-ik\tau}\mathcal J(\tau),\quad J^{\rm fi}=\int_0^\infty d\tau\,e^{-ik\tau}\mathcal J(\tau).\tag{E.26}$$
In LCOPT, $J^{\rm in}$ ($J^{\rm fi}$) is the sum of orderings with $O$ before (after) $V_H$; by E.22 its
$k$-dependent denominators are all initial (final). $R(k)$ is the external factor (all $k^-$-dependent factors
outside $J_A$). An insertion is "on a spectator" if its $J_A$ line does not end at $V_H$; lines ending at $V_H$
(absorbed active partons and longitudinal gluons, and longitudinal gluons emitted by $V_H$) are active.

**Lemma E.28 (equal-light-cone-time insertion).** Assume:
(J1) $J^{\rm in}$ is analytic in $\operatorname{Im}k>0$ and $J^{\rm fi}$ in $\operatorname{Im}k<0$, both
continuous up to the real axis (with each denominator's own $i0$);
(J2) there are constants $\mathcal J_\pm$, $M\ge|\mathcal J_\pm|$ and $\lambda_J>0$ such that for $|k|\ge2\lambda_J$,
$\big|J^{\rm in}(k)-\frac{i\mathcal J_-}{k}\big|\le\frac{M\lambda_J}{|k|^2}$ ($\operatorname{Im}k\ge0$) and
$\big|J^{\rm fi}(k)+\frac{i\mathcal J_+}{k}\big|\le\frac{M\lambda_J}{|k|^2}$ ($\operatorname{Im}k\le0$);
and $R$ is analytic in $|k|<\Lambda$ with $|R(k)|\le C_R|R(0)|$, $\Lambda\ge8\lambda_J$. Then:
(i) (Equal-time values) $\mathcal J_\pm=\mathcal J(0^\pm)$. In operator language $\mathcal J_-$ ($\mathcal J_+$)
is the matrix element with $O(0)$ just before (after) $V_H(0)$:
$\mathrm T\{V_H(0)O(0^-)\}=V_H(0)O(0)$ and $\mathrm T\{V_H(0)O(0^+)\}=O(0)V_H(0)$, so $\mathcal J_+-\mathcal J_-$
is a matrix element of $[O(0),V_H(0)]$. At graph level
$$\mathcal J_+-\mathcal J_-=\mathcal J_+^{\rm act}-\mathcal J_-^{\rm act},\tag{E.27}$$
the LCOPT terms with $O$ on an active line immediately before (after) $V_H$. If $O$ is on a spectator and no
other non-commuting insertion is already localized at $\tau=0$ on the same side (e.g. on the same parton,
non-abelian), then $\mathcal J_+=\mathcal J_-\equiv\mathcal J(0)$.
(ii) (Window form) For $2\lambda_J\le K\le\Lambda/2$,
$$\int_{-K}^K\frac{dk}{2\pi}J(k)R(k)=R(0)\frac{\mathcal J_++\mathcal J_-}{2}+\frac{iKR'(0)}{\pi}(\mathcal J_--\mathcal J_+)+O\Big(M|R(0)|\Big[\frac{\lambda_J}{K}+\frac{K^2}{\Lambda^2}\Big]\Big),\tag{E.28}$$
with $O$-constants depending only on $C_R$. For spectators this is
$R(0)\mathcal J(0)[1+O(\lambda_J/K+K^2/\Lambda^2)]$, independent of window shape ($[-K_1,K_2]$, $K_{1,2}\ge K$).
(iii) (Full real axis) If moreover $R(k)=\sum_ir_i/(k-z_i)$ is a finite sum of simple poles with
$|z_i|\ge2\Lambda$ (poles may sit on $\mathbb R\mp i0$), $R_\downarrow$ ($R_\uparrow$) the terms with
$\operatorname{Im}z_i<0$ ($>0$) and $\|R\|\equiv\sum_i|r_i/z_i|$:
$$\int_{-\infty}^\infty\frac{dk}{2\pi}J(k)R(k)=\mathcal J_+R_\downarrow(0)+\mathcal J_-R_\uparrow(0)+O\Big(\frac{M\lambda_J}{\Lambda}\|R\|\Big)
=R(0)\big[\mathcal J_-+c(\mathcal J_+-\mathcal J_-)\big]+O\Big(\frac{M\lambda_J}{\Lambda}\|R\|\Big),\quad c\equiv\frac{R_\downarrow(0)}{R(0)}.\tag{E.29}$$
Proof:
* (i) Integration by parts gives $J^{\rm in}=i\mathcal J(0^-)/k+O(k^{-2})$. In LCOPT, $\mathcal J(0^-)$ comes only
  from orderings with $O$ last before $V_H$ (others have a vanishing-duration state). If $O$'s line does not
  end at $V_H$, the orderings $(\dots,O,V_H,\dots)$ and $(\dots,V_H,O,\dots)$ correspond one-to-one with equal
  factors (zero-duration state between them), unless another localized $O'$ with $[O,O']\neq0$ sits at
  $\tau=0$. If $O$ is on a line ending (starting) at $V_H$, only the former (latter) ordering exists.
* (ii) Move $J^{\rm in}$ to the upper semicircle $C^+$ of radius $K$ and $J^{\rm fi}$ to $C^-$. Use
  $R=R(0)+R'(0)k+\rho$ with Cauchy estimates, $\int_{C^+}dk/k=-i\pi$, $\int_{C^-}dk/k=i\pi$, $\int_{C^\pm}dk=2K$.
  The result is $\pi R(0)(\mathcal J_-+\mathcal J_+)+2iKR'(0)(\mathcal J_--\mathcal J_+)+\mathcal R$. ✔ (I
  re-derived all constants.)
* (iii) $\int J^{\rm in}R_\downarrow=0=\int J^{\rm fi}R_\uparrow$ (close the contour). Write
  $J^{\rm fi}=-i\mathcal J_+/(k-i\lambda_J)+E(k)$, with $E$ analytic in the closed LHP and
  $|E|\le3M\lambda_J/|k|^2$. The first piece gives $\mathcal J_+R_\downarrow(i\lambda_J)$. For $\int ER_\downarrow$:
  semicircle of radius $\Lambda/2$, tails, and near poles $E(k)=E(z_i)+[E(k)-E(z_i)]$ with
  $|E'|\lesssim M\lambda_J/\Lambda^3$ (see §8, E2).
**Grade [R]** (the statement is correct; the proof of (iii) has a fixable gap). **Verified numerically** (§6, C4):
error $\propto\lambda_J/\Lambda$ (ratios 10.0 per decade); window errors within bound; asymmetric windows error
$O(\lambda_J/\min K)$.

**Note E.29 (checking the assumptions; constant $c$; regularization dependence)** (pp. 193–194).
(1) (J1)(J2) hold in LCOPT. By E.22, $J^{\rm in}$ is a sum of products of at least one initial denominator
$(A_D+k+i0)^{-1}$, and $J^{\rm fi}$ of $(A'_D-k+i0)^{-1}$, times numerators. E.22(ii) allows polynomial
$k$-dependence through three-gluon and ghost–gluon vertices (linear in adjacent minus components, possibly
containing injected $k^-$) and E.16 contractions. At leading power these are absent: a numerator $\propto$ an
A-collinear minus component (or $k^-\sim\lambda_J$) has boost weight lower by 2 than one $\propto$ a plus
component, i.e. relative $O(\lambda^2)$; adjacent instantaneous lines vanish (Note E.17). Dropped polynomial
terms count as (S2) scaling errors; if kept, $k^r$ × denominators would give $\delta^{(n)}$ contact terms at the
soft vertex, for which (J2) fails. So (J1) holds and $|(A_D+k)^{-1}-k^{-1}|\le2|A_D|/|k|^2$ for
$|k|\ge2\max|A_D|$. (J2) holds with $\lambda_J=\max_D(|A_D|,|A'_D|)$ plus the not-yet-localized $|k_i^-|$, and
$\mathcal J_-$ is the coefficient of terms with exactly one $k$-dependent denominator ($O$ adjacent to $V_H$).
All $|A_D|,|A'_D|\le\nu_A$, so $\lambda_J\le C_\Gamma(\nu_A+\sum_i|k_i^-|)$.
(2) External factor of a Glauber line and the regularization dependence of the weights. For a direct line at
fixed $k^+\neq0$ and $\mathbf k_T$, $J_B$ is $k^-$-independent (E.19(b)), and
$R\propto(2k^+k-\mathbf k_T^2+i0)^{-1}$ has one pole at $k=\mathbf k_T^2/(2k^+)$: LHP for $k^+>0$, UHP for
$k^+<0$. Take $\Lambda=\mathbf k_T^2/(4|k^+|)$ ($|R|\le2|R(0)|$); (iii) gives $c=\theta(k^+)$. With the
$G_\delta$ fibre window $|k|\le K=\delta\mathbf k_T^2/|k^+|=4\delta\Lambda$, (ii) gives $c=\tfrac12+O(\delta)$
(since $KR'(0)=2\delta\,\mathrm{sgn}(k^+)R(0)$). These are the weights of Ex. E.23(c) and Ex. E.37: $\tfrac12$
each for symmetric truncation, and $0$ or $1$ by $\mathrm{sgn}\,k^+$ with the full propagator. The difference
comes from outside $G_\delta$ ($|k^+k^-|\sim\mathbf k_T^2$, connecting line near shell). Only for
$\mathcal J_+\neq\mathcal J_-$ (active lines) does the choice matter; spectators give $R(0)\mathcal J(0)$ either
way. Window errors contain $O(K^2/\Lambda^2)=O(\delta^2)$, not a power of $\lambda$, so (R2) uses only (iii),
whose error $\lambda_J/\Lambda$ is a positive power of $\lambda$ on the E.5 windows. **Verified (§6, C4):**
$c_{\rm window}=\tfrac12-\tfrac{2i}{\pi}\delta\,\mathrm{sgn}(k^+)+O(\delta^3)$, i.e. the $O(\delta)$ correction is
purely imaginary, and $c_{\rm full}=\theta(k^+)$.
(3) Equal-time ordering: (E.29) is the ordering convention
$\mathrm T_c\{V_H(0)O(0)\}\equiv(1-c)V_H(0)O(0)+cO(0)V_H(0)=V_H(0)O(0)+c[O(0),V_H(0)]$. The first term is the
initial insertion; the second is a contact term with the soft line attached to an active line at $\tau=0$
(E.27). $c$ is complex in general, fixed by the pole structure of the external factor, and absent when
$\mathcal J_+=\mathcal J_-$.
(4) Consistency with Ward identities: for spectator insertions the $1/k$ tails of $J^{\rm in}$ and $J^{\rm fi}$
cancel, $J(k)=O(\lambda_J/k^2)$ for $|k|\gg\lambda_J$. So eikonal-region soft gluons ($|k^-|\gg\lambda_J$) do not
couple to spectators at leading power and see only active lines: the LCOPT form of Cor. 3.17. ✔ (nice)
(5) Chain terms $T_c$ do not change ordering structure or the graph-theoretic characterization of
$\mathcal J_\pm$. Eikonalizing $J_A$ lines zeroes their on-shell energies, so $\lambda_J$ only decreases (for
spectators $J\to2\pi\mathcal J(0)\delta(k)$ ✔). So the lemma holds with the same constants for every $T_c\Gamma$.

### E.4.4 Spectator–spectator Glauber exchange (pp. 194–196)
For $(\Gamma,R)\in\mathcal G_0$, classify uncut direct lines as SS (both ends on spectators), SA (spectator in
$J_A$, active in $J_B$), AS, and AA. By E.28(i) the classification is exact near $\tau=0$: only lines ending at
$V_H$ make $\mathcal J_+\neq\mathcal J_-$, provided no other non-commuting localized insertion sits on the same
side and parton. Notation: $\nu_A,\nu_B,\Theta_j$, $w_j=\lambda^{a_G}|\mathbf k_{Tj}|$,
$\Lambda_j\equiv\mathbf k_{Tj}^2/(4|k_j^+|)$.

**Localizing one SS line.** Take an amplitude-side SS line $j$; fix $\mathbf k_{Tj}$ and all else (other
insertions real). By E.19(a)(b), $J_A$ depends only on $k_j^-$ and $J_B$ only on $k_j^+$; otherwise only
$G_j=(2k_j^+k_j^--\mathbf k_T^2+i0)^{-1}$ depends on $k_j^\pm$. It is **not** replaced by $-1/\mathbf k_T^2$ (whose
$O(\delta)$ error is not a power of $\lambda$).
Step 1: fix $k_j^+\neq0$ and integrate $k_j^-$ over $\mathbb R$. $R(k_j^-)=G_j\times(\dots)$ has one pole at
distance $2\Lambda_j$, with $\|R\|=|R(0)|$. If $\Lambda_j\ge8\lambda_J^A$ ($\lambda_J^A\le C_\Gamma\nu_A$), Lemma
E.28(i)(iii) ($\mathcal J_+=\mathcal J_-$, no $c$) gives
$$\int_{-\infty}^\infty\frac{dk_j^-}{2\pi}J_A(k_j^-)R(k_j^-)=R(0)\mathcal J_A(0)\big[1+e_j(k_j^+)\big],\qquad|e_j(k_j^+)|\le C\frac{\lambda_J^A}{\Lambda_j}\le\frac{4CC_\Gamma\nu_A|k_j^+|}{\mathbf k_T^2}.\tag{E.30}$$
Step 2: integrate $k_j^+$. $R(0)=-\mathbf k_T^{-2}\times(\dots)$ is $k_j^+$-independent. For spectator endpoints
$J_B(k^+)=O(\lambda_J^B/(k^+)^2)$ (Note E.29(4)), and Fourier inversion gives
$\int\frac{dk^+}{2\pi}J_B(k^+)=\mathcal J_B(\tau=0)$ exactly (continuity at $\tau=0$). Step 1's error applies for
$|k_j^+|\le X_j\equiv\mathbf k_T^2/(32C_\Gamma\nu_A)$; beyond that $J_B$ is in its tail
$O(\nu_B/(k_j^+)^2)$. After both fibre integrals the relative error scales as
$$\frac{\nu_A\nu_B}{\mathbf k_T^2}\Big(1+\ln\frac{\mathbf k_T^2}{\nu_A\nu_B}\Big),\tag{E.31}$$
which is $O(\lambda^{a_G}L)$ on the SS window ($4\nu_A\nu_B\le\lambda^{a_G}\mathbf k_T^2$). "Scaling": step 1 is an
exact inequality, but $M$ and $|R(0)|$ still carry $i0$ distributions in other variables (other $J_B$
denominators, initial denominators of $\mathcal J_A(0)$). Integrating these bounds is E.34; the
$|k_j^+|>X_j$ estimate is also only a scaling estimate. The order of the two steps can be swapped.
**Verified (§6, C6):** in an explicit toy (Ex. E.23-type $J_A$, $J_B$, full $G$), the relative error divided by
$x(1+\ln(1/x))$, $x=\nu_A\nu_B/\mathbf k_T^2$, is constant ($\approx0.41$) over eight decades; error divided by
$x$ grows like $\ln(1/x)$. **So the logarithm is genuine, not an artefact of the bound.**
Vertex factors: $J_A$'s leading coupling is "$-igJ_A^{+a}$" (good component, Note E.17) and $J_B$'s
"$-igJ_B^{-a}$". The Feynman propagator is $-ig_{\mu\nu}\delta^{ab}/(k^2+i0)$ with $g_{+-}=1$. (The vertex sign is
opposite to the document's own (2.5) convention, which gives $+ig\gamma^\mu t^a$; harmless here because two
vertices appear; §8 E4.) An amplitude-side SS line in its window inserts, in the closed-time-path matrix element of
$J_A\otimes J_B$ at $\tau_A=0$, $\tau_B=0$:
$$-iV_G,\qquad V_G=\int d^2\mathbf b\,d^2\mathbf b'\,\rho_A^a(\mathbf b)\,\chi(\mathbf b-\mathbf b')\,\rho_B^a(\mathbf b'),\qquad
\chi(\mathbf b)=g^2\int\frac{d^2\mathbf k_T}{(2\pi)^2}\frac{e^{i\mathbf k_T\cdot\mathbf b}}{\mathbf k_T^2}\Theta(\mathbf k_T),\tag{E.32}$$
with $\rho_A^a(\mathbf b)=\int dx^-J_A^{+a}(0,x^-,\mathbf b)$ the transverse colour-charge density of $J_A$ at $\tau=0$,
restricted to spectators ($\rho_B$ likewise with $x^+$). $\Theta$ is the SS window, real (and even), so $\chi$ is
real. Caveat in the text: $\Theta$ depends via $\nu_A,\nu_B$ on all plus and transverse jet components (both
sides and on the cut), hence on the graph and the side assignment, and is not a $\tau=0$ Fock-state quantity.
Only where $\Theta=1$ (the window core) is $\chi$ a kernel in $\mathbf k_T$ alone; statements about diagonality and
side assignment hold strictly there (E.31(iii)). The conjugate side gives $+iV_G$. On a $\tau=0$ Fock state
$\rho_A^a(\mathbf b)=\sum_it_i^a\delta^{(2)}(\mathbf b-\mathbf b_i)$, $V_G$ is diagonal in transverse positions and
Hermitian ($\chi$ real, $[\rho_A^a,\rho_B^b]=0$). It is the operator form of (9.2) (where $\chi$ is given only up
to a constant, with opposite sign convention). $\Theta$ cuts off $\mathbf k_T\to0$, so $\chi$ is IR finite. SS
exchanges outside the window ($\mathbf k_T^2\lesssim\lambda^{-a_G}\nu_A\nu_B$) belong to $K_R$. (Normalization
checked: $(\mp ig)^2\cdot(-i)/(-\mathbf k_T^2)=-ig^2/\mathbf k_T^2$ ✔. It agrees with our
$V(k_T)=-ie_Ae_B/(\mathbf k_T^2+m_g^2)$, $\chi_{\rm ours}=-e_Ae_BK_0(m_gb)/(2\pi)$ with $e_Ae_B\to g^2T_A\cdot T_B$
and with Rothstein–Stewart's $\phi(b)$: $e^{-iV_G}=e^{i\phi}$ ✔.)

**Several SS lines: commuting vs non-commuting insertions.** With $N$ amplitude-side SS lines:
* Commuting case (different spectator partons on each jet's side, or abelian): localizing one line, all other
  insertions (at $\tau=0$ or $\tau'\neq0$) commute with it, because the two orders of vertices on different
  lines are separated by a zero-duration state and the integrand is continuous at equal times. So $\mathcal J$
  is continuous at $\tau=0$ in each insertion time, (J2) holds for each, and the two steps can be done line by
  line in any order, with additive errors.
* Non-commuting case (non-abelian, two insertions $O_1,O_2$ on the same parton on the same side, $t^at^b$ vs
  $t^bt^a$): $\mathcal J(\tau_1,\tau_2)$ jumps at $\tau_1=\tau_2$. (J2) fails for a single insertion ($J(k_1)$ has
  oscillating tails $e^{-ik_1\tau_2}[\mathcal J]/k_1$), so E.28 cannot be applied one at a time. Joint
  localization gives a weighted sum of the two orders with $w_{12}+w_{21}=1$ depending on both external factors.
  Example: exact single poles $z_j=\mathbf k_{Tj}^2/(2k_j^+)$, both in the LHP. Integrating
  $\prod_j\theta(\tau_j)e^{-iz_j\tau_j}$ over the ordering regions gives $w_{12}=z_1/(z_1+z_2)$; for $N$
  insertions, order $\pi$ has weight $\prod_jz_j/\prod_{m=1}^N\sum_{l\ge m}z_{\pi(l)}$. **(Verified, §6, C5;
  weights sum to 1.)** These $J_A$-side weights depend on $k_j^+$, a $J_B$ variable, so the orders in the two jets
  are correlated. With the strict approximation $G_j\to-1/\mathbf k_{Tj}^2$ and independent symmetric truncations
  in $k^-$ and $k^+$, each side's order appears independently with weight $\tfrac12$ (Lemma E.48(d)). The general
  joint localization is **not proved**; it is part of E.33(c). The same issue arises between an SS insertion and
  SA/AS localized insertions on the same parton.

**Definition E.30 (spectator-exchange family and integration map).** $(\Gamma_0,R_0)\in\mathcal G_0$ without SS
lines (the "core"). The family $\mathfrak F(\Gamma_0)$ of a given order is the set of forward graphs obtained by
adding any number of SS lines to $\Gamma_0$'s spectator partons (each on the amplitude or conjugate side, ends on
some A and some B spectator parton) up to the given order. For $\Gamma\in\mathfrak F(\Gamma_0)$, $R_\Gamma$ is
"$R_0$ plus all SS lines soft". A *connection* $p$ is a multiset of SS lines, each recording its partons
$(i_l,j_l)$; a *side assignment* gives each line a side.
$\Theta^{\rm SS}_\Gamma=\prod_{l\in{\rm SS}}\Theta_l$. $\mathfrak P_\Gamma$ integrates all four components of all
SS momenta in $\Theta^{\rm SS}_\Gamma\tilde F_{\Gamma,R_\Gamma}$, giving a function on $\Gamma_0$'s space. $p$ is
*coincident* if two localized insertions (SS, or SS with SA/AS on the same parton) sit on the same spectator
parton, on any sides.
Remainder: parts of $\Gamma\in\mathfrak F$ with some SS momentum outside its window ($\Theta_l<1$) do not enter
$\mathfrak P_\Gamma$. They are in $K_{R_\Gamma}$ (E.44(c)) or in $\Gamma$'s other regions (SS line collinear or
hard), handled by App. D's subtractions, with no SS Glauber pinch. An A-collinear SS line ($k^+\sim1$) attached
to B spectators is power suppressed by $J_B$'s tail $O(\nu_B/(k^+)^2)$.

**Proposition E.31 (cancellation of SS Glauber exchange)** (p. 196). Assume E.18 and E.34, $\mathfrak F(\Gamma_0)$
as in E.30, $J_A$ and $J_B$ cut-summed per E.26, and non-SS soft endpoints localized by (R2) or initial-only.
(i) (Localization) For non-coincident connections, on $\operatorname{supp}\Theta^{\rm SS}_\Gamma$, each SS line's
ends become equal-time insertions at $\tau=0$: $-iV_G$-terms (amplitude) and $+iV_G$-terms (conjugate), with
relative error of type (E.31) after $k_l^\mp$ fibre integration.
(ii) (Commutation) $\rho_A$ and $\rho_B$ act only on spectators and commute with $V_H$ and $J_B$'s hard vertex:
SS insertions before or after $V_H$ give the same result (E.27).
(iii) (Cancellation for non-coincident connections) For each non-coincident $p$ ($|p|\ge1$), summing over all
side assignments and cuts and then applying $\mathfrak P$ gives **zero on the window core** (points where all
SS windows of $p$ equal 1 under all side assignments), up to (i)'s error. Since $\Theta_l$ depends on the graph
and side via $\nu_A,\nu_B$, the side sum need not factorize at window edges. These "window-edge terms" lie in some
$K_{R_\Gamma}$ and are not treated (E.44(c)). In the abelian theory there is no coincidence problem:
$$\sum_{\Gamma\in\mathfrak F(\Gamma_0)}\mathfrak P_\Gamma\big[\Theta^{\rm SS}_\Gamma\tilde F_{\Gamma,R_\Gamma}\big]=\tilde F_{\Gamma_0,R_0}+(\text{error})+(\text{window-edge terms}),\tag{E.33}$$
with error a sum of fibre-integrated $O(\lambda^{a_G}L)$ relative errors, integrated over the rest by E.34.
(iv) (Coincident connections) In the non-abelian theory these are not covered by (i)(iii): localization needs
joint ordering weights, and the side sum need not vanish. For two lines attached to the same parton in both
jets, the $O(\chi^2)$ residue is $-\tfrac12C_A\chi^2(\operatorname{Re}p_{\rm opp})X$ (Ex. E.38), with
$p_{\rm opp}=\tfrac12$ in the strict window approximation (Lemma E.48(d)). Whenever the order allows two SS lines,
coincident connections occur in the family. [The segment ends here. Continuation on p. 197: if the core has an
SA/AS insertion on a parton, one SS line to it is already coincident ($[t_i^a,t_i^b]$ residue). So in the
non-abelian theory (E.33) holds only when all connections in the family are non-coincident (e.g. one SS line and
no localized core insertions on its partons), independently of E.33, still under E.18 and E.34. Otherwise (E.33)

## 8. ERRORS (verified), with page and correction

No sign error, wrong normalization or false theorem was found in this segment. All key formulas reproduce. The
issues below are gaps in proofs, imprecise statements and convention slips.

* **E1 (p. 186, (E.19)) — not an error in the PDF, a trap for readers of extracted text.** The text layer reads
  "= −1/(…)"; the rendered formula is $+1/[(k^--\alpha+i0)(k^--\beta-i0)]$, which is correct (sympy). Anyone
  quoting from `refdoc.txt` must use the image. (Likewise elsewhere: pdftotext detaches superscript minus signs.)
* **E2 (p. 193, proof of Lemma E.28(iii)) — gap.** The estimate of $\int ER_\downarrow$ uses
  $|E'|\lesssim M\lambda_J/\Lambda^3$ near real-axis poles $z_i$. But (J1)(J2) control $E$ only in the closed
  lower half-plane, so a derivative bound at real points does not follow from the hypotheses (it holds in
  LCOPT, where $J^{\rm fi}$ is rational with poles only at $|k|\lesssim\lambda_J$, but the lemma is stated
  abstractly). **Correct, shorter proof:** $E$ is analytic in the closed LHP and $O(|k|^{-2})$, and
  $R_\downarrow$ has only LHP poles. Closing in the LHP gives
  $\int\frac{dk}{2\pi}E\,R_\downarrow=-i\sum_{\operatorname{Im}z_i<0}r_iE(z_i)$, and by (J2)
  ($|z_i|\ge2\Lambda\ge16\lambda_J$) $|E(z_i)|\le3M\lambda_J/|z_i|^2$, so
  $\big|\int\frac{dk}{2\pi}ER_\downarrow\big|\le\frac{3M\lambda_J}{2\Lambda}\|R\|$. (Also $|E|\le2M\lambda_J/|k|^2$
  suffices instead of 3.) The statement (E.29) is correct (verified numerically).
* **E3 (p. 192, Lemma E.28(ii), last sentence; p. 193 proof) — imprecise.** "Independent of the window shape
  $[-K_1,K_2]$, $K_{1,2}\ge K$" needs also $K_{1,2}\le\Lambda/2$. The phrase "other estimates are the same"
  hides a path-length issue: for $K_2\gg K_1$ the arcs must be replaced by the semicircle of radius $K_1$ plus
  the real segment $[K_1,K_2]$, where (J2) holds on the real axis. The correct error is
  $O(\lambda_J/\min(K_1,K_2))$. For spectators ($\mathcal J_+=\mathcal J_-$) the $K^2/\Lambda^2$ term is in fact
  absent: $\oint\rho/k=0$ and the $1/k$ tails cancel. So the stated bound is valid but not sharp. Verified
  numerically (C4).
* **E4 (p. 195, before (E.32)) — convention slip.** "$J_A$'s leading coupling is $-igJ_A^{+a}$." With the
  document's own $D_\mu=\partial_\mu-igA_\mu^at^a$ (its eq. (2.5); vertex $+ig\gamma^\mu t^a$, also our binding
  convention) the coupling is $+igJ^{\mu a}A^a_\mu$. Harmless for $V_G$ (two vertices; $(\pm ig)^2=-g^2$), but
  wrong for one-vertex quantities (e.g. Wilson-line phases, gluonic-pole signs). Correct to $+ig$.
* **E5 (p. 194, (E.30)) — imprecise "relative" error.** $|e_j|\le C\lambda_J^A/\Lambda_j$ silently absorbs
  $M/|\mathcal J_A(0)|$ into $C$. Since $\mathcal J_A(0)$ can vanish or be small relative to $M$ (both carry
  $i0$ distributions), state it as an absolute bound:
  $\big|\int\frac{dk^-}{2\pi}J_AR-R(0)\mathcal J_A(0)\big|\le C'M\lambda_J^A|R(0)|/\Lambda_j$. (The document
  half-acknowledges this in the "标度" paragraph after (E.31).)
* **E6 (p. 182, status of E.18 (2)) — notation clash.** $\delta$ is used for "$|l^+|\ge\delta>0$" although p. 162
  reserves $\delta$ for the Glauber window width.
* **E7 (p. 181, Assumption E.18(iii)) — over-classification.** Hermiticity of the cut rules ($\alpha_i=\beta_i$)
  for three-gluon and ghost vertices is a standard consequence of the Hermiticity of the gauge-fixed Lagrangian
  under a suitable ghost conjugation convention (e.g. Kugo–Ojima's Hermitian $c,\bar c$ with
  $\mathcal L_{\rm FP}=-i\partial^\mu\bar c^a D_\mu^{ab}c^b$; the precise convention to be fixed in our App. J,
  **verify**), plus the fact that LCOPT on-shell numerators are the same polynomials on both sides of the
  forward graph. It is provable, not an assumption. Our App. J (BRST and ghosts in cut diagrams, TOC v1) should prove it. The document is honest that
  it did not check.
* **E8 (p. 165 and p. 172) — "(i) is the strongest form that can hold" (Thm E.8 remark).** Rhetorical. What is
  shown is that integral-norm statements are meaningless for $F_\Gamma$. Distributional statements (e.g. against
  smooth test functions in the remaining variables) are of the same strength and might be cleaner.
* **E9 (p. 195) — minor.** "$\Theta$ is real, so $\chi$ is real" also needs $\Theta(-\mathbf k_T)=\Theta(\mathbf k_T)$
  (true, since it depends on $\mathbf k_T^2$).
* **E10 (pp. 162–196) — citation gap.** The approach (hybrid LCPT: $x^+$-ordering for one jet and $x^-$ for the
  other; Glauber pinch replaced by a mismatch of deformation directions for initial and final states; cancellation

## 10. GAPS (missing or weak) and comparison with our material

1. **Non-abelian coincident insertions (E.33(c))** are the central unproven point. At orders allowing two SS
   lines on the same parton pair, the document proves *non*-cancellation in the strict window approximation
   ($p_{\rm opp}=\tfrac12$). Our material can do better at the [A] level:
   (a) the colour identity $X=L+(C_A/2)c$ (our c_glauber [U5a], re-verified in C5) shows that the residue is the
   crossed-ladder colour structure;
   (b) Rothstein–Stewart (2016; verified in c_glauber §1.6): with their rapidity regulator every crossed Glauber
   diagram vanishes, which in the reference's language corresponds to $p_{\rm opp}=0$ (my identification). The
   ladders exponentiate to $e^{i\phi(b)}$ with $\phi$ a Hermitian colour matrix, and the non-abelian part of the
   crossed topology is carried by the soft region (two soft gluons);
   (c) hence $\mathrm{Re}\,p_{\rm opp}$ is a scheme-dependent split between Glauber and soft regions, and E.33(c2)
   is the statement that the soft region (incl. real emission) absorbs it; unitarity $\mathrm{Tr}[U\rho U^\dagger]=\mathrm{Tr}\rho$
   (c_glauber [SS4a,b]) then gives the cancellation.
   Our Ch. 11 should present this chain (with grade [A], citing RS and CSS's "soft gluons cannot resolve the
   hadron" argument and Aybat–Sterman's "colour representation same in amplitude and conjugate"). The document
   has the RS comparison only in Note E.56 (outside this segment), without this resolution.
2. **The soft-angle region $K_R$** (incl. genuine soft-scale SS exchange, which is leading power and intersects
   $G_\delta$) is not treated (E.44(c)). So the proof covers only the window part of Glauber exchanges. This is a
   large hole, honestly declared. No literature closes it; we should keep it as a numbered open assumption.
3. **Window-edge terms** (E.31(iii)) arise because $\Theta_l$ depends on the side assignment through $\nu_A,\nu_B$.
   Possible improvement (my suggestion, unverified): build the window from side-independent quantities, e.g.
   $P_A^-$, the on-shell energies of the cut lines, and core-graph lines only. Then $\Theta$ is the same for all
   side assignments and the edge terms vanish identically.
4. **Compatibility (Def. E.7(d), E.44(b))** is a structural artefact of combining sharp region sets $\mathfrak B$
   with region-dependent deformations. Worth considering: a single global contour (as in D.7) with the rewrite
   done only inside windows that depend on undeformed variables, which would remove (d).
5. **LCOPT validity (E.18)**: zero modes, instantaneous loops, UV renormalization. The document cites only the
   review [132]. Missing literature (to be verified by the refs agent): Chang–Yan 1973 (light-front Feynman
   rules); Ligterink–Bakker, PRD 52 (1995) 5954 (equivalence of LF and covariant PT; minus integrals);
   Schoonderwoerd–Bakker 1998 (zero modes in triangle/vertex graphs); Collins arXiv:1801.03960 (non-triviality of
   the LF vacuum, an elementary treatment). E.18(iii) is provable (§8 E7).
6. **Integrable envelopes (E.34)**: the analytic core gap, parallel to D.7(A3). Not addressed; keep as an
   assumption.
7. **General soft subgraphs (E.33(a)(b)), H-emission endpoints (E.44(d)), one-jet soft branches (E.44(a))**: only
   $\mathcal G_0$ is really covered.
8. **External states are perturbative colour-singlet models** (point-like splitting vertices). Nothing here
   addresses non-perturbative hadrons (fine for "all orders in PT", but must be said in our status line).
   Coloured external states (DFT [51]) are excluded, and the power suppression of DFT effects
   (Frenkel–Gatheral–Taylor 1984) is not discussed here.
9. **No explicit numbers or IR-regulated formulas.** $\chi$ is regulated by a window $\Theta$, not by a gluon
   mass, and no Bessel-function form or numerical illustration is given. Our c_glauber/c_dy material
   ($\chi(b)=-e_Ae_BK_0(m_gb)/2\pi$, pointwise non-cancellation ≈30% vs exact cancellation after $\int d^2\Delta$)
   is more concrete and should be used as the worked example.
10. **No mention in this segment of the explicit higher-order checks** (Lindsay–Ross–Sachrajda 1982/83 two-loop
    spectator cancellation; Brandt–Frenkel–Taylor 1989), of Gaunt 2014 vs Schwartz–Yan–Zhu 2018 (pure Glauber
    ladders vs soft-gluon Lipatov-vertex graphs), or of Diehl–Gaunt–Ostermeier–Plößl–Schäfer 2016 (revisiting
    the single-DY Glauber argument while proving double DY). Some appear elsewhere in the reference (refs [61],
    [136], [137]); our c_glauber §1.6(3)(5) has them.
11. **Readability**: the appendix is extremely dense. Many statements carry long chains of caveats inline. For
    our monograph: state each lemma cleanly, collect caveats in a "status" remark, and give the one-gluon example
    first.
