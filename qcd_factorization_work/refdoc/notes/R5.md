### 1.1 Intro to App. C (pp. 88–89) and Fig. 54

* Aim: split "the OPE has been proven" into separately checkable statements. Prove what can be proven, state
  precise assumptions for the rest, and prove their Thm 5.3 (DIS moments) under those assumptions.
* Announced results:
  1. **PT, all orders, massive model theories.** The algebraic part of the OPE (chain expansion, Zimmermann
     identities, OPE identity for the bilocal insertion) and the convergence of every term are fully proved
     (Thm C.25(i)). The only external input is the Zimmermann–Hepp convergence theorem for compatible oversubtracted
     forests (Thm C.8, cited). The remainder estimate is an *explicitly listed input* (Assumption C.24). It is the
     momentum-space form of Zimmermann's short-distance theorem [18], which Zimmermann proved in *coordinate* space;
     the frozen-$q$ momentum-space form is "not verbatim in the literature" (Rem. C.9). Given C.24 they prove:
     derivative bounds, scaling and uniqueness of the coefficients, and the coordinate-space remainder
     (Thm C.25(ii)–(iv), Cor. C.33). For $a\ge d_A+d_B-1$ a polynomial-in-$x$ term remains unidentified (Rem. C.34).
     Massless QCD (Euclidean, non-exceptional) is cited (Thm C.35).
  2. **Gauge theory.** Renormalization of GI operators mixes only into "alien" operators (Joglekar–Lee; cited, with
     proof sketch), and aliens have zero physical matrix elements. The twist-2 GI classification is fully proved
     (Lemma C.42). Under an explicit "BRST-symmetric renormalization" assumption, the $J^\mu J^\nu$ OPE needs only GI
     and alien operators (Prop. C.44).
  3. **From local OPE to moments.** Fixed-$n$ moments need only the Euclidean local OPE (plus covariance/growth of
     coefficients, twist and chirality conditions), analyticity, $F_2\ge0$ and a $Q$-uniform finite-subtraction
     dispersion relation. No sum over all spins is needed (Thm C.50). The fixed-order all-spin sum follows from a
     forward remainder assumption (Prop. C.54).
  4. **Nonperturbative.** 4D QCD is not constructed (Wightman/OS), so unconditional proofs are beyond current
     mathematics. Under Assumptions C.56–C.60 the nonperturbative Thm 5.3 holds (Thm C.66). For even $n\ge n_0$
     ($n_0$ independent of $Q$) only C.56–C.59 are needed; $n<n_0$ (incl. the momentum sum rule $n=2$) also needs
     C.60. The x-space Thm 5.6 holds only weakly and under an extra assumption (Cor. C.52). Renormalon ambiguity:
     Prop. C.68, Rem. C.69.

### 1.2 §C.1 The OPE statement needed by §5 (pp. 89–91)

* $J^\mu=\sum_qe_q\bar\psi_q\gamma^\mu\psi_q$ with $d_J=3$, and
  **(C.1)** $\tilde T^{\mu\nu}(q)\equiv i\int d^4z\,e^{iq\cdot z}TJ^\mu(z)J^\nu(0)$, $T^{\mu\nu}(P,q)=\langle P|\tilde T^{\mu\nu}(q)|P\rangle_c$ (connected).
* Euclidean point: in the rest frame take complex $q=(iq^4,\mathbf q)$ with $q^4>0$, i.e. $q_E=(q^4,\mathbf q)$. Then
  $Q^2=|q_E|^2$ and ${\rm Im}\,q=(q^4,\mathbf 0)\in V_+$, which lies in the forward tube (retarded amplitude); $q^4<0$ uses the
  advanced amplitude. Here $\omega=2P\cdot q/Q^2=2iMq^4/|q_E|^2$ is purely imaginary, the point does not cross the
  cuts $|{\rm Re}\,\omega|\ge1$, and $|\omega|<1$ once $|q_E|>2M$.
* **Def. C.1 (the OPE statement used in §5).**
  - **(O1)** For each integer $N$ there are finitely many local $O_j$ ($d_j\le N$) and c-number $\tilde C_j^{\mu\nu}(q;\mu)$ with
    **(C.2)** $T^{\mu\nu}(P,q)=\sum_{d_j\le N}\tilde C^{\mu\nu}_j(q;\mu)\langle P|O_j(\mu)|P\rangle+O(|q|^{1-N}\ln^k|q|)$ as $|q|\to\infty$
    (Euclidean), uniformly in $q/|q|$.
  - **(O2)** Only GI operators are needed. The twist-2 GI operators are exactly $O_q^{(n)}$, $O_g^{(n)}$ of (5.3), (5.4).
  - **(O3)** With $T_2=\sum_nt_n(Q^2)\omega^{n-1}$ ($|\omega|<1$), for even $n\ge2$:
    **(C.3)** $t_n(Q^2)=4\sum_{i=q,g}C^{(n)}_{2,i}(Q^2/\mu^2,\alpha_s(\mu))A^{(n)}_i(\mu)+O(Q^{-2}\ln^kQ^2)$.
  - **(O4)** RG equations (5.9), $\gamma^{(n)}_{ij}$ a power series in $\alpha_s$, and (5.8).
  - **(O5)** There is a $Q$-independent even $n_0$ with $t_n=4\int_{[0,1]}x^{n-2}F_2\,dx$ for even $n\ge n_0$. Here $F_2dx$ is a positive measure on
    $[0,1]$, including the elastic point mass at $x=1$.
* **Rem. C.2.** The proof of Thm 5.3 formally treats (5.6) as a convergent series in $|\omega|<1$, i.e. a sum over all
  spins and an interchange with the OPE truncation. But a moment involves a *single* $t_n$; (O3)+(O5) give (5.7).
  §C.7 derives (O3) from (O1), (O2), covariance/growth, twist $\ge2$, chiral suppression, analyticity, $F_2\ge0$ and

* **Rem. C.3.** In PT the hadron is replaced by partons or off-shell legs, and (O1) by Green-function statements

  IR analysis (Rem. C.37). Partonic fixed-order "structure functions" are not positive measures (plus
  distributions, negative virtual terms, $1/\epsilon$ poles), so the positivity-based §C.7 applies only
  nonperturbatively. The PT analogue is Prop. C.54.

### 1.3 §C.2 Graphs, power counting, BPHZ forest formula (pp. 91–93)

Model: 4D Euclidean massive scalars (dim 1) and spinors (dim 3/2) with dimension-4 monomial interactions, so
everything is IR finite. Massless and gauge fields are deferred to §C.6 (cited).

* **Def. C.4** Graph $\Gamma$: vertex set $V$, internal lines $L$, external legs with incoming $p_j$. Vertex $v$ has
  dimension $d_v$ and $\delta_v$ derivatives ($d_v=4$ ordinary, $d_v=d_O$ composite). (a) A subgraph $\gamma$ is a subset of
  lines plus endpoints. $E(\gamma)$ = lines of $\Gamma$ attached to $\gamma$'s vertices but not in $\gamma$ (counted twice if both ends
  on $\gamma$), plus external legs at $\gamma$'s vertices. (b) 1PI = connected and stays connected after removing any one
  line ("renormalization part"). (c) Disjoint = no common vertex; non-overlapping = disjoint or nested (Zimmermann's
  convention). (d) $\Gamma/\gamma$ contracts each component of $\gamma$ to a point.
  Propagator $\Delta_\ell(k)=P_\ell(k)/(k^2+m_\ell^2)$, $\deg P_\ell=2d_\ell-2$ (scalar 1, spinor $m-i\slashed k$), large-$k$ power $2d_\ell-4$.
* **Def. C.5 (C.4)** $\omega(\gamma)=4L(\gamma)+\sum_{\ell\in\gamma}(2d_\ell-4)+\sum_{v\in V(\gamma)}\delta_v$.
* **Lemma C.6 (C.5)** $\omega(\gamma)=4-\sum_{e\in E(\gamma)}d_e+\sum_v(d_v-4)$. With one composite vertex, $\omega=d_O-\sum_Ed_e$. With two, $\omega=d_A+d_B-4-\sum_Ed_e$.
  Proof: $L=I-V+1$ and $\sum_v(d_v-4)=2\sum_\ell d_\ell+\sum_Ed_e+\sum_v\delta_v-4V$. **Checked.**
* Canonical (Zimmermann) routing: external momenta are distributed by Kirchhoff (minimal $\sum q_\ell^2$), which is
  compatible under nesting [78]. $p_\gamma$ denotes the independent external momenta of $\gamma$.
* **Def. C.7** (a) **(C.6)** $t^\delta_\gamma I_\gamma(k_\gamma,p_\gamma)=\sum_{|\alpha|\le\delta}\frac{p_\gamma^\alpha}{\alpha!}\partial^\alpha_{p_\gamma}I_\gamma(k_\gamma,0)$, with $t^\delta\equiv0$ for $\delta<0$.
  (b) Subtraction degrees $\delta(\gamma)\ge\omega(\gamma)$; a forest is a set of pairwise non-overlapping renormalization parts.
  (c) **(C.7)** $R_\Gamma=\sum_F\prod^{\curvearrowleft}_{\gamma\in F}(-t^{\delta(\gamma)}_\gamma)I_\Gamma$ (smaller subgraphs act first).
* **Fig. 55** (p. 92): (a) forest $\{\gamma_1,\gamma_2,\Gamma\}$, two disjoint one-loop bubbles in a 1PI two-point graph, giving the term
  $(-t_\Gamma)(-t_{\gamma_1})(-t_{\gamma_2})I_\Gamma$. (b) Two triangles sharing a rung overlap; their joint region is subtracted by $t_\Gamma$.
  (Note: the drawn graph has cubic vertices, outside the stated dimension-4 model; harmless.)
* **(C.8)** Bogoliubov recursion: $R_\Gamma=(1-t^{\delta(\Gamma)}_\Gamma)\bar R_\Gamma$, $\bar R_\Gamma=\sum_{\{\gamma_1..\gamma_s\}}I_{\Gamma/\{\gamma_j\}}\prod_j(-t^{\delta(\gamma_j)}_{\gamma_j}\bar R_{\gamma_j})$, summed over sets of disjoint proper renormalization parts.
* **Thm C.8 (Zimmermann convergence [78, 77, 17]).** Positive masses, real Euclidean external momenta. General
  integrands are allowed: line factors $P_\ell(k)/(k^2+m^2_\ell)^{n_\ell}$ and arbitrary polynomial vertices, with $\omega$ by actual degrees.
  **(C.9)** Vertex-additive degrees $\delta(\gamma)=\omega(\gamma)+\sum_{v\in V(\gamma)}c_v$, $c_v\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$. Then $\int\prod d^4k\,R_\Gamma$ converges absolutely, is
  smooth in external momenta, and the subtractions equal local counterterm insertions. $c_v\equiv0$ is standard BPHZ.
  [cited; grade R-cited]
* **Rem. C.9.** Sketch of Zimmermann's sector method. (C.9) cannot be weakened to $\delta\ge\omega$. Counterexample: in $\varphi^4$, a
  two-loop four-point $\Gamma$ with a bubble $\gamma$ at a vertex, $\omega(\gamma)=\omega(\Gamma)=0$, with $\delta(\gamma)=2$ and $\delta(\Gamma)=0$. Then $-t^2_\gamma$ produces $c_2P_\gamma^2$, and
  inserted into $\Gamma/\gamma$ this has degree 2, which a zeroth-order $t_\Gamma$ cannot cure. **Checked:** correct for both two-loop topologies
  (log divergence in the s-channel chain, quadratic in the "eye" graph). Zimmermann obtains the short-distance
  expansion from bilocal normal products, in **coordinate space**, with remainder $|x|^{a+1-d_A-d_B}\times$logs [17, 18, 79]. Here the
  bilocal subtraction acts only on the external momenta of AB parts, not on the large $q$ ("frozen $q$"). For
  convergence this reduces to Thm C.8 (Lemma C.22). For asymptotics they need a direction-uniform momentum-space
  Weinberg-type estimate on frozen-$q$ forest sums. They found no verbatim proof, so it becomes Assumption C.24.

### 1.4 §C.3 Normal products and Zimmermann identities (pp. 93–96)

* **Def. C.10.** For a monomial $O$ ($d_O$) and $a\ge d_O$, $a-d_O\in\mathbb Z$, $N_a[O]$ uses (C.7) with **(C.10)** $\delta_a(\gamma)=a-\sum_{E(\gamma)}d_e$ for
  $\gamma\ni V_O$, and $\delta=\omega$ otherwise. By Lemma C.6, $\delta_a-\omega=a-d_O$, i.e. (C.9) with $c_{V_O}=a-d_O$. $N[O]\equiv N_{d_O}[O]$ is minimal; $a>d_O$ oversubtracted.
* **Lemma C.11 (contraction and bridges).** For $\gamma^*\subset\sigma$ with $\gamma^*$ connected: $\sigma$ is connected ⇔ $\sigma/\gamma^*$ is connected; a line of $\sigma\setminus\gamma^*$ is a
  bridge of $\sigma$ ⇔ it is a bridge of $\sigma/\gamma^*$; a bridge of $\sigma$ lying in $\gamma^*$ is a bridge of $\gamma^*$. Hence for 1PI $\gamma^*$: $\sigma$ is 1PI ⇔ $\sigma/\gamma^*$ is 1PI. [R, elementary]
* **Lemma C.12 (chain expansion).** Fix marked vertices $M\neq\emptyset$ and a family $\mathcal S$ of connected subgraphs containing $M$.
  Admissible = in $\mathcal S$, or a renormalization part not containing all of $M$. Forests = non-overlapping admissible sets.
  (i) The $\mathcal S$-elements of a forest form a chain.
  (ii) With operators $\tau_\gamma,\tau'_\gamma$ on $\mathcal S$ and $t^{\delta(\gamma)}$ elsewhere:
  **(C.11)** $R^\tau_\Gamma=R^{\tau'}_\Gamma+\sum_{\gamma^*\in\mathcal S}\sum_{F\ni\gamma^*}\big[\prod^{\curvearrowleft}_{\gamma\in F,\gamma\not\subseteq\gamma^*}(-\tau_\gamma)\big](\tau'_{\gamma^*}-\tau_{\gamma^*})\big[\prod^{\curvearrowleft}_{\gamma\in F,\gamma\subsetneq\gamma^*}(-\tau'_\gamma)\big]I_\Gamma$.
  (iii) Under **(H)** (for $\sigma\supsetneq\gamma^*$: $\sigma$ admissible ⇔ $\sigma/\gamma^*$ is a renormalization part of $\Gamma/\gamma^*$ containing $v_*$), each forest $F\ni\gamma^*$
  splits uniquely as $F_{\rm in}\sqcup\{\gamma^*\}\sqcup F_{\rm out}$, with $F_{\rm out}$ in bijection with the forests of $\Gamma/\gamma^*$. The $\gamma^*$-term of (C.11) is the
  forest sum of $\Gamma/\gamma^*$ (with $\tau$ on subgraphs containing $v_*$), with vertex factor
  **(C.12)** $v_{\gamma^*}=(\tau'_{\gamma^*}-\tau_{\gamma^*})\bar R^{\tau'}_{\gamma^*}$, $\bar R^{\tau'}_{\gamma^*}=\sum_{F_{\rm in}}\prod_{\gamma\in F_{\rm in}}(-\tau'_\gamma)I_{\gamma^*}$.
  Proof: write $-\tau=-\tau'+(\tau'-\tau)$ along the chain and group by the innermost difference; routing compatibility
  under contraction [78]. For $M=\{V_O\}$, (H) follows from Lemma C.11. **Checked** the combinatorics and signs. [R]
* **Thm C.13 (Zimmermann identity [17, 79]).** For $d_O\le a<a'$ in $d_O+\mathbb Z$:
  **(C.13)** $N_a[O]=N_{a'}[O]+\sum_{O':\,a<d_{O'}\le a'}u_{O'}N_{a'}[O']$.
  $O'$ runs over local monomials. $u_{O'}$ are finite numbers given by zero-momentum Taylor coefficients of 1PI vertex
  functions of $N_a[O]$, of mass dimension $d_O-d_{O'}<0$: $u_{O'}=m^{d_O-d_{O'}}\hat u_{O'}$.
  Proof: Lemma C.12 with $\tau=t^{\delta_{a'}}$, $\tau'=t^{\delta_a}$. Then $v_{\gamma^*}=-\sum_{\delta_a<|\alpha|\le\delta_{a'}}\frac{p^\alpha}{\alpha!}\partial^\alpha_p\bar R^{(a)}_{\gamma^*}|_{p=0}$. These are convergent (no
  overall subtraction is needed: $\delta_a(\gamma^*)-|\alpha|<0$ and (C.9) still holds after differentiation). $p^\alpha$ times the legs $E(\gamma^*)$ is
  the vertex of $O'$ with $d_{O'}=\sum_Ed_e+|\alpha|\in(a,a']$, and in $\Gamma/\gamma^*$ the subtraction degrees are those of $N_{a'}[O']$. **Signs checked.** [R given C.8]
* **Cor. C.14.** $N_a[O]=\sum_{d_O\le d_{O'}\le a}z_{OO'}N[O']$, $z_{OO}=1$ (induction on $a-d_O$). [R]
* **Ex. C.15** ($\varphi^4$, $O=\varphi^2$, $a=2\to a'=4$): $m^2N_2[\varphi^2]=m^2N_4[\varphi^2]+\hat u_{\varphi^4}N_4[\varphi^4]+\hat u_{\varphi\partial^2\varphi}N_4[\varphi\partial^2\varphi]+\hat u_{(\partial\varphi)^2}N_4[\partial_\mu\varphi\partial^\mu\varphi]$
  ($\varphi^3$ excluded by $\mathbb Z_2$, $\varphi\partial\varphi$ by Lorentz). This is the Zimmermann identity behind the Callan–Symanzik equation [17, 120, 119].
  **Checked:** the coefficient of $N_{a'}[O]$ is exactly 1 in zero-momentum BPHZ, because $|\alpha|>\delta_a(\gamma^*)=a-d_O\ge0$.

### 1.5 §C.4 Weinberg power counting (pp. 96–97)

* **Def. C.16 (C.14).** $f(P,K)=\prod_jF_j(\ell_j(P,K))$, $F_j=P_j(\ell)/(\ell^2+m_j^2)^{n_j}$, $m_j>0$. For $S'\subset\mathbb R^{m+n}$: $\alpha_{S'}(F_j)=r_j-2n_j$ if $\ell_j|_{S'}\not\equiv0$, else 0.

  $\alpha(L)=\max\{D_{S'}:\pi(S')=L\}$. Then **(C.16)** $|I(\eta\hat\ell+c)|\le K\eta^{\alpha(L)}(\ln\eta)^\beta$ for $\eta\ge2$, uniformly for $\hat\ell$ in compacta of the unit sphere of $L$ and $c$ in compacta.

* **Cor. C.18 (C.17).** $\alpha(L)\le\max\{\omega(\gamma):\gamma\text{ carries }L\}$. Proof via $S'_\gamma=\{P\in L,\ \ell_j=0\ \forall j\notin\gamma\}$ and $\dim(S'_\gamma\cap\ker\pi)=4L(\gamma)$.
  **Checked.** (Minor: the maximizing $\gamma$ may be disconnected, and Def. C.5 defines $\omega$ only for connected $\gamma$.)
* Thm C.17 cannot be applied term by term to (C.7). Individual forest terms are not integrable and grow faster than
  the sum; they recombine only sector by sector. For standard BPHZ (zero-momentum subtractions),

### 1.6 §C.5 OPE at every order of PT (pp. 97–105)

* Setup: $A,B$ monomials, $D\equiv d_A+d_B-4$. Connected amputated $\tilde G_{AB}(q;p)$ of $TA(x)B(0)$ with $n$ elementary fields; $q$ enters at $V_A$,
  and $q+\sum p_j$ leaves at $V_B$. An auxiliary "x-line" $\ell_x$ ($V_A$–$V_B$, "propagator" $e^{-iq\cdot x}$) gives $\Gamma^\circ=\Gamma\cup\{\ell_x\}$. Everything is order by order.
* **Def. C.19 (AB part).** $V_A,V_B\in\gamma$ and $\gamma\cup\{\ell_x\}$ 1PI in $\Gamma^\circ$; $q$ is a loop momentum of $\gamma\cup\ell_x$. **(C.18)** $\omega(\gamma)=D-\sum_{E(\gamma)}d_e$. Renormalization parts
  containing both vertices are AB parts; the converse fails (a single $V_A$–$V_B$ propagator is an AB part, and is the mechanism of
  Taylor-expanding $e^{ip\cdot x}$; Ex. C.26). The parameter $a\in D+\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$.
* **Lemma C.20.** With $M=\{V_A,V_B\}$ and $\mathcal S$ = AB parts, admissible subgraphs = AB parts + renormalization parts not containing both
  vertices. (H) holds (Lemma C.11 applied to $\gamma^*\cup\ell_x\subset\sigma\cup\ell_x$). $q$ does not flow in $\Gamma/\gamma^*$ ($\ell_x$ becomes a self-loop). [R]
* **Def. C.21 (two subtractions).** (a) T-product: standard BPHZ with $q$ external. 1PI AB parts use $t^{\omega(\gamma)}_{(p,q)}$; non-1PI AB parts are not
  subtracted. Result $\tilde G^T_{AB}$. (b) $N_a[A(x)B(0)]$: every AB part (incl. non-1PI) uses $t^{(a)}_\gamma\equiv t^{\delta_a(\gamma)}_p$, $\delta_a=a-\sum_Ed_e$ (Taylor in $p$ only, $q$ frozen);
  others use $t^\omega$. Result $\tilde N_a(q;p)$. $\delta_a-\omega=a-D\ge0$. Not of the form (C.9), so it is not directly covered by Thm C.8.
* **Lemma C.22 ($N_a$ well-defined; chain decomposition).** With $\tau=t^{(a)}$, $\tau'=t^{\omega}_{(p,q)}$ (1PI) or $0$ (non-1PI), $\vartheta$ = 1PI indicator:
  (i) **(C.19)** $R^T_\Gamma=R^{(a)}_\Gamma+\sum_{\gamma^*\in\mathcal S}(\text{forest sum of }\Gamma/\gamma^*\text{ with }t^{(a)}\text{ on subgraphs}\ni v_*)[v_{\gamma^*}]$, $v_{\gamma^*}=(t^{(a)}_{\gamma^*}-\tau'_{\gamma^*})\bar R^T_{\gamma^*}$.
  (ii) **(C.20)** $v_{\gamma^*}=\sum_{|\alpha|\le\delta_a(\gamma^*)}\frac{p^\alpha_{\gamma^*}}{\alpha!}\hat c^{\gamma^*}_\alpha(k_{\gamma^*};q)$, $\hat c^{\gamma^*}_\alpha=\big(1-\vartheta(\gamma^*)t_q^{\omega(\gamma^*)-|\alpha|}\big)\partial^\alpha_p\bar R^T_{\gamma^*}(k;q,p)|_{p=0}$. Each $\hat c$ is absolutely integrable,
  and the integral $c^{\gamma^*}_\alpha(q)$ is smooth. For 1PI $\gamma^*$, $\hat c$ is the full BPHZ integrand of $\gamma^*$ (external momentum $q$ only, degree $\omega-|\alpha|$). For non-1PI
  $\gamma^*$, it is the product of fully subtracted 1PI blobs and bridge propagators.

### 1.7 §C.6 QCD: massless fields, BRST, GI operators (pp. 106–112)

* In massless theories the Taylor coefficients $\partial^\alpha_p\bar R_{\gamma^*}(q;0)$ are IR divergent (e.g. $\partial^\alpha(k+p)^{-2}|_0\sim|k|^{-2-|\alpha|}$). Two rigorous frameworks:
  (a) **BPHZL** [80, 81]: auxiliary mass $(s-1)M$; UV operator $t^{\delta(\gamma)}_{p,s}$ at $p=0,s=0$, IR operator $t^{\rho(\gamma)-1}_{p,s-1}$ at $p=0,s=1$, then $s=1$. Convergence for
  non-exceptional momenta (no proper partial sum of external momenta vanishes) and IR power counting (Lowenstein–Zimmermann).
  (b) **DR+MS**: local pole counterterms, action principle and normal products [83, 86]; IR counterterms ($R^*$) for coefficient functions [123, 124];
  OPE in MS [87]; asymptotic expansion theorems [85]. The two schemes differ by finite renormalizations.
* **Thm C.35 (OPE in massless QCD; cited [87, 85, 80, 81]).** $n_f$ massless quarks, covariant gauge, MS, Euclidean non-exceptional $p,q$. At each order:
  (a) there are $\tilde C_O(q;\mu)$ (basis incl. ghost and gauge-variant operators) with $\tilde G^T_{AB}-\sum_{d_O\le a}\tilde C_O\tilde G_{[O]_\mu}=O(|q|^{D-1-a}\ln^k)$, uniformly in direction and in compact

## 3. ERRORS (verified or plausible, with page and correction)

**E2 (p. 108–109, Lemma C.41(a) and Table 2; over-claim, PLAUSIBLE).** The claim is that EOM operators have zero matrix elements between on-shell partonic states **"including
forward"**, because "each term on the right has no single-particle pole in leg $k$". Two problems:
(i) At zero momentum transfer, the contact term $\langle TX(y_k)\prod_{l\neq k}\phi_l\rangle$ depends on $p_k+\Delta=p_k$ and does carry the single-particle pole in the *same* variable through the
other leg. Vanishing then follows only from counting (two LSZ factors $(p^2-m^2)^2$ against at most one pole), so the stated reason is imprecise.
(ii) For massless on-shell gluons at zero momentum transfer in DR, the naive argument is known to fail through IR singularities. This is the Hamberg–van Neerven
puzzle, resolved by Collins–Scalise 1994: "subtle IR singularities of gluonic matrix elements taken on shell at zero momentum transfer" (our SURVEY item 1). The paper is the
document's own [108], cited only as a Joglekar–Lee reference.
*Correction:* state Lemma C.41 for non-zero momentum transfer or with an IR regulator (massive or off-shell external legs, then the limit). For forward on-shell partonic matrix
elements, add a caveat and cite Collins–Scalise 1994 and Hamberg–van Neerven, Nucl. Phys. B379 (1992) 143. Modern alien-operator results (Falcioni–Herzog 2022;
Gehrmann–von Manteuffel–Yang 2023; flagged "(verify)" in our BLUEPRINT T2.18) belong here too. The grade "proved" in Table 2 should become "proved for
off-forward/IR-regulated states".

**E3 (p. 118, Assumption C.56 (W2); CONFIRMED).** "The spectrum of the baryon-number-1 sector is $\subset\{p^2=M^2\}\cup\{p^2\ge M_1^2\}$ with $M_1>M$, $M$ the isolated lowest mass (nucleon)." This is

(a) In the chiral limit (massless quarks, used throughout §C.6 and Rem. C.48) pions are massless, the $N\pi$ continuum starts at $M$, and there is no gap. So (W2) silently requires

(b) For $m_u\ne m_d$ (or with QED) the $B=1$ sector contains both $p$ and $n$ below the $N\pi$ threshold, so the stated two-piece spectrum is false.
*Correction:* formulate (W2) in the superselection sector carrying the **target's conserved flavour numbers** (the intermediate states of $\langle P|JJ|P\rangle$ lie there, since $J$ conserves

## 5. GAPS (missing or weak in the segment)

1. **The analytic heart is assumed.** Even in the massive model the remainder estimate (C.24) is an assumption with only a heuristic (Rem. C.28). So App. C proves the
   *algebraic* OPE, not the asymptotic statement. The literature check (Zimmermann's coordinate-space theorem plus Lemma C.32 in reverse; Anikin–Zav'yalov; Zav'yalov 1990; Bergère–de
   Calan–Malbouisson; Smirnov) was not completed (E4).
2. **QCD is only cited.** Thm C.35, the main target, rests on [87, 85, 80, 81]. The appendix's own proofs cover only massive scalar/Yukawa models. (O2) in PT needs the extra
   assumptions (B1)(B2); the relevant literature, Joglekar 1977 and Hollands 2008, is uncited (E5).
3. **Forward kinematics.** The exceptional-momentum / on-shell forward gap is identified but not attacked. Possible routes: off-forward limits $p'\to p$ with uniformity; spacelike off-shell
   partons with BPHZL IR power counting; DR large-momentum theorem for $(p,-p)$.
4. **Alien operators.** No discussion of Hamberg–van Neerven / Collins–Scalise (zero-momentum-transfer IR subtleties) or of the modern explicit alien counterterms for twist-2
   gluon operators (Falcioni–Herzog; Gehrmann–von Manteuffel–Yang) (E2).
5. **Only $F_2$/$T_2$.** No $T_1/F_1$, $F_L$, $F_3$, polarized $g_1$ (odd $n$, Bjorken sum rule, axial anomaly), all covered in TOC v1 Ch. 6. Target-mass corrections (Nachtmann,
   Georgi–Politzer) and twist-4 structure are lumped into $O(M^2/Q^2)$. No explicit one-loop Wilson coefficient or anomalous dimension in App. C.
6. **Moment problem.** Only measures (Lemma C.51); no treatment of distributions with support at $x=0$ (the $\delta'$ counterexample, our L2 / BLUEPRINT L5.14). No Carlson/analytic
   continuation in $n$ ("+" vs "−" continuations; our O6/L4).
7. **No light-ray OPE.** Balitsky–Braun and Müller are absent; the light-ray ↔ local moment identity (our App. L) is not here. Only Rem. C.55 touches the light-cone expansion.
8. **Massive vs massless.** The nonperturbative framework needs massive quarks ((W2) gap, C.57(b) mass mixing), while the coefficients are massless-MS (C.59). The bridge (mass
   corrections $m_q^2/Q^2$ as power corrections, chiral limit) is not discussed (E3).
9. **$e^+e^-$ timelike side.** Rem. C.62 covers only the Euclidean Adler function. Nothing on continuation to $s>0$, PQW smearing or duality violations (our BLUEPRINT P4.15/R4.16). These may be
   in the main text §4.
10. **IR finiteness at non-exceptional momenta.** The simple Poggio–Quinn (1976) proof for gauge theories and our explicit IR-degree count (BLUEPRINT T4.6) are not used. BPHZL is invoked
    abstractly.
11. **Minor technical gaps** E7–E10 (disconnected $\omega$, routing for frozen $q$, slice edge-of-the-wedge, mass-mixing norm).
