### Lessons (`sec:summary-lessons`, ≈2 pp)
1. Rigour is local: the tag belongs to a statement, not to a process; the same process has [R] (PT moments), [R, R_cond] (non-perturbative moments), [A] ($x$ space) statements.
2. Moment space vs $x$ space: moments determine $x$-space objects only as distributions; the OPE gives weak convergence, never pointwise $O(1/Q^2)$ (`rem:dis-nonuniform`, `cor:dis-weak-xspace`).
3. Inclusivity in the spectator final state is a theorem per observable: the measurement must commute with the light-cone evolution (`prop:break-measurement`).
4. Soft factors and rapidity: only properly combined objects are regulator independent; Euclidean realizations of TMD soft physics (Ch23).
5. EFT and graphical proofs are complementary; neither supersedes the other's hypotheses (`rem:scet-assumptions`).
6. **Rigour statements are about grouped expressions, never single terms** (refdoc S44): single chain terms need not be integrable (`ex:method-naive-induction`, `ex:disall-ladder`).
7. Scaling statements are not bounds: the gap between E-S and E-N is exactly what the technical assumptions of App N/O formalize; the per-order exponents of the general method can be far from sharp (`rem:intro-sharpness`).
8. Conditional theorems with numbered assumptions make the frontier of knowledge precise: every [A] grade is traced to named `asm:` entries; every upgrade path is a list of assumptions to discharge.

### P.7 Generator specification (for the integrator; not printed)

**Program.** `spec_v2/checks/blueprint_gen.py` (to be written by the integrator), Python 3 standard library only, run as `python3 -I spec_v2/checks/blueprint_gen.py --root <book dir> --master master.tex --aux master.aux --outline spec_v2/FINAL_OUTLINE_v2.md --register spec_v2/ASSUMPTIONS.md --out build/appP --level <0..3> [--final]`. Deterministic output (sorted keys, fixed float formatting); exit code 0 if no check of severity E fails, 1 otherwise; `--final` turns C-P13 into an error.

**Inputs and parsing.**
1. *Unit order:* the `\include`/`\input` sequence of `master.tex` (front, ch01…ch25, appA…appQ); file → unit key by the registry headings of FINAL_OUTLINE_v2 (`### ChNN`, `### AppX`, `### front`) and WRITER_SPEC_v2 §1.1.
2. *Comments:* strip `%`-comments, except marker lines kept verbatim: `% uses: none`, `% errtype: E-S|E-P|E-F|E-N|E-Nphi|E-W` (the pending-verification comment type is retired by the verify patch; C-P13).
3. *Environments:* `\begin{definition|assumption|theorem|lemma|proposition|corollary|conjecture|example|remark}` with an optional title `[...]` parsed with **balanced** brackets and braces (titles contain `$…$`, `\cref{…}`, nested brackets); the first `\label{…}` inside the environment (warning if not within the first three lines); environment type and printed type from the environment name.
4. *Tag:* exactly one of `\tagR \tagA \tagP \tagC \tagV` in the title, or none (definitions, pure mathematics, untagged remarks/examples, convention assumptions); more than one is an error.
5. *Qualifier* (for `\tagR`): the first sentence of the body compared with the four verbatim strings of WRITER_SPEC_v2 §4.3 after normalizing `–`→`--` and whitespace; the short $R_{\rm OPE}$ form is accepted after the first long form in the same unit.
6. *Premise sentence* ($R_{\rm cond}$): the sentence beginning with 「前提：」 that follows the qualifier; every label inside `\cref{…}`/`\Cref{…}` (comma lists, including the parenthetical 「（及 …）」) is collected; inner item suffixes such as `(T2)` after a `\cref` are ignored for the graph and kept as text.
7. *`\uses{…}`:* exactly one per environment of types def/asm/lem/prop/thm/cor/conj (zero allowed only with `% uses: none`); for tagged remarks and for examples: at most one. Comma-separated labels; whitespace trimmed; only the prefixes `def thm lem prop cor conj rem ex asm` are allowed (others → error C-P1).
8. *Lines:* 「非微扰地位：」 with its `\cref` labels; the partonic line 「（本…只涉及部分子态。）」 (flag).
9. *Status paragraph:* after `\end{…}` and an immediately following `proof`/`proofsketch`, the next paragraph starting with `\paragraph{地位与缺口}` or 「\textbf{地位与缺口：}」; the labels it `\cref`s are recorded.
10. *Location:* the last `\section`/`\subsection` label before the environment; source file and line.
11. *Printed numbers and pages* from `master.aux`: `\newlabel{<label>}{{<number>}{<page>}{<title>}{<anchor>}{}}` (hyperref form; the `@cref` duplicates are ignored).
12. *Registry and register:* owners and tags from the FINAL_OUTLINE_v2 registry (same parser as `check_unit_v2.py`: headings `### ChNN`/`### AppX`/`### front`/`### Assumptions`, rows whose first cell is a backticked label); the assumption data (owner, tag, one-line content, status, users, upgrade path) from the summary table of `ASSUMPTIONS.md` §1.
13. *Master table:* rows of `tab:summary-master` in `ch25_summary.tex` (label column, tag column, 瓶颈 column).

**Data model** (`blueprint.json`). Node: `{label, type, unit, unit_index, file, line, section, title, tag, qualifier, premises, uses, np_line, partonic, status_paragraph, status_labels, errtype, pending, number, page}`. Edges: $p\to S$ for $p\in$ `uses`(S) (premise-sentence labels are a subset by check C-P8). #### `asm:appM-brst-ope` — OPE 的 BRST 结构：带一个复合旁观者的欧氏展开 (Eε) [\tagA]
- **所属：** App M §M.6 (`sec:appM-brst`). Technical. (Reworded in v2.3 after the X3 verification; the label is kept.)
- **陈述：** Massless QCD in bare dimensional regularization at fixed generic $\varepsilon$ (any gauge parameter $\xi_0$, $B$ off shell, no $\gamma_5$, $D$-dimensional algebra). **(B2) ≡ (Eε):** for one composite spectator $Z=s\phi$ ($\phi$ an elementary field) and products $\Phi$ of elementary fields, the large-$q$ expansion $\langle\mathcal J(q)\,Z\,\Phi\rangle=\sum_{d_O\le a}C_O\langle O\,Z\,\Phi\rangle+\sum_{O'}C^Z_{O'}(q;p_Z)\langle O'\,\Phi\rangle+\mathcal R'$ holds, with the coefficients $C_O$ of the expansion without $Z$ (`prop:dis-ope-euclid`), **merged coefficients** $C^Z_{O'}$ (hard subgraphs containing the $Z$ vertex) polynomial in $p_Z$, all coefficients graded-homogeneous, and $\mathcal R'=O(|q|^{1-a}\ln^k|q|)$. **(B1)** is retired (the name is not reused): its Ward identities hold exactly in DR and its contact-term clause is not needed. The earlier form of (B2), without merged terms, is not the correct generalization of the Euclidean expansion: the merged terms turn $sX_d=0$ into $sX_d\in\mathcal E$ (`thm:appM-brst-reduction`).
- **地位：** 开放（v2.3）：槽位 X3d 部分完成——在 (Eε) 下，物理矩阵元只需规范不变算符这一化归已证明（`thm:appM-brst-reduction`，[R, R_cond]；形式上的定域 BRST 上同调 `lem:appM-lbc-formal`）；(Eε) 本身是带一个复合旁观者的逐图维数正规化欧氏展开，未证明。Joglekar 1977 只在摘要层次读过；Hollands 2008 与 Fröb–Holland 2016（预印本）覆盖纯 Yang–Mills，其对含夸克 QCD 的适用范围未核实（S63-3）。
