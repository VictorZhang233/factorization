## Chapter 7 — 严格核心 II：深度非弹散射与算符乘积展开 / Rigorous core II: DIS and the operator product expansion

`\label{ch:dis}` · `ch07_dis.tex` · **18 pp** · full (complete proofs of the non-perturbative layer and of the OPE machinery in App M; uniqueness lemmas in App K) · Part II

**Purpose.** Moment factorization of DIS structure functions, along **two proof routes** (refdoc S18): (I) partonic PT — analyticity and crossing of the forward Compton amplitude, the dispersion relation identifying the Taylor coefficients at $\omega=0$ with moments, the Euclidean OPE split into its three sub-statements (Euclidean graph-by-graph expansion, reduction to gauge-invariant operators, forward on-shell partonic matrix elements) with separate tags, RG; (II) hadrons, non-perturbative and conditional — the Euclidean OPE informs only on a segment of length $O(M/Q)$ of the imaginary $\omega$ axis, so fixed-$n$ moments need positivity and a $Q$-uniform finite-subtraction dispersion relation proved from the Wightman axioms; only the lowest moments (incl. the momentum sum rule) need a genuine growth assumption. Then: moments ⇒ $x$ space only in the sense of distributions (weak convergence; pointwise remainder not controlled; counterexample); light-cone OPE as the $x$-space structure ([A]); twist classification and chirality; higher twist and target mass; polarized moments (Bjorken sum rule). The $x$-space theorem with operator-defined PDFs is completed in Ch8 (`thm:pdf-dis-xspace`).

**Depends on.** Ch2 (`thm:prelim-composite-renorm`, `prop:prelim-rg`, `prop:qcd-rge`, `thm:qcd-renormalizability`, `prop:prelim-beta`), Ch3 (`prop:prelim-optical`, Mellin, `lem:prelim-boost`, `lem:prelim-scaleless`, `def:prelim-gamma5`), Ch4 (`def:ir-landau-setup`), Ch5 (`prop:sudakov-oneloop`), Ch6 (methodological model; `thm:ee-euclid-ir`); App M, App K, App I.

#### `asm:appM-brst-ope` — OPE 的 BRST 结构：带一个复合旁观者的欧氏展开 (Eε) [\tagA]
- **所属：** App M §M.6 (`sec:appM-brst`). Technical. (Reworded in v2.3 after the X3 verification; the label is kept.)
- **陈述：** Massless QCD in bare dimensional regularization at fixed generic $\varepsilon$ (any gauge parameter $\xi_0$, $B$ off shell, no $\gamma_5$, $D$-dimensional algebra). **(B2) ≡ (Eε):** for one composite spectator $Z=s\phi$ ($\phi$ an elementary field) and products $\Phi$ of elementary fields, the large-$q$ expansion $\langle\mathcal J(q)\,Z\,\Phi\rangle=\sum_{d_O\le a}C_O\langle O\,Z\,\Phi\rangle+\sum_{O'}C^Z_{O'}(q;p_Z)\langle O'\,\Phi\rangle+\mathcal R'$ holds, with the coefficients $C_O$ of the expansion without $Z$ (`prop:dis-ope-euclid`), **merged coefficients** $C^Z_{O'}$ (hard subgraphs containing the $Z$ vertex) polynomial in $p_Z$, all coefficients graded-homogeneous, and $\mathcal R'=O(|q|^{1-a}\ln^k|q|)$. **(B1)** is retired (the name is not reused): its Ward identities hold exactly in DR and its contact-term clause is not needed. The earlier form of (B2), without merged terms, is not the correct generalization of the Euclidean expansion: the merged terms turn $sX_d=0$ into $sX_d\in\mathcal E$ (`thm:appM-brst-reduction`).

#### `asm:appM-remainder` — 冻结 $q$ 双局域正规乘积的动量空间余项估计 [\tagA]
- **所属：** App M §M.4 (`sec:appM-remainder`). Technical.
- **陈述：** Massive four-dimensional Euclidean model of §M.2 (scalars and Dirac fermions, dimension-4 monomial interactions), at each order.
