### M.2 `sec:appM-bphz` BPHZ 与正规乘积 (2.5 pp)

Model of §M.2–§M.4: four-dimensional Euclidean theories of massive scalars (dimension 1) and Dirac fermions (dimension 3/2) with dimension-4 monomial interactions; composite vertices of dimension $d_O$ with $\delta_v$ derivatives; everything IR finite. Massless and gauge fields: §M.5–§M.6.

- **定义 `def:appM-bphz` — 图、表观发散度、森林与 $R$ 运算** (no tag). Graph $\Gamma$ (vertices $V$, internal lines $L$, external legs with incoming $p_j$); subgraph $\gamma$ = set of lines with their endpoints; $E(\gamma)$ = lines of $\Gamma$ attached to vertices of $\gamma$ but not in $\gamma$ (counted twice if both ends lie on $\gamma$) plus external legs at vertices of $\gamma$; renormalization part = 1PI subgraph; non-overlapping = disjoint or nested; $\Gamma/\gamma$ = contraction of each component of $\gamma$ to a point. Propagator $\Delta_\ell(k)=P_\ell(k)/(k^2+m_\ell^2)$, $\deg P_\ell=2d_\ell-2$. Superficial degree $\omega(\gamma)=4L(\gamma)+\sum_{\ell\in\gamma}(2d_\ell-4)+\sum_{v\in V(\gamma)}\delta_v$ (additive extension to disconnected $\gamma$: sum over components). Taylor operator $t^\delta_\gamma I_\gamma(k_\gamma,p_\gamma)=\sum_{|\alpha|\le\delta}\frac{p_\gamma^\alpha}{\alpha!}\partial^\alpha_{p_\gamma}I_\gamma(k_\gamma,0)$, $t^\delta\equiv0$ for $\delta<0$; Zimmermann's canonical (Kirchhoff) routing of external momenta; forest formula $R_\Gamma=\sum_F\prod^{\curvearrowleft}_{\gamma\in F}(-t^{\delta(\gamma)}_\gamma)I_\Gamma$ (smaller subgraphs act first) with subtraction degrees $\delta(\gamma)\ge\omega(\gamma)$; Bogoliubov recursion $R_\Gamma=(1-t_\Gamma)\bar R_\Gamma$. `% uses: none`.
- **引理 `lem:appM-degree` — 表观发散度的外线公式** (数学). $\omega(\gamma)=4-\sum_{e\in E(\gamma)}d_e+\sum_{v\in V(\gamma)}(d_v-4)$; with one composite vertex $\omega=d_O-\sum_{E}d_e$, with two composite vertices $A,B$: $\omega=d_A+d_B-4-\sum_Ed_e$. `\uses{def:appM-bphz}`. Proof: full ($L=I-V+1$, $\sum_v(d_v-4)=2\sum_\ell d_\ell+\sum_Ed_e+\sum_v\delta_v-4V$).
- **定理 `thm:appM-zimmermann` — Zimmermann 收敛定理（顶点可加的超减除）** [R, R_PT]. （$R_{\rm PT}$ …）（本定理只涉及部分子态。）Positive masses, real Euclidean external momenta, general line factors $P_\ell(k)/(k^2+m_\ell^2)^{n_\ell}$ and polynomial vertices ($\omega$ by actual degrees). If the subtraction degrees are **vertex-additive**, $\delta(\gamma)=\omega(\gamma)+\sum_{v\in V(\gamma)}c_v$ with $c_v\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$ fixed per vertex, then $\int\prod d^4k\,R_\Gamma$ converges absolutely, is smooth in the external momenta, and the subtractions are equivalent to local counterterm insertions. $c_v\equiv0$ is standard BPHZ. `\uses{def:appM-bphz}`. Proof: cited — `Zimmermann1969` (convergence of the forest formula in momentum space; canonical momenta), `Zimmermann1970` (oversubtractions, normal products), `Hepp1966`, `BogoliubovParasiuk1957` (locations: 「位置待核实」 for theorem numbers). Error type: exact (absolute convergence).
- **例 `ex:appM-nonadditive` — 非顶点可加的超减除会失败**. $\varphi^4$, two-loop four-point $\Gamma$ with a one-loop bubble $\gamma$ at a vertex, $\omega(\gamma)=\omega(\Gamma)=0$; choose $\delta(\gamma)=2$, $\delta(\Gamma)=0$ (so $\delta\ge\omega$ but not vertex-additive): $-t^2_\gamma$ produces $c_2P_\gamma^2$, which inserted into $\Gamma/\gamma$ has degree 2, not cured by $t^0_\Gamma$; both two-loop topologies (s-channel chain: logarithmic; "eye": quadratic divergence). Moral: the condition of `thm:appM-zimmermann` cannot be weakened to $\delta\ge\omega$. `\uses{thm:appM-zimmermann, lem:appM-degree}`. (Re-derived; reader check R5.)
- **定理 `thm:appM-weinberg` — Weinberg 定理（Hahn–Zimmermann 形式）** (数学，经典，引用). For $f(P,K)=\prod_jF_j(\ell_j(P,K))$, $F_j=P_j(\ell)/(\ell^2+m_j^2)^{n_j}$, $m_j>0$, with $\alpha_{S'}(F_j)=r_j-2n_j$ if $\ell_j|_{S'}\not\equiv0$ else 0, $D_{S'}(f)=\dim(S'\cap\ker\pi)+\sum_j\alpha_{S'}(F_j)$: (a) if $D_S<0$ for all non-zero $S\subset\ker\pi$, $I(P)=\int dK\,f$ converges absolutely; (b) with $\alpha(L)=\max\{D_{S'}:\pi(S')=L\}$, $|I(\eta\hat\ell+c)|\le K\eta^{\alpha(L)}(\ln\eta)^\beta$ for $\eta\ge2$, uniformly for $\hat\ell$ in compacta of the unit sphere of $L$ and $c$ in compacta. `% uses: none`. Proof: cited (`Weinberg1960`, `Hahn:1968HZ`).
- **推论 `cor:appM-weinberg-degree` — 渐近指数与子图发散度** (数学). $\alpha(L)\le\max\{\omega(\gamma):\gamma\text{ carries }L\}$, where $\gamma$ may be disconnected and $\omega$ is the additive extension (corrects the gap ERR-m24/R5 E7). `\uses{thm:appM-weinberg, def:appM-bphz}`. Proof: full ($S'_\gamma=\{P\in L,\ \ell_j=0\ \forall j\notin\gamma\}$, $\dim(S'_\gamma\cap\ker\pi)=4L(\gamma)$).
- **注 `rem:appM-forest-asymptotics` — 森林公式不能逐项用 Weinberg 定理** (no tag). Single forest terms are not integrable and grow faster than the sum; they recombine sector by sector (Zimmermann's sector method). Asymptotic expansions of zero-momentum BPHZ integrals: `Bergere:1977wz`; large-momentum and small-mass expansions `Smirnov:1990rz`, `Smirnov2002`; flow-equation bounds `Hollands:2011gf`. The frozen-$q$ form needed in §M.4 is not covered verbatim (hence `asm:appM-remainder`; its reduction: `rem:appM-remainder-reduction`).

### M.3 `sec:appM-chain` 链展开与 OPE 恒等式 (4 pp)

- **定义 `def:appM-normal-product` — 正规乘积与冻结 $q$ 的双局域正规乘积** (no tag). (i) For a monomial $O$ ($d_O$) and $a\ge d_O$, $a-d_O\in\mathbb Z$: $N_a[O]$ is the forest formula with $\delta_a(\gamma)=a-\sum_{E(\gamma)}d_e$ for $\gamma\ni V_O$ and $\delta=\omega$ otherwise ($\delta_a-\omega=a-d_O$, i.e. $c_{V_O}=a-d_O$ in `thm:appM-zimmermann`); $N[O]\equiv N_{d_O}[O]$. (ii) For monomials $A,B$, $D\equiv d_A+d_B-4$; auxiliary line $\ell_x$ ($V_A$–$V_B$, "propagator" $e^{-iq\cdot x}$), $\Gamma^\circ=\Gamma\cup\{\ell_x\}$; $\gamma$ is an **AB part** iff $V_A,V_B\in\gamma$ and $\gamma\cup\{\ell_x\}$ is 1PI in $\Gamma^\circ$; $\omega(\gamma)=D-\sum_{E(\gamma)}d_e$. (iii) Two subtractions, $a\in D+\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$: the **T-product** $\tilde G^T_{AB}(q;p)$ = BPHZ with $q$ external (1PI AB parts $t^{\omega}_{(p,q)}$, non-1PI AB parts unsubtracted); the **frozen-$q$ bilocal normal product** $\tilde N_a(q;p)$ of $N_a[A(x)B(0)]$: every AB part (including non-1PI) with $t^{(a)}_\gamma\equiv t^{\delta_a(\gamma)}_p$, $\delta_a=a-\sum_Ed_e$ (Taylor in $p$ only, $q$ frozen), other renormalization parts with $t^\omega$. `\uses{def:appM-bphz}`.
- **引理 `lem:appM-contraction` — 收缩与桥** (数学). For $\gamma^*\subset\sigma$, $\gamma^*$ connected: $\sigma$ connected ⇔ $\sigma/\gamma^*$ connected; a line of $\sigma\setminus\gamma^*$ is a bridge of $\sigma$ ⇔ it is a bridge of $\sigma/\gamma^*$; a bridge of $\sigma$ lying in $\gamma^*$ is a bridge of $\gamma^*$. Hence for 1PI $\gamma^*$: $\sigma$ 1PI ⇔ $\sigma/\gamma^*$ 1PI. `\uses{def:appM-bphz}`. Proof: full.
- **引理 `lem:appM-chain-expansion` — 链展开恒等式** (数学). Marked vertices $M\ne\emptyset$; family $\mathcal S$ of connected subgraphs containing $M$; *admissible* = in $\mathcal S$, or a renormalization part not containing all of $M$; forests = sets of pairwise non-overlapping admissible subgraphs. (i) The $\mathcal S$-elements of a forest form a chain. (ii) For operators $\tau_\gamma,\tau'_\gamma$ on $\mathcal S$ and $t^{\delta(\gamma)}$ elsewhere,
  $R^\tau_\Gamma=R^{\tau'}_\Gamma+\sum_{\gamma^*\in\mathcal S}\sum_{F\ni\gamma^*}\Big[\prod^{\curvearrowleft}_{\gamma\in F,\gamma\not\subseteq\gamma^*}(-\tau_\gamma)\Big](\tau'_{\gamma^*}-\tau_{\gamma^*})\Big[\prod^{\curvearrowleft}_{\gamma\in F,\gamma\subsetneq\gamma^*}(-\tau'_\gamma)\Big]I_\Gamma .$
  (iii) Under the contraction condition **(Ctr)** — for $\sigma\supsetneq\gamma^*$: $\sigma$ admissible ⇔ $\sigma/\gamma^*$ is a renormalization part of $\Gamma/\gamma^*$ containing the image vertex $v_*$ — each forest $F\ni\gamma^*$ splits uniquely as $F_{\rm in}\sqcup\{\gamma^*\}\sqcup F_{\rm out}$ with $F_{\rm out}$ in bijection with the forests of $\Gamma/\gamma^*$, and the $\gamma^*$-term of (ii) is the forest sum of $\Gamma/\gamma^*$ (with $\tau$ on subgraphs containing $v_*$) with vertex factor $v_{\gamma^*}=(\tau'_{\gamma^*}-\tau_{\gamma^*})\bar R^{\tau'}_{\gamma^*}$, $\bar R^{\tau'}_{\gamma^*}=\sum_{F_{\rm in}}\prod_{\gamma\in F_{\rm in}}(-\tau'_\gamma)I_{\gamma^*}$. For $M=\{V_O\}$, (Ctr) follows from `lem:appM-contraction`. `\uses{def:appM-bphz, lem:appM-contraction}`. Proof: full (write $-\tau=-\tau'+(\tau'-\tau)$ along the chain and group by the innermost difference; routing compatibility under contraction, `Zimmermann1969`). Renamed hypothesis: the reference's "(H)" is (Ctr) here (no clash with (H1)–(H5) of `thm:method-template`).
- **定理 `thm:appM-zimmermann-identity` — Zimmermann 恒等式** [R, R_PT]. （$R_{\rm PT}$ …）（本定理只涉及部分子态。）For $d_O\le a<a'$ in $d_O+\mathbb Z$: $N_a[O]=N_{a'}[O]+\sum_{O':\,a<d_{O'}\le a'}u_{O'}N_{a'}[O']$, $O'$ local monomials, $u_{O'}=m^{d_O-d_{O'}}\hat u_{O'}$ finite (zero-momentum Taylor coefficients of 1PI vertex functions of $N_a[O]$). `\uses{def:appM-normal-product, lem:appM-chain-expansion, thm:appM-zimmermann}`. Proof: full — `lem:appM-chain-expansion` with $\tau=t^{\delta_{a'}}$, $\tau'=t^{\delta_a}$; $v_{\gamma^*}=-\sum_{\delta_a<|\alpha|\le\delta_{a'}}\frac{p^\alpha}{\alpha!}\partial^\alpha_p\bar R^{(a)}_{\gamma^*}|_{p=0}$ converges (no overall subtraction since $\delta_a(\gamma^*)-|\alpha|<0$; vertex-additivity survives differentiation, $\partial_pt^\delta=t^{\delta-1}\partial_p$); $p^\alpha$ times the legs $E(\gamma^*)$ is the vertex of $O'$ with $d_{O'}=\sum_Ed_e+|\alpha|\in(a,a']$. Literature: `Zimmermann1970`, `Zimmermann:1972te`.
- **推论 `cor:appM-reduction` — 化到最小正规乘积** [R, R_PT]. （$R_{\rm PT}$ …）$N_a[O]=\sum_{d_O\le d_{O'}\le a}z_{OO'}N[O']$, $z_{OO}=1$. `\uses{thm:appM-zimmermann-identity}`. Proof: full (induction on $a-d_O$).
- **例 `ex:appM-callan-symanzik` — $\varphi^4$ 中的 Zimmermann 恒等式**. $O=\varphi^2$, $a=2\to a'=4$: $m^2N_2[\varphi^2]=m^2N_4[\varphi^2]+\hat u_{\varphi^4}N_4[\varphi^4]+\hat u_{\varphi\partial^2\varphi}N_4[\varphi\partial^2\varphi]+\hat u_{(\partial\varphi)^2}N_4[\partial_\mu\varphi\partial^\mu\varphi]$ ($\varphi^3$ excluded by $\mathbb Z_2$, $\varphi\partial\varphi$ by Lorentz invariance); the coefficient of $N_{a'}[O]$ is exactly 1 because $|\alpha|>\delta_a(\gamma^*)=a-d_O\ge0$. The identity behind the Callan–Symanzik equation (`Callan:1970yg`, `Symanzik:1970rt`). `\uses{thm:appM-zimmermann-identity}`.
- **引理 `lem:appM-routing` — 冻结 $q$ 时外动量分配的相容性** (数学). (New auxiliary lemma; repairs ERR-m24/R5 E8.) Proof: full — (1) homogeneity of integrands and of zero-momentum Taylor operators under $(q,p,m,k)\to\lambda(\cdot)$ and the Euler relation $q\cdot\partial_qh=D_hh-\sum p\partial_ph-\sum m\partial_mh\in\mathcal H$; (2) $O(4)$ covariance $L^{(q)}_{\mu\nu}h=-\sum_jL^{(p_j)}_{\mu\nu}h+\Sigma_{\mu\nu}h\in\mathcal H$; (3) Lagrange identity $|q|^2|v|^2=|q\cdot v|^2+\sum_{\mu<\nu}|q_\mu v_\nu-q_\nu v_\mu|^2$ (real $q$, complex $v$) ⇒ $|q||\partial_qh|\le|q\cdot\partial h|+\sum|L^{(q)}h|$; (4) induction on $|\beta|$.

- **引理 `lem:appM-ne` — 条件 (NE) 的适用范围** (数学). #### `asm:appM-brst-ope` — OPE 的 BRST 结构：带一个复合旁观者的欧氏展开 (Eε) [\tagA]
- **所属：** App M §M.6 (`sec:appM-brst`). Technical. (Reworded in v2.3 after the X3 verification; the label is kept.)
