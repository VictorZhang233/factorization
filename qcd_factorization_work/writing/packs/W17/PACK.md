* **引理 `lem:tmd-soft-oneloop` — 单圈软因子** [R, R_PT] · v1 (+ VF-REF-19)
  - **陈述.** (One loop.) (a) Large rapidity separation: $\tilde S(b_T;y_A,y_B)=1+\tilde K(b_T;\mu)(y_A-y_B)+O(\alpha_s^2)+O((y_A-y_B)^0\alpha_s)$, $\tilde K=-\frac{\alpha_sC_F}\pi\ln\frac{\mu^2b_T^2}{b_0^2}$ (VF-TMD-1). (c) Space-like Wilson-line self-energies are non-integrable: $n_A(y_A)\cdot k=-\sqrt2|\mathbf k_T|e^{-y_A}\sinh(y_k-y_A)$ vanishes inside the real-gluon phase space, giving a $1/\sinh^2$ double pole (length divergence) (VF-REF-19); any self-energy constant cancels identically in the TMD combination, so self-energies must be excluded or defined by one convention in $\tilde f^{\rm unsub}$ and $\tilde S$ (the time-like vs space-like difference $2\pi^2$ is an illustration: our one-loop derivation, claim N20 confirmed; the mechanism is stated qualitatively in `CollinsMetz2004` and Collins 2011 §10.6.4 (pp. 349–350), §10.11.1 (p. 381), Exercise 13.8 (p. 538); not marked new). * **命题 `prop:tmd-small-b` — 小 $b$ 展开** [A] · v1
  - **陈述.** $\tilde f_{q/H}(x,b_T;\mu,\zeta)=\sum_j\int_x^1\frac{d\xi}\xi\tilde C_{q/j}(\frac x\xi,b_T;\mu,\zeta)f_{j/H}(\xi;\mu)+O((mb_T)^p)$, $p>0$ ($p=2$ for unpolarized leading-twist TMDs: the leading small-$b_T$ power corrections are $O(b_T^2)$, `Scimemi:2016ffw`, `Moos:2020wvd`).
