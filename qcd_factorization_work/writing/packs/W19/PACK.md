## Chapter 16 — 喷注、事件形状、非全局对数与超领头对数 / Jets, event shapes, non-global and super-leading logarithms

`\label{ch:jets}` · `ch16_jets.tex` · **10 pp** · short format (precise statement + status + proof idea + one worked example) · Part IV

**Purpose.** Give IR-safe jet observables a home: what IR safety gives and does not give (M9, M10); the classic Sterman–Weinberg calculation and the LO thrust distribution; the status of hadronization corrections; non-global logarithms (existence, BMS, EFT factorization); super-leading logarithms as computed and resummed results, with the three-way separation of their status (refdoc S35): (i) amplitude-level violation of strict collinear factorization [V], (ii) SLLs at fixed order and resummed, (iii) PDF factorization of gap cross sections [C] with restoration evidence. The factorization-violation side belongs to Ch18; coherence-violating logarithms in one-jettiness and other frontier items are in Ch24 (pointers).

* **命题 `prop:jets-sw` — Sterman–Weinberg 两喷注截面** [R, R_PT] · v1
  - **陈述.** (At $O(\alpha_s)$.) $$\sigma_{2j}=\sigma_0\Big\{1-\frac{C_F\alpha_s}\pi\Big[4\ln\delta\ln2\epsilon+3\ln\delta+\frac{\pi^2}3-\frac52\Big]\Big\},\qquad\epsilon<\delta\ll1;$$ if $\epsilon$ is a fraction of the *beam* energy, the log is $\ln\epsilon$; the variant "$4\ln\delta\ln\epsilon$" with $\epsilon$ a fraction of the total energy is wrong by $4\ln2|\ln\delta|$ (a convention remark, not an erratum of `StermanWeinberg1977`: no printed source of the variant was found; numerically at $(\epsilon,\delta)=(10^{-6},0.003)$ the exact coefficient $-288.2811$ agrees with the $\ln2\epsilon$ form, $-288.2812$, not with the $\ln\epsilon$ form, $-304.39$); the formula fails for $\epsilon\gg\delta$ (c_ee §8, 6/6 checks; VF-EE-12).
  - **证明.** Full (worked example).
  - `\uses{def:jets-algorithms, prop:ee-oneloop, prop:qcd-soft-collinear-limits}`

* **推论 `cor:jets-ir-finite` — 红外安全喷注截面的有限性** [A] · v1
  - **陈述.** IR-safe jet cross sections in $e^+e^-$ are finite order by order (`thm:ir-safe-finite` applied to jet algorithms; `StermanWeinberg1977`, `Sterman1978a`, `Sterman1978b`, `Sterman:1979uw`).
  - **地位与缺口.** Covers final-state singularities only; with incoming hadrons it needs collinear factorization (M8, M9).

* **命题 `prop:jets-thrust-lo` — 领头阶推力分布** [R, R_PT] · v1
  - **陈述.** (At $O(\alpha_s)$.) Distribution $\frac{C_F\alpha_s}{2\pi}\big[\frac{2(3T^2-3T+2)}{T(1-T)}\ln\frac{2T-1}{1-T}-\frac{3(3T-2)(2-T)}{1-T}\big]$ (VF-SCET-13; `DeRujula:1978vmq`); singular terms and cumulant $1+\frac{C_F\alpha_s}{2\pi}[-2\ln^2\tau-3\ln\tau+\frac{\pi^2}3-1]+O(\tau\ln\tau)$ (critic c3; VF-SCET-12); exact identity $\frac{M_L^2+M_R^2}{Q^2}=\tau-\frac{\tau^2}2+\frac{(M_R^2-M_L^2)^2}{2Q^4}$ for massless particles (VF-SCET-15).
  - **证明.** Full.
  - `\uses{def:jets-algorithms}`

* **猜想 `conj:jets-hadronization` — 强子化修正的普适性** [C] · v1
  - **陈述.** The leading hadronization correction to event shapes is a shift $\propto\Lambda/Q$ with a universal coefficient (dispersive model `DokshitzerWebber1995`; renormalon motivation: Ch21).
  - **地位与缺口.** For $Q\tau\sim\Lambda$ the soft function becomes a non-perturbative shape function (`Korchemsky:1994is`, `Korchemsky:1999kt`, `Bauer:2003di`, `Lee:2006nr`, `Bauer:2008dt`); universality is [C] (critic B18); M10: event shapes have $O(\Lambda/Q)$ corrections.
  - `\uses{thm:scet-thrust}`

* **命题 `prop:jets-ngl` — 非全局对数的存在** [R, R_PT] · v1

For an energy-flow veto in a patch (Dasgupta–Salam hemisphere case) the leading NGL appears at $O(\alpha_s^2)$ with colour factor $C_FC_A$ and a $\pi^2$ coefficient (`DasguptaSalam2001`, `Dasgupta:2002bw`; normalization to be checked by the writer); the exact fixed-order coefficients are known.

#### `asm:intro-bridge` — 从部分子微扰论到强子的桥接假设 [\tagC] (global)
- **所属：** Ch1 `sec:intro-tags`; technical commentary (which axiom is used where) in App M §M.8. Global (not in the grade propagation of ordinary statements).
- **Binding text** (FINAL_OUTLINE_v2 Ch1 entry, reproduced verbatim; the 「被使用于」 field there lists the direct `\uses` users computed at the merge):
    - **陈述.** (Br1) *Region completeness:* the leading-power regions found in PT for IR-regulated partonic states are the only leading-power regions in QCD with hadron states; confinement modifies only the IR-sensitive factors and creates no new leading region. (Br2) *Power-counting transfer:* all $\Lambda$-sensitivity of a hadronic cross section covered by a factorization theorem sits in the hadronic matrix elements of the same operators (PDFs, FFs, TMDs, DAs, soft factors) that appear in the partonic theorem. (Br3) *Asymptoticity:* the remainder is $O((\Lambda/Q)^p)$ for some $p>0$ as an asymptotic statement; perturbative series are asymptotic and renormalon ambiguities are of the order of power corrections, so "exact up to power corrections" has meaning only once the power-correction term is defined.
    - **地位：** open; supported by phenomenology, lattice consistency and renormalon analyses; the value of $p$ is not fixed by the perturbative proofs except for OPE-accessible quantities. **文献：** `CollinsSoperSterman1989`, `Collins2011` (ch. 14 discussion), `Beneke1999`. **被使用于：** direct `\uses` users (computed at the merge): `thm:method-template`, `thm:disall-factorization`, `thm:disall-heavy`, `prop:disall-nuclear`, `thm:coll-sia`, `thm:coll-sidis`, `thm:coll-direct-photo`, `thm:dy-factorization`, `thm:dy-colour-singlet`, `thm:dy-polarized`, `thm:dy-higher-twist`, `thm:tmd-factorization`, `prop:tmd-small-b`, `cor:tmd-sivers-observable`, `prop:tmd-collins-universality`, `thm:scet-thrust`, `thm:jets-ngl-factorization`, `thm:break-ddis`, `thm:break-double-dy`, `thm:excl-pion-ff`, `thm:excl-transition`, `thm:excl-dvcs`, `thm:excl-meson-L`, `thm:heavy-bdpi`, `prop:heavy-btopi-ff`, `prop:heavy-qcdf-lp`, `thm:heavy-inclusive-b`, `thm:heavy-nrqcd-decay`, `prop:heavy-quarkonium-fragmentation`, `prop:power-dis-twist4-x`; in addition every hadron-level statement whose 「非微扰地位」 line names it (`def:intro-MA` refers to all six global entries). **解除途径：** non-perturbative control of QCD (Ch25 open problem OP-A1).

### VF-EE-12 — Sterman–Weinberg 两喷注截面
$$\sigma_{2j}=\sigma_0\Big\{1-\frac{C_F\alpha_s}\pi\Big[4\ln\delta\ln2\epsilon+3\ln\delta+\frac{\pi^2}3-\frac52\Big]\Big\},\qquad \epsilon<\delta\ll1,$$
where $\epsilon$ is the energy fraction of the **total** energy $\sqrt s$. With a fraction $\epsilon'$ of the beam energy the log reads $4\ln\delta\ln\epsilon'$. The formula does not apply for $\epsilon\gg\delta$.
- **Status:** V (S1–S6, exact numerics).
- **Source:** c_ee §8.
- **Used in:** `prop:jets-sw`, `ex:jets-sw`.

### VF-FRO-7 — 超领头对数的塔与闭式
$$\hat\sigma_n^{\rm SLL}=\Big(\frac{\alpha_s}{4\pi}\Big)^{n+3}L^{2n+3}\frac{(-4)^nn!}{(2n+3)!}\sum_{r=0}^n\frac{(2r)!}{4^r(r!)^2}C_{rn};\qquad \Delta\hat\sigma^{(S)}=-\hat\sigma_B\frac{4C_F}{3\pi}\alpha_s^3L^3\Delta Y\,{}_2F_2\Big(1,1;2,\frac52;-w\Big),\quad w=\frac{N_c\alpha_s}\pi L^2$$
($q\bar q$ singlet exchange, forward scattering); first SLL $\hat\sigma_1^{(S)}=\hat\sigma_B\frac4{15\pi^2}C_FN_c\alpha_s^4L^5\Delta Y$; ${}_2F_2(1,1;2,\frac52;-w)\simeq\frac32\frac{\ln w}w$ at large $w$ ($1.497$ at $w=10^8$).
- **Status:** V (OTHERS s4: term by term $n\le8$ from `Becher:2021zkk` eqs. (14), (19)). [A].
- **Used in:** Ch16 (`prop:jets-sll`), Ch24, `fig:front-sll`.

### VF-FRO-14 — 非全局可观测量的因子化（Becher–Neubert–Rothen–Shao）
$$\sigma(\beta,\delta)=\sum_{m\ge2}\big\langle\mathcal H_m(\{n\},Q,\delta)\otimes\mathcal S_m(\{n\},Q\beta,\delta)\big\rangle,\quad \mathcal S_m=\sum_{X_s}\langle0|S_1^\dagger\cdots S_m^\dagger|X_s\rangle\langle X_s|S_1\cdots S_m|0\rangle\,\theta(Q\beta-2E_{\rm out}),$$
$S_i(n_i)={\bf P}\exp(ig_s\int_0^\infty ds\,n_i\cdot A^a_s(sn_i){\bf T}_i^a)$ (their sign convention; convert $g\to-g$ for ours); RGEs $\frac d{d\ln\mu}\mathcal H_m=-\sum_{l\le m}\mathcal H_l\Gamma^H_{lm}$, $\frac d{d\ln\mu}\mathcal S_l=\sum_{m\ge l}\hat\Gamma^H_{lm}\otimes\mathcal S_m$.
- **Status:** TR (`Becher:2016mmh` §2 read). [A] ($e^+e^-$).
- **Used in:** Ch16 (`sec:jets-ngl`), Ch24.

### VF-GLA-13 — 超领头对数：首项的阶与系数
First super-leading logarithm at $O(\alpha_s^4)$ relative to Born, $\propto\alpha_s^4L^5$ (FKS eq. (3.22)). For quark–quark scattering with one gluon outside the gap (FKS eq. (3.24)):
$$\sigma_{1,\rm SLL}=-\sigma_0\Big(\frac{2\alpha_s}\pi\Big)^4\ln^5\frac Q{Q_0}\,\pi^2\,Y\,\frac{3N^2-4}{480}.$$
All-order tower $\alpha_s^{n+3}L^{2n+3}$, $n\ge1$ (BNS 2021 eq. (14)), i.e. $\alpha_s^mL^{2m-3}$; absent at large $N_c$; requires two incoming coloured partons; $2\to1$ processes from four loops, $2\to0$ from five loops.
- **Status:** VL-quoted (c_glauber §4.5 read FKS 2006 and BNS 2021; coefficients quoted, not recomputed). Replaces the "(verify against the abstract)" of the outline.
- **Used in:** `prop:jets-sll`, `prop:break-fss`.

### VF-SCET-12 — $O(\alpha_s)$ 推力分布的奇异项
$$\frac1{\sigma_0}\frac{d\sigma}{d\tau}=\delta(\tau)+\frac{C_F\alpha_s}{2\pi}\Big\{\Big(\frac{\pi^2}3-1\Big)\delta(\tau)-3\Big[\frac1\tau\Big]_+-4\Big[\frac{\ln\tau}\tau\Big]_+\Big\}+\text{nonsingular},$$
$$\text{cumulant: }1+\frac{C_F\alpha_s}{2\pi}\Big[-2\ln^2\tau-3\ln\tau+\frac{\pi^2}3-1\Big]+O(\tau\ln\tau).$$
The plus distributions are taken at $\tau=0$ on $[0,1]$. The $\delta$ coefficient from the sum rule is $2.289868133697$, against $\frac{\pi^2}3-1=2.289868133696$.
- **Status:** V (c_scet; critic c3, 20 digits).
- **Used in:** `prop:scet-thrust-oneloop`, `prop:jets-thrust-lo`, `ex:scet-thrust-oneloop`.

### VF-SCET-13 — 领头阶推力分布

- **Status:** V (integrated from the matrix element, to $7\times10^{-15}$; DEFG 1978 recalled).

### VF-SCET-15 — 推力与半球质量
For massless particles:
$$\frac{M_L^2+M_R^2}{Q^2}=\tau-\frac{\tau^2}2+\frac{(M_R^2-M_L^2)^2}{2Q^4}\ \text{exactly}.$$
For three partons the left-hand side equals $\tau$ exactly.
- **Status:** V.
- **Used in:** `prop:jets-thrust-lo`, `thm:scet-thrust`.
